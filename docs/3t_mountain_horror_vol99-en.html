S1

序章：小五暴雨独攀黄山（真实氛围）

Prologue: Xiao Wu Solo-Climbs Mount Huangshan in a Storm

情境暴雨浓雾中独自登山的真实心理——恐惧被感官放大，怪谈记忆自动代入。语域：casual 亲历讲述 + 氛围铺垫。

01
中文

核心机制：北方人对梅雨认知不足，景区暴雨天登山口空无一人，全程浓雾+暴雨，视线受阻、全身湿透、越爬越冷

EN

The mechanism: northerners underestimate the rainy season. On that stormy day the trailhead was completely empty — thick fog and heavy rain the whole way, limited visibility, soaking wet, and colder with every step up.

表达提示rainy season → 梅雨；trailhead → 登山口；visibility → 视线

02
中文

关键理解：未知环境会放大不安——既怕迷路又怕遇人，感官在白噪音中过敏，脑中自动套入看过的怪谈故事

EN

The key insight: an unfamiliar environment amplifies the unease — afraid of getting lost, afraid of meeting anyone. Your senses go hypersensitive in the white noise, and your brain automatically plays back the ghost stories you've heard.

表达提示amplify unease → 放大不安；hypersensitive → 过敏；white noise → 白噪音

03
中文

典型场景：天都峰垂直阶梯在浓雾中如怪物围城；手机全是水无法联系外界；4小时后到缆车区才见人

EN

A typical scene: Tiandu Peak's vertical stairs loomed through the fog like a monster surrounding a city; the phone was soaked and useless; only after four hours did he finally see people at the cable car station.

表达提示loom through the fog → 在雾中隐现；soaked and useless → 全是水没法用

04
中文

（引用）原文依据：「你怕走错路，又怕遇不着人……又怕遇到人，你也不知道会遇到什么东西。」

EN

"You're afraid of taking the wrong path, and afraid of not meeting anyone... then again, afraid of meeting someone — you don't know what you might run into."

表达提示take the wrong path → 走错路；run into → 遇到

05
中文

（引用）相关概念：为后续《山岳怪谈》四则故事提供情绪底色，与「雨中引路人」心态高度共鸣

EN

Related concept: this sets the emotional tone for the four mountain tales that follow, and it strongly resonates with the mindset behind "the guide in the rain."

表达提示emotional tone → 情绪底色；resonate with → 与…共鸣

06
中文

原因：路滑、雷击、失温、迷路、无救援；小五自己也承认「有很大危险性」

EN

The reason: slippery trails, lightning, hypothermia, getting lost, no rescue — Xiao Wu himself admits it was "highly dangerous."

表达提示slippery trail → 路滑；hypothermia → 失温；no rescue → 无救援

    第一句口语描述，第二句更有画面

S2

作品背景：《山岳怪谈》与安谈润平

The Book: Mountain Spirits and Antan Junpei

情境作者身份与创作脉络——登山家转怪谈讲述人，用现代登山经验重构民俗。语域：casual 介绍性口吻。

01
中文

登山家：深耕日本飞驒山脉（北阿尔卑斯），1999年脑溢血后从挑战高峰转向低海拔村落文化

EN

As a mountaineer: he knew the Hida Mountains of Japan — the Northern Alps — deeply; after a cerebral hemorrhage in 1999, he shifted from conquering high peaks to documenting low-altitude village culture.

表达提示mountaineer → 登山家；cerebral hemorrhage → 脑溢血；village culture → 村落文化

02
中文

怪谈讲述人：经营网站「北阿尔卑斯的风」，收集山间小屋与村民口述故事

EN

As a storyteller: he runs a website called "Wind of the Northern Alps," collecting oral stories from mountain huts and villagers.

表达提示oral stories → 口述故事；mountain hut → 山间小屋

03
中文

山岳怪谈开创者：2004年起在怪谈杂志连载「山之灵」，以现代登山活动为基准的新品类

EN

As a genre founder: from 2004 he serialized "Mountain Spirits" in ghost-story magazines, a new category grounded in modern mountaineering.

表达提示serialize → 连载；grounded in → 以…为基准

04
中文

适用边界：故事来源为山间小屋夜谈、村落采集与个人经历，2018年邀请伊藤润二等漫画家改编为漫画

EN

The boundary: the stories come from midnight talks in mountain huts, village fieldwork, and personal experience; in 2018 he had manga artists including Junji Ito adapt them into comics.

表达提示midnight talks → 夜谈；fieldwork → 实地采集；adapt into comics → 改编为漫画

05
中文

（引用）「山的恐怖王不是妖怪吃人，更多是无视自然规则的人类遭来的灾祸。」

EN

"The horror of the mountains is not monsters eating people — more often, it's disaster brought on by humans who ignore nature's rules."

表达提示horror → 恐怖；ignore nature's rules → 无视自然规则

06
中文

核心机制：纵贯100+公里，气候多变、浓雾频发、山脊乱流严重，日本山难失踪率数一数二

EN

The mechanism: the range stretches over 100 kilometers, with volatile weather, frequent fog, and violent ridge winds — it ranks among the top mountain ranges in Japan for accidents and missing persons.

表达提示volatile weather → 气候多变；violent ridge winds → 山脊乱流；missing persons → 失踪

  1. 想说「从登顶转向记录村落」

    chasing summits · documenting villages · oral histories
    第一句直白，第二句带文学感

  2. 想说「恐怖来自不守规则的人」

    ignoring the rules · punishes the careless
    第一句直接引用观点，第二句是概括
S3

故事一：山脊笑脸男 + 双面祟

Tale One: The Smiling Man on the Ridge and the Double-Faced Curse

情境石田在山脊遭遇的红衣笑脸男——幻象如何干扰判断；双面祟挤压帐篷。语域：casual 恐怖故事讲述。

01
中文

在讲什么：石田五年前山脊徒步，绕过巨石后全队消失，迎面走来穿红衣的高瘦男子——嘴在笑、眉紧皱、步伐与速度不匹配（心灵滑步），追至背后摸到背包

EN

The story: five years ago, Ishida was ridge-hiking; after rounding a boulder, his whole group vanished, and a tall, thin man in red came toward him — mouth smiling, brows knitted, gait and speed mismatched, a "spirit moonwalk." It even caught up and touched his backpack from behind.

表达提示ridge-hiking → 山脊徒步；brows knitted → 眉头紧皱；spirit moonwalk → 心灵滑步

02
中文

关键理解：同期有50岁女性独自登山坠亡，偏离山脊步道十几米踩雪线滑落；石田推测她未像自己一样原路返回，而是被逼向山脊侧面坠谷

EN

The key insight: around the same time, a 50-year-old woman died in a solo climb, slipping on the snow line just a dozen meters off the ridge trail. Ishida suspects she didn't backtrack like he did — she was pushed toward the ridge's side and fell into the valley.

表达提示backtrack → 原路返回；snow line → 雪线；fall into the valley → 坠谷

03
中文

怎么用：山脊遇异常人影，优先原路退回有遮蔽处，切勿横向偏离步道

EN

What to do: if you see an abnormal figure on a ridge, backtrack to a sheltered spot first — never step sideways off the trail.

表达提示abnormal figure → 异常人影；sheltered spot → 有遮蔽处

04
中文

（引用）原文依据：「嘴是笑脸的形状，但没有笑意……步伐跟速度完全不匹配，像心里滑步。」

EN

"The mouth was shaped like a smile, but there was no warmth in it... the gait and speed didn't match at all, like a moonwalk in the mind."

表达提示no warmth in it → 没有笑意；moonwalk → 滑步

05
中文

山里遇难者亡灵无法离开，通过幻象干扰登山者判断，引其发生事故以「抓替身」解脱。红衣=山神显现色+尸体出血色；僵硬笑脸=怨灵难以模拟人类表情；山脊石碑上刻笑脸花纹是笑脸来源之一

EN

The lore: spirits of accident victims can't leave the mountain; they use illusions to disturb climbers' judgment and cause accidents, seeking a "body double" to escape. Red clothes stand for the mountain god's color and the red of blood; the stiff smile is a vengeful spirit's failure to mimic human faces; and smiley carvings on ridge stone markers are one source of the face.

表达提示body double → 替身；vengeful spirit → 怨灵；mimic → 模拟

06
中文

（引用）「不会直接伤害人，但会给登山者造成精神压力，从而又使对方出现事故。」

EN

"It won't harm you directly, but it puts psychological pressure on the climber, which then leads to an accident."

表达提示psychological pressure → 精神压力；lead to an accident → 导致事故

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「被盯上、被跟踪的感觉」

    behind me · following at my pace
    第一句描述瞬间，第二句描述持续感

  2. 想说「别看，原路退回去」

    don't engage · backtrack · the way you came
    第一句命令式，第二句更口语
S4

故事二：采蘑菇（松茸迷失）

Tale Two: Mushroom Picking — Lost After Finding Matsutake

情境采菌人贪念深入密林，废弃缆车上的干尸；迷失时的自救原则。语域：casual 恐怖故事讲述。

01
中文

原因：菌类长在潮湿背阴处，必须离开步道；发现松茸后贪念驱使其深入，铃当联络中断、手机无信号

EN

The cause: mushrooms grow in damp, shaded spots, so you have to leave the trail; after spotting matsutake, greed drove him deeper — the bell contact broke off, and the phone had no signal.

表达提示damp and shaded → 潮湿背阴；matsutake → 松茸；greed drove him deeper → 贪念驱使其深入

02
中文

解法：采菌必须保持铃当可闻范围；不贪心、不深入未标记区域

EN

The solution: when foraging, stay within earshot of the bell; don't be greedy, and don't push into unmarked areas.

表达提示within earshot → 可闻范围内；foraging → 采菌；unmarked areas → 未标记区域

03
中文

（引用）龙川每年提醒：「新手一定不要让他找到松茸——找到过就对其他菌类没兴趣了。」

EN

"Never let a beginner find a matsutake — once they've found one, they lose interest in every other mushroom."

表达提示lose interest in → 对…没兴趣；beginner → 新手

04
中文

在讲什么：安谈迷失后见废弃缆车突然运行，座椅上挂着满头白发、腰间挂菌篓的干尸；闭眼不敢看时龙川呼喊打破魔咒

EN

The story: lost, Antan saw the abandoned cable car suddenly start running, carrying a mummified body — white hair, a mushroom basket at its waist. As he squeezed his eyes shut, Longchuan's shouts broke the spell.

表达提示mummified body → 干尸；break the spell → 打破魔咒；squeeze one's eyes shut → 闭紧双眼

05
中文

关键理解：若沿缆车向下走，暴雨冲毁的山体可能「有去无回」——失踪老大爷或许正是走了这条路。废弃建筑被山林收回后成为结界边界，最易遇见「恶幽灵」

EN

The key insight: following the cable car downhill could mean a point of no return on a hillside washed out by storms — the missing old man may have walked exactly that route. Once reclaimed by the forest, abandoned buildings become a border zone where "malignant spirits" are most easily met.

表达提示point of no return → 有去无回；washed out → 被冲毁；malignant spirit → 恶幽灵

06
中文

怎么用：迷路时沿原路或已知步道返回，勿被看似捷径的废弃设施吸引

EN

What to do: when lost, return along your original route or a known trail — don't be lured by abandoned structures that look like shortcuts.

表达提示original route → 原路；shortcut → 捷径；be lured by → 被…吸引

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「贪念让人越走越深」

    one more mushroom · greed kept pulling
    第一句具象，第二句概括

  2. 想说「走回头路最保险」

    turn around · the one you came from
    第一句短促有力，第二句更正式
S5

故事三：雨中引路人（善幽灵）

Tale Three: The Guide in the Rain — A Kind Spirit

情境失温遇难者的灵魂反被传为善灵，指引迷路的登山者；结界与善幽灵概念。语域：casual 讲述 + 民俗分析。

01
中文

安谈夏末浓雾暴雨中攀峭壁，恐惧放大后遇蓝衣重装男子坐于石上。男子拒绝结伴、指了反方向说「六分钟到小木屋」——实际安谈已走错路

EN

In late-summer fog and rain, Antan climbed a cliff with fear swelling; he met a heavy-gear man in blue sitting on a rock. The man refused to hike together, pointed the opposite way, and said "six minutes to the hut" — when in fact Antan had already lost his way.

表达提示heavy-gear → 重装；opposite direction → 反方向；lose one's way → 迷路

02
中文

到小屋烘干室，衣架下露出同款雨靴，蹲着那人抬头说「我30分钟内到了，你得请我喝啤酒」——客厅担架上躺着同一人的尸体，数小时前已失温身亡

EN

In the hut's drying room, the same rain boots peeked out under a clothes rack; the crouching man looked up and said, "I made it in 30 minutes — you owe me a beer." On a stretcher in the living room lay the same man's body, dead from hypothermia hours earlier.

表达提示drying room → 烘干室；hypothermia → 失温；you owe me a beer → 你得请我喝啤酒

03
中文

（引用）「他走的是反方向……如果按那个路径走就迷路了，不及时到小屋就可能失温。」

EN

"He walked the opposite way... following that path would have meant getting lost — and without reaching the hut in time, hypothermia."

表达提示opposite path → 反方向的路径；in time → 及时

04
中文

（引用）相关概念：与善幽灵（当地猎户/守山人灵魂受山神庇护）对应；飞驒山脉多人目击穿白衣无脚印指路的登山者

EN

Related concept: he matches the "kind spirit" — hunter and mountain-keeper souls under the mountain god's protection; many in the Hida Mountains have seen a hiker in white, leaving no footprints, who points the way.

表达提示kind spirit → 善幽灵；leave no footprints → 不留脚印；point the way → 指路

05
中文

为什么重要：山脊开阔无遮挡=阴阳交界=结界最弱；单人过夜=被视为闯入者；偏离步道采菌=离开受庇护区域

EN

Why it matters: an open, unobstructed ridge marks the boundary between worlds, where the "barrier" is weakest; camping alone marks you as an intruder; and foraging off-trail means leaving protected ground.

表达提示boundary between worlds → 阴阳交界；barrier → 结界；intruder → 闯入者

06
中文

适用边界：景区成熟路线+好天气+团队行动时，风险可控；野山、暴雨、冬季、单人时需最高警惕

EN

The boundary: on developed park routes in good weather with a group, risk is manageable; on wild mountains, in storms, in winter, or solo, you need maximum caution.

表达提示developed routes → 成熟路线；maximum caution → 最高警惕

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「遇到好人指了错误方向」

    wrong way · led me right
    两句都玩反差点，第一句更直白

  2. 想说「结界概念」

    boundary zones · protected zone
    第一句民俗表述，第二句实用化

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

用「hypothermia」说：雨夜山里很容易失温。

Out in the mountains on a rainy night, hypothermia is a real risk.

用「backtrack」说：看到异常别犹豫，原路退回去。

Don't hesitate when you see something off — just backtrack.

用「point of no return」说：跟着废弃缆车走下去可能有去无回。

Following that abandoned cable car downhill could be a point of no return.

用「break the spell」说：同伴的喊声打破了魔咒。

His partner's shout broke the spell.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The mountain ghosts are very afraid, I climbed alone.
✓ The stories made me uneasy, but I still climbed alone.

「很恐怖」直译成 very afraid 指人而非故事；主语应是 stories/experience，用 uneasy / unsettling。

✕ I met a bad spirit, so I very fast ran away.
✓ I backed away slowly and didn't make eye contact.

中式语序 very fast ran away 不成立；用 backed away / backed off，且故事里主张慢退、不直视。

✕ The mushroom smell made me think to go deeper.
✓ Finding one matsutake made me want to go deeper.

think to do 是中式表达；「想深入」用 made me want to / drew me deeper。

✕ He lost temperature and died.
✓ He died of hypothermia.

lost temperature 是「失温」的直译；英文用 hypothermia / his body temperature dropped。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

怪谈恐怖来自妖怪吃人 → 山的恐怖更多来自无视自然规则的人自己
迷路时找看起来近的路走 → 原路返回或走已知步道，废弃捷径最危险
一个人爬山很酷 → 结界概念提醒：独行、离道、暴雨时警惕最高

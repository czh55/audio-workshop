避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The rocket's price became 200 times cheaper.
✓ The cost dropped to one two-hundredth of what it was.

200 times cheaper 语义混乱；「降到1/200」用 dropped to one two-hundredth 或 200x cost reduction。

✕ Musk is very shy when speaking.
✓ Musk is extremely introverted on stage — he's uncomfortable public-speaking.

shy 太弱且带「害羞」歧义；台上不适用 introverted / uncomfortable on stage。

✕ The rocket can be recovered, so it's a recycled rocket company.
✓ SpaceX is a rocket company that recovers and reuses rockets.

recycled 用于垃圾回收；火箭复用是 recover and reuse。

✕ The first three launches all failed, the company was going to close.
✓ The first three launches all failed, and the company nearly went under.

going to close 是「将要关门」；表达濒临倒闭用 nearly went under / was on the brink of collapse。

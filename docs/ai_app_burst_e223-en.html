避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The video can grow to one minute long.
✓ The video length extended from 10 seconds to a full minute.

表达时长变化用 extended from A to B / went up to；grow to 太口语且不精准。

✕ The model can control more than before.
✓ The model gives far more controllability than the previous lucky-draw mode.

名词化用 controllability（可控性）更专业；与抽卡模式对比才完整。

✕ Enterprises can make their own model but accuracy is not enough.
✓ An enterprise's self-developed model struggles to reach even 70% accuracy.

struggle to reach 比 not enough 更地道；补上 70% 这个数字更有信息量。

✕ Users can't say their need clearly.
✓ Users often can't articulate their editing intent precisely.

表达「说出/阐明意图」用 articulate / put into words，比 say 更正式准确。

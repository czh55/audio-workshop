EN

In the past you chose a car by first trusting a BBA badge; now, buying a new-energy car, the first questions are which cockpit chip, an 8295, which driving solution, Momenta R6 or R7, and which battery, CATL or Sunwoda. The supply chain's brand weight now outweighs the car brand's.

表达提示systemic dividend → 体系化红利；brand preference → 品牌偏爱

EN

The mechanism: the car is evolving from a means of transport into a mobile terminal and then into an embodied intelligence robot. 01
中文

EN

Smart Cockpits and the 9-Series Universe

01
中文

核心机制：L3普及后手不用扶方向盘、脚不用搭踏板——座椅不必朝前，可横坐倒坐甚至车内健身。内饰设计师被告知不要用老逻辑，现在很多天马行空设计是为自动驾驶后做准备

表达提示owner-plus-butler → 主人+管家；sleep-commute → 睡觉打卡上班

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The car is very big and good.
✓ Flagships exist to carry the latest tech — three lidars, Thor chips, 900V — not because the market needs that many big cars.

把冲9系理解为「车大车好」就错了；要翻出「旗舰是技术落地的载体」这一产业逻辑。

不是简单的好坏比较，而是「中国团队/技术上车」的策略本质。

✕ L3 means I can sleep in the car.

L3 是「特定路段合法脱手」，不是随时睡觉；责任归属也是关键信息。

✕ Buying a car is only about the brand.
✓ Buyers now ask which chip, which driving solution, and which battery — the supply chain outweighs the badge.

品牌权重已被供应链权重超过，是本集核心洞察，不可省略。

二、双保险制度与后台定责机制

Dual Insurance and Backend Fault Attribution

情境讨论事故后车主如何同时走商业险与比亚迪兜底、后台数据如何逐帧定责，以及「车企既是运动员又是裁判员」的争议。语域：casual 商业分析 + 消费者视角。

01
中文

核心机制：出事故后车主既可报商业车险，也可向比亚迪申请兜底。保险行业基本原则是同一损失不可重复获利

EN

Core mechanic: after an accident the owner can either file a commercial insurance claim or apply for BYD's safety net. The basic insurance principle is that you can't profit twice from the same loss.

表达提示file a claim → 报案理赔；profit twice from the same loss → 同一损失重复获利；double-dip → 双重获利

02
中文

EN

Key point: if BYD confirms coverage, BYD pays it directly — no insurance claim, no record, no premium increase. If it's judged not to be a system fault, the case goes back to the normal auto-insurance process.

表达提示pay it directly → 直接承担赔付；no claim record → 无出险记录；back to the normal process → 回到常规流程

EN

Typical flow: for a minor accident, report it to insurance first to lock down scene evidence, then apply for the safety net in the app; BYD pulls the before-and-after driving data and rules on it frame by frame.

表达提示lock down evidence → 固定证据；frame by frame → 逐帧；rule on → 作出判定

三、L2宣传与法律责任的根本矛盾

The Fundamental Gap Between L2 Marketing and Legal Responsibility

情境讨论发布会话术如何让消费者脑补自动驾驶，而法律上驾驶员仍是第一责任人，以及比亚迪的解法。语域：casual 汽车行业观察。

01
中文

原因：车企发布会讲「自动变道、像老司机」，消费者脑补自动驾驶；出事故时回归L2框架——驾驶员是第一责任人

EN

The root cause: launch events talk about 'auto lane changes, driving like a veteran,' so consumers picture full self-driving — but in an accident, everything snaps back to the L2 framework, where the driver is the primary responsible party.

表达提示drive like a veteran → 像老司机；full self-driving → 自动驾驶；snap back to → 回到；primary responsible party → 第一责任人

02
中文

解法：比亚迪未宣称L3，兜底是在「合规使用+驾驶员仍须监管」前提下，若确认系系统问题则厂家担责

EN

The fix: BYD never claimed L3. The safety net only applies under compliant use with the driver still supervising — and if a system fault is confirmed, the manufacturer takes responsibility.

表达提示claim L3 → 宣称L3级；supervising → 保持监管；take responsibility → 担责

03
中文

适用边界：L3法规落地前，所有「高阶智驾」「L2.999」本质仍是L2，驾驶员全程监控义务不可免除

EN

Boundary: until L3 regulations land, all 'advanced driver assistance' and 'L2.999' marketing is still L2 at heart — the driver's duty to monitor the road at all times can't be waived.

表达提示L2.999 → 擦边球宣传话术；at heart → 本质上；monitoring duty → 监控义务；waived → 被免除

04
中文

（引用）高速事故案例：L2辅助驾驶≠自动驾驶，驾驶员长时间依赖智驾、未及时接管，车企不承担主责

EN

Highway case: L2 driver assistance is not self-driving — when a driver relies on the system too long and fails to take over in time, the automaker isn't primarily liable.

表达提示take over → 接管；primarily liable → 承担主要责任；rely on → 依赖

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ BYD pays all the money with no upper limit.
✓ BYD pays the full amount with no cap on the payout.

upper limit 更偏技术参数上限；赔付语境用 cap / payout cap 更地道。

✕ The driver is the first responsible person.
✓ The driver is the primary responsible party.

first responsible person 是逐字直译；法律语境用 primary responsible party / primarily liable。

✕ The car company is the athlete and the referee.
✓ The automaker is acting as both player and referee.

比喻中的「运动员」用 player 而非 athlete；both…and… 结构更自然。

✕ The system didn't know the front car stopped.
✓ The system failed to detect the vehicle ahead braking suddenly.

didn't know 太口语且拟人化；描述系统识别失误用 fail to detect 更准确。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为智能驾驶比拼的是功能参数和发布会话术 → 敢不敢承诺「无上限兜底」才是智驾竞争真正的分水岭

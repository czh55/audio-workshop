"置身厂内"的我们，如何找回自洽的人生？

Finding a Coherent Life When You're Stuck Inside the Factory

Mengmeng, a former product manager at Baidu, Ctrip, and Meituan, walked through 15 years of the internet's golden age and retired in 2024. She paid a 6-million-yuan tuition bill when Chinese tech stocks crashed, and evolved from Duan Yongping-style stock conviction to David Swensen's asset allocation, then iterated again to Taleb-style options hedging — building an investment system to fund a FIRE life. Retiring early wasn't escaping; it was finding coherence on both the financial and the psychological dimension.

一、从"厂内"到退休：15年大厂路

From Inside the Factory to Retirement: 15 Years on the Big-Tech Assembly Line

情境萌萌的职业轨迹：百度、铝平网、携程、点评/美团，以及为什么 2024 年离开。语域：casual 个人叙事 + 行业观察。

01
中文

核心机制：萌萌2012年入行，经历百度（国际化/日文产品）→铝平网（携程旗下旅游社区创业，对标TripAdvisor，最终失败）→携程攻略社区→点评/美团（推荐菜、到店综合），横跨产品黄金增长期

EN

Core path: Mengmeng entered the industry in 2012 — Baidu (internationalization / Japanese products) → Lvping.com, a Ctrip-owned travel community startup benchmarked against TripAdvisor that eventually failed → Ctrip's travel-guide community → Dianping/Meituan (recommended dishes, offline-to-online). She rode the golden growth years of product management.

表达提示internationalization → 国际化；benchmarked against → 对标；offline-to-online (O2O) → 到店综合；golden growth years → 黄金增长期

02
中文

关键理解：早期互联网是"草台班子"时代，产品经理缺口巨大，跳槽极易。但2024年时行业增长放缓，重复性工作增多，团队氛围内卷，兴趣驱动的工作模式难以为继

EN

Key insight: the early internet was the 'make-shift crew' era — product managers were scarce and job hopping was easy. By 2024 growth slowed, repetitive work multiplied, team culture turned inward, and interest-driven work was no longer sustainable.

表达提示make-shift crew → 草台班子；job hopping → 跳槽；turned inward → 内卷；sustainable → 难以为继

03
中文

典型场景：当你发现工作不再带来正向收益（成就感下降），而负面因素（不自由、身体不适）持续叠加时，就是重新审视职业选择的信号

EN

Typical signal: when work no longer brings positive returns (shrinking sense of achievement) while negatives (no freedom, declining health) keep stacking up, it's time to re-examine your career choice.

表达提示positive returns → 正向收益；stack up → 持续叠加；re-examine → 重新审视

表达提示sense of achievement → 成就感；decline year by year → 逐年下降；positive/negative → 正向/负面

二、投资进化史：600万学费换来的体系

Investment Evolution: The System Paid for with 6 Million Yuan

情境从重仓中概股个股，到耶鲁模型式资产配置，再到期权对冲——萌萌投资体系的两次迭代。语域：casual 投资复盘。

01
中文

核心机制：2015年起做美股中概股，重仓美团。逻辑是"我对这家公司认知最深，没理由投资别的个股能赚钱"

EN

Core mechanism: from 2015 she traded US-listed Chinese tech stocks, heavily weighted on Meituan. The logic: 'I know this company better than anyone, so I have no reason to expect other stocks to make me money.'

表达提示US-listed Chinese stocks (ADR) → 美股中概股；heavily weighted → 重仓；have no reason to → 没理由

02
中文

关键理解：个股投资在牛市中收益惊人，但中概股从高点暴跌后，"熬"的策略失效——美股2008后最多半年回原位，中概股从406下去至今未回

EN

Key insight: individual stocks pay off spectacularly in a bull market, but after the Chinese ADRs crashed from their peak, the 'just wait it out' play failed — after 2008 US stocks always recovered within six months, while Chinese ADRs fell from 406 and never came back.

表达提示pay off → 收益惊人；bull market → 牛市；wait it out → 熬；crash from the peak → 从高点暴跌

03
中文

（引用）原文依据：「我有什么理由认为我可以投资别的个股赚钱？中概股你在406下去了，熬多少年也没到406。」

EN

Direct quote: 'What reason do I have to believe I can make money on other stocks? Chinese ADRs fell from 406, and no matter how many years you wait, they never got back to 406.'

表达提示make money on → 靠…赚钱；never got back → 从未回到原位

04
中文

核心机制：2021年规划退休时，借鉴耶鲁模型：A股（沪深300/中证500）+美股（纳指/标普500）+港股+债券+黄金，每个指数持仓上限20%，基于PE分位动态调仓

EN

Core mechanism: planning retirement in 2021, she borrowed the Yale model: A-shares (CSI 300 / CSI 500), US stocks (Nasdaq / S&P 500), Hong Kong stocks, bonds, and gold — each index capped at 20 percent, rebalanced dynamically by PE percentile.

表达提示Yale model → 耶鲁模型（史文森资产配置）；cap at → 上限；PE percentile → PE分位；rebalance → 动态调仓

05
中文

典型配置：预留3-5年生活费，其余滚动投资；控制单一资产大类不超过30-40%

EN

Typical allocation: keep 3–5 years of living expenses aside and roll the rest into investments; keep any single asset class under 30–40 percent.

表达提示living expenses → 生活费；set aside → 预留；asset class → 资产大类；roll into → 滚动投入

06
中文

适用边界：在牛市中会miss大量收益——纳指PE分位80%以上可以躺35年，严格按纪律会导致牛市大部分收益吃不到

EN

Boundary: in a bull market you will miss a lot of gains — when the Nasdaq PE sits above the 80th percentile, strictly following the discipline means you skip most of the rally's upside.

表达提示miss gains → 错过收益；80th percentile → 80%分位；discipline → 纪律；upside → 上行收益

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「重仓个股在暴跌面前没有退路」

    no escape when it crashes · one-way bet with no floor
    第一句直白，第二句偏投资术语

  2. 想说「纪律会错过牛市收益」

    discipline costs upside · giving up part of the rally
    第一句中性，第二句更像自我提醒

三、FIRE决策框架与生活实况

The FIRE Decision Framework and What Life After Retirement Looks Like

情境财务上算清楚账、精神上找到成就感来源，FIRE 才可持续。语域：casual 个人复盘 + 方法论。

01
中文

为什么重要：财务没算清楚会天天焦虑（萌萌辞职前一天算十几遍"会不会破产"）；成就感来源不对会很快重返职场

EN

Why it matters: if the finances aren't sorted out, you'll be anxious every day — the day before she resigned she ran the 'will I go broke' calculation a dozen times. And if your source of achievement isn't right, you'll soon be back in the workforce.

表达提示sorted out → 算清楚；go broke → 破产；source of achievement → 成就感来源；back in the workforce → 重返职场

02
中文

适用边界：有人不算账也能FIRE（萌萌的朋友），但她是J人必须算明白；目前接触的"FIRE失败"案例仅一人，且因成就感缺失而非经济原因重返职场

EN

Boundary: some people FIRE without crunching numbers (a friend of hers did), but she's a planner who has to see the math. So far she's met only one 'FIRE failure,' and that person went back to work for loss of achievement, not money.

表达提示crunch numbers → 算账；a planner (J person) → J人（计划型人格）；FIRE failure → 失败案例

03
中文

（引用）"财务上得算个账——未来花钱几项、投资收益率可能什么、不同环境下承担什么风险。第二，成就感来源是什么？如果来自组织一群人干成一件事，FIRE后体验可能不太好。"

EN

Direct quote: 'Financially you have to run the numbers — what you'll spend, what return rate is possible, what risks under different environments. Second: where does your achievement come from? If it comes from organizing a group to get something done, the FIRE experience may not be great.'

表达提示run the numbers → 算账；return rate → 投资收益率；organizing a group → 组织一群人

04
中文

（引用）怎么落地：列出家庭支出项、预估投资收益率区间、做压力测试（熊市能否挺住）；诚实评估自己的成就感来自独立创作还是团队协作

EN

How to apply it: list household spending, estimate a return-rate range, stress-test whether you can weather a bear market; honestly assess whether your achievement runs on independent creation or team collaboration.

表达提示stress-test → 压力测试；weather a bear market → 挺过熊市；independent creation → 独立创作

05
中文

萌萌坦言挺失望没赶上AI裁员大礼包，但她的逻辑是：在公司的注意力必须放在公司关注的事上（如足疗），作为独立个人可以用AI研究自己真正感兴趣的领域（如心理学）

EN

Mengmeng admits she's a bit disappointed to have missed the AI-layoff severance windfall, but her logic: inside a company your attention must go to company priorities (foot spas, say), while as an independent person you can use AI to explore what truly interests you, like psychology.

表达提示severance windfall → 裁员大礼包；company priorities → 公司关注的事；explore interests → 研究感兴趣的领域

06
中文

（引用）争议点：是"错过大礼包"还是"获得注意力自由"？嘉宾立场明确偏向后者

EN

The debate: 'missed the windfall' or 'gained attention freedom'? The guests clearly side with the latter.

表达提示missed the windfall → 错过好处；attention freedom → 注意力自由；side with the latter → 偏向后者

  1. 想说「财务和成就感两件事都要想清楚」

    money math and achievement · finance and purpose, both collapse alone
    第一句平实，第二句对仗有力度

  2. 想说「错过裁员大礼包，换来了注意力自由」

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

用「stack up」说：负面的东西在不断叠加。

The negatives kept stacking up while the positives shrank.

用「wait it out」说：中概股暴跌后「熬」的策略失效了。

After the ADR crash, the wait-it-out strategy stopped working.

用「cap at」说：每个指数持仓上限 20%。

Each index is capped at 20 percent of the portfolio.

用「stress-test」说：要压力测试熊市是否挺得住。

You should stress-test whether you can survive a bear market.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The work's good feeling decreased year by year.
✓ The sense of achievement and joy work gave me declined year by year.

good feeling 太含糊；职场语境用 sense of achievement / satisfaction 更准确。

✕ I have no reason to invest other stocks can make money.
✓ What reason do I have to believe other stocks would make me money?

直译会把句子结构搞乱；用 What reason do I have to + believe/expect 才是地道疑问式。

✕ My achievement comes from leading a group of people to do something well.
✓ My sense of achievement comes from organizing a team to pull something off.

do something well 可以但平淡；pull something off 表达「干成一件事」更地道。

✕ She didn't catch the AI layoff big gift.
✓ She missed out on the AI-layoff severance windfall.

big gift 是直译；裁员补偿用 severance package / windfall，错过用 miss out on。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为投资赚钱靠的是看准一只好股票拿住不动 → 个股信仰在暴跌面前没有退路，资产配置+对冲才是可持续体系
以为 FIRE 就是攒够钱辞职，财务和心态都不用准备 → 财务账+成就感来源两个维度都想清楚，FIRE 才可持续
以为错过裁员大礼包是损失 → 那是用现金换来了注意力自由——能研究自己真正感兴趣的领域

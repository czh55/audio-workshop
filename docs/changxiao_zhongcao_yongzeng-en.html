✕ We should change from grabbing traffic to grabbing mind.
✓ Growth must shift from grabbing traffic to winning mindshare.

grab mind 是直译；「抢占心智」在营销语境是 win mindshare / occupy the mind。

✕ The users are very silent.
✓ These users are 'silent users' — they downloaded but never engaged.

very silent 字面误用；沉默用户是营销术语 silent users，指下载但不活跃。

✕ We plant grass in the users' hearts.
✓ We plant a seed in the users' minds through content seeding.

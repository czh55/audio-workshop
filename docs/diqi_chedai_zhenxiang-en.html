Part 1 · 七年低息方案从何而来

Part 1 · Where the 7-Year Low-Interest Plans Come From

情境2026 年初车企掀起七年低息车贷大战，从特斯拉到小米、理想、小鹏。语域：casual 播客拆解 + 消费者警示。

01
中文

核心机制：特斯拉1月6日率先推出Model 3/Y七年低息（首付7.99万/5.99万，月供1918/2263元），10天后雷军跟进YU7（首付4.99万，月供2593元），理想再卷到首付3.25万，小鹏Mona M03首付仅1.79万、月供1355元

EN

The mechanism: on January 6, Tesla kicked off seven-year low-interest plans on the Model 3 and Model Y — 79,900 and 59,900 yuan down, with monthly installments of 1,918 and 2,263 yuan. Ten days later Lei Jun followed with the YU7 at 49,900 down and 2,593 a month; Li Auto pushed down to 32,500; and the Xpeng Mona M03 dropped to just 17,900 down and 1,355 a month.

表达提示low-interest plan → 低息方案；down payment → 首付；monthly installment → 月供；kick off → 率先推出

02
中文

关键理解：这不是单纯降价，而是用未来几年的厂家利润和利息补贴换销量，本质是时间和杠杆的游戏

EN

The key insight: this isn't plain price-cutting — it's trading years of future manufacturer profit and interest subsidies for sales volume. At its core, it's a game of time and leverage.

表达提示price-cutting → 降价；interest subsidy → 利息补贴；sales volume → 销量；leverage → 杠杆

03
中文

典型场景：原本二三十万新车买不起的人，现在首付三四万、月供两千多就觉得「没压力」

EN

The typical scene: people who couldn't afford a 200-300-thousand-yuan new car now feel "no pressure" with a 30-40-thousand down payment and 2,000-plus monthly payments.

表达提示afford → 买得起；feel no pressure → 觉得没压力

04
中文

（引用）原文依据：「原先二三十万的新的原车自己根本就买不起，现在首付只要几万块钱，月供就两千多块钱就可以把车开走了。」

EN

表达提示drive it away → 把车开走；a few thousand down → 几万块首付

05

（引用）相关概念：与五年免息相比，七年多出24期月供；门槛更低但绑定时间更长

EN

表达提示zero-interest → 免息；locked in → 绑定；barrier → 门槛

06
中文

Model Y后驱标续：不贴息年化4.69%、七年利息约4万；贴息后年化1.36%、利息仅1万多，相当于厂家贴了约3万。小米YU7标准版七年省2万多利息。但三刀直言：「如果你觉得很划算你就准备上车了，那我就告诉你你就上套了。」

EN

For the Model Y RWD long-range: without subsidies, the annual rate is 4.69% and seven years of interest runs about 40,000 yuan; with the subsidy it's 1.36% and only about 10,000 — so the manufacturer effectively subsidized around 30,000. The Xiaomi YU7 base model saves over 20,000 in interest across seven years. But Sando is blunt: "If you think it's a great deal and you're about to get on board, then let me tell you — you've taken the bait."

表达提示annual rate → 年化；subsidize → 贴息；great deal → 很划算；take the bait → 上套/上钩

Part 2 · Two Models: Traditional Auto Loan vs. Financing Lease

情境特斯拉走银行直贷，国产车企多走融资租赁；教听众识别两种合同的差异。语域：casual 播客拆解 + 避坑指南。

01
中文

原因：特斯拉七年低息走银行直接贷款（年化0.98%-2.99%），但对个人征信要求较高。小米、理想、小鹏、吉利银河、蓝图、东风奕派等基本都走平安租赁、狮桥、易鑫金融或车企自家融资租赁公司

EN

The reason: Tesla's seven-year low-interest plan runs through direct bank loans — annual rates of 0.98%-2.99% — but requires solid personal credit. Xiaomi, Li Auto, Xpeng, Geely Galaxy, Voyah, and Dongfeng Yipai mostly go through Ping An Leasing, Shibei, Easy Finance, or the carmakers' own financing-lease companies.

表达提示direct bank loan → 银行直贷；personal credit → 个人征信；financing lease → 融资租赁；leasing company → 租赁公司

02

EN

The fix: before signing, check whether the contract title contains "financing lease" or "sale-and-leaseback"; ask who the car is registered to and who holds the green certificate; and read every page of the contract, front and back, word by word.

表达提示sale-and-leaseback → 售后回租；green certificate → 绿本；word by word → 逐字阅读

表达提示critical → 致命/极其严重；switch back → 改回

Part 3 · 融资租赁的隐性成本清单

Part 3 · The Hidden-Cost Checklist of Financing Leases

情境融资租赁的强制全险、GPS、登记证书费用等隐性成本清单。语域：casual 播客拆解 + 算账教学。

01
中文

原因：融资租赁公司为降低风险，合同要求七年全程店内购买全险。以小米YU7标准版为例，第一年全险8818元 vs 正常购买约6700元，每年差近2000元，七年累计多交1万多

EN

The reason: financing-lease companies want to lower risk, so the contract requires buying full in-store insurance for all seven years. Take the Xiaomi YU7 base model: first-year comprehensive insurance is 8,818 yuan versus about 6,700 for a normal purchase — nearly 2,000 more per year, over 10,000 extra across seven years.

表达提示comprehensive insurance / full insurance → 全险；in-store → 店内；versus → 对比；markup → 溢价

02
中文

解法：签合同时核算全险溢价是否抵消厂家贴息；若七年全险多花1万+，2-3万贴息实际到手可能只剩1万多

EN

The fix: at signing, calculate whether the full-insurance markup cancels out the manufacturer subsidy — if the seven-year premium adds over 10,000, the 20-to-30-thousand subsidy may really leave you only 10,000-plus.

表达提示cancel out → 抵消；premium → 保费；in effect → 实际到手

03
中文

严重程度：小心（金额可预期，但很多人签约时未计入）

EN

Severity: moderate — the amount is predictable, but many people don't factor it in when signing.

表达提示predictable → 可预期的；factor in → 计入；moderate → 中等

04
中文

（引用）三刀说法：「必须店内全险购买，合同里面写得清清楚楚……你每一年都要多交这个钱。」

EN

"In-store full insurance is mandatory, and it's written clearly in the contract… you pay that extra money every single year."

表达提示mandatory → 强制；written clearly → 写得清清楚楚；every single year → 每一年

05
中文

原因：融资租赁默认你信用不足，装GPS（500-2000元）防追车；改色备案需借登记证书120元/次，加急另收50元；还清后拆GPS还要再付钱

EN

The reason: financing leases assume your credit is weak, so they install a GPS device — 500 to 2,000 yuan — to track the car. Repainting requires borrowing the registration certificate at 120 yuan per trip, plus 50 for rush service; and after you pay off the loan, removing the GPS costs even more.

表达提示GPS tracker → GPS 定位；registration certificate → 登记证书；pay off the loan → 还清贷款

06
中文

解法：把这些费用加进总成本表，与贴息金额对冲后再决定是否划算

EN

The fix: add these costs to your total-cost table and offset them against the subsidy before deciding whether it's worth it.

表达提示total-cost table → 总成本表；offset against → 与…对冲；worth it → 划算

Paraphrase & Chunks 3 组表达

  1. 想说「强制全险吃掉了贴息」

    eat 表示「侵蚀/吞掉」，口语传神

  2. 想说「合同里藏着很多费用」

    fine print · add-on fees · line item
    fine print 是「合同小字」的常用说法

  3. 想说「算总账才能判断划不划算」

    run the full numbers · call it a deal
    run the numbers 是「算账」口语；deal 指「划算的交易」

Part 4 · 为什么是现在？金融战的深层逻辑

Part 4 · Why Now? The Deeper Logic of the Financial War

情境特斯拉发起金融战的时机选择，以及低首付如何把资产决策偷换成消费决策。语域：casual 播客点评 + 商业逻辑。

01
中文

2026年1月车市「相当惨劣」，补贴退坡、价格战乏力，特斯拉用七年银行低息打金融战——用未来利润换量，在国产车企精疲力尽时收割市场。国产跟进的融资租赁方案成本更低、风控更狠，但把风险转嫁给了消费者

EN

In January 2026 the car market was "quite miserable": subsidies were phasing out and the price war had lost steam. Tesla launched a financial war with seven-year bank low-interest loans — trading future profit for volume, harvesting the market while domestic carmakers are exhausted. The domestic follow-ups use cheaper financing-lease structures with harsher risk control, but they shift the risk onto consumers.

表达提示price war → 价格战；lose steam → 乏力；phase out → 退坡；harvest the market → 收割市场；shift risk onto → 把风险转嫁给

02
中文

（引用）「特斯拉他发起金融战的时间点应该是内部精确算过的……看你们这些国产汽车品牌互相厮杀，现在都打累了吧，好我来刺激一波。」

EN

"Tesla must have calculated the timing of this financial war internally… watching you domestic brands slash each other — tired now, right? Fine, let me stir up another wave."

表达提示calculated timing → 精确算过时机；slash each other → 互相厮杀；stir up a wave → 刺激一波

03
中文

核心机制：买30万车本是重大资产决策，需要十几万存款+下决心；现在变成四五万首付+两千多月供，对标的是「开个视频会员」的消费心理

EN

The core mechanism: buying a 300,000-yuan car used to be a major asset decision — requiring over 100,000 in savings and real resolve. Now it's a 40-to-50-thousand down payment plus a 2,000-plus monthly bill, benchmarked against the psychology of "opening a video-streaming subscription."

表达提示asset decision → 资产决策；savings → 存款；resolve → 下决心；benchmark against → 对标

04
中文

关键理解：钱包厚度跟不上想法高度，就是超前消费。新能源车迭代快（电池、智驾），多数人3-5年就想换车，但贷款绑七年，第4-5年可能出现贷款余额超过车辆残值的负资产

EN

The key insight: when your wallet's thickness can't keep up with your ambition's height, that's living beyond your means. EVs iterate fast — batteries, assisted driving — so most people want a new car in 3-5 years, yet the loan binds them for seven; by years four to five, the outstanding balance can exceed the car's residual value — negative equity.

表达提示live beyond your means → 超前消费；assisted driving → 智驾；residual value → 车辆残值；negative equity → 负资产

05
中文

（引用）原文依据：「这叫做钱包的厚度跟不上想法的高度，这就是超前消费，这就是把资产决策偷换成消费决策的典型案例。」

EN

表达提示swap A for B → 把 A 偷换成 B；consumption decision → 消费决策；spend ahead of your means → 超前消费

表达提示replacement cycle → 换机周期；iterate → 迭代；increasingly unrealistic → 越来越不现实

Part 5 · 贷款期限与换车周期匹配

Part 5 · Matching Loan Term with Your Replacement Cycle

情境收尾：为什么等一等可能是更好的选择，以及如何用折旧计算器算总账。语域：casual 播客点评 + 决策建议。

01
中文

为什么重要：低首付低月供是用时间换来的，总成本并没有少多少。二十多万的车仍然是二十多万，利息该有还是有

EN

Why it matters: low down payment and low monthly installments are bought with time — the total cost isn't actually much lower. A 200-plus-thousand-yuan car is still a 200-plus-thousand-yuan car, and interest is still interest.

表达提示bought with time → 用时间换来的；total cost → 总成本

02
中文

适用边界：2026年三刀判断「100%是等等党的胜利」，媒体施压后部分品牌可能调整为银行车贷，已签融资租赁合同则无法反悔

EN

The boundary: for 2026, Sando judges "it's 100% the waiters' victory." After media pressure, some brands may switch to bank auto loans — but if you've already signed a financing-lease contract, there's no going back.

表达提示waiters' victory → 等等党的胜利；media pressure → 媒体施压；no going back → 无法反悔

03

（引用）「风险不在于市场下跌时投资，而在于你不理解自己持有什么、为何持有。」——霍华德·马克斯《投资最重要的事》

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The car loan is very cheap, I can buy a car now.
✓ Low monthly payments don't mean the car is cheap — run the full seven-year cost.

「便宜」要分清是车价、利率还是月供；用 low monthly payments + total cost 区分，避免被月供数字误导。

✕ The car is under my name after I buy it.
✓ With a financing lease, the car isn't registered to you until the contract ends.

融资租赁期间车辆登记在租赁公司名下，用 isn't registered to you 说明法律归属，而非想当然的 under my name。

✕ I must buy insurance in the store for 7 years.
✓ The contract mandates in-store comprehensive insurance for all seven years.

「必须店内买」是合同强制条款，用 mandate/require 表达约束力；in-store 表地点。

✕ The car price will become lower than my loan.
✓ By year four or five, the outstanding balance can exceed the car's resale value.

「贷款比车还贵」要用专业概念：outstanding balance（未还余额）与 resale value（残值），即 negative equity。

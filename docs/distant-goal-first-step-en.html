一、Be Human：Think Big 的第一块地基

Be Human: The Foundation of Thinking Big

情境为什么敢想敢做的第一步是做人——真诚尊重、理解、鼓励别人，而不是把别人当工具。语域：casual 播客讨论 + 心理学依据（皮格马利翁效应）。

01
中文

核心机制：与其把别人当作帮助自己成功的工具，不如真诚地尊重、理解和鼓励别人。作者把它放在五条的第一位，主播们最初想跳过，细想后才意识到：你可以 think big，但不能只顾自己，也不能觉得别人不如你

EN

Instead of treating people as tools for your own success, sincerely respect, understand, and encourage them. The author put this principle first among the five, and the hosts almost skipped it at first — until they realized you can think big, but you can't only care about yourself, and you can't look down on others.

表达提示think big → 敢想大事；look down on sb → 看不起别人；put sth first → 把…放在第一位

02
中文

关键理解：Think Big 不应带来优越感——恰恰相反，它让你意识到每个人都有自己对"big"的理解，不拿自己的标准去衡量别人。它在保证你的价值观和出发目的不是"彰显自己"，而是"真的奔向自己渴望的方向"

EN

Thinking Big shouldn't give you a sense of superiority — quite the opposite: it makes you realize everyone has their own version of "big," so you stop measuring others by your standards. It makes sure your starting point isn't showing off, but genuinely running toward the direction you long for.

表达提示a sense of superiority → 优越感；measure sb by one's standards → 用自己的标准衡量别人；long for → 渴望

03
中文

典型场景：你是创业者，就别觉得朝九晚五上班的人是"安逸"；你在坚持读书，也别觉得刷短视频的人"在浪费时间"。这正应了书里那个"农场采摘"的随手例子：结账时后面没人排队、农场主在微笑，他没有匆忙交钱走人，而是选择和对方闲聊——无论身处什么位置，都给予他人信任和耐心

EN

If you're an entrepreneur, don't call nine-to-five workers "comfortable"; if you're committed to reading, don't call short-video scrollers "wasting time." It echoes the book's farm-picking example: no one in line at checkout, the farmer smiling — the visitor didn't rush off, but chose to chat with him. Whatever position you're in, give others trust and patience.

表达提示nine-to-five → 朝九晚五的；scroller → 刷短视频的人；echo → 呼应/印证

04

The psychological evidence, the Pygmalion effect: researchers randomly picked a group of students but told the teachers these were the most promising kids — a year later their grades really jumped. What changed wasn't IQ but the teachers' belief, which led to more encouragement, patience, and opportunity. Expectations shape performance — for others, and for yourself.

表达提示Pygmalion effect → 皮格马利翁效应（期望效应）；promising → 有潜力的；expectations shape performance → 期待塑造表现

（引用）相关概念：与"吸引力法则""显化"等流行词同源，但这里的落点是行动导向的信念，而非玄学等待

EN

It shares roots with popular terms like the law of attraction and manifestation, but here it lands on action-oriented belief, not mystical waiting.

二、"相信就会实现"：从"我希望"到"我要让它发生"

Believe and It Will Happen: From "I Wish" to "I'll Make It Happen"

情境把"能不能做到"改写成"该怎么做到"，用求职直邮、瑜伽老师的真实例子说明信念如何改变行为。语域：casual 播客 + 个人故事。

01
中文

核心机制：当一个人相信一件事有可能，行为模式就会随之改变——更愿意尝试、更愿意争取、更容易坚持。逻辑链是：不是因为事情做不到所以我们不去做，而是因为我们先认定做不到，很多事情才真的没有发生

EN

When a person truly believes something is possible, their behavior changes — they're more willing to try, to push, to persist. The logic chain runs backwards: it's not that we skip things because they're impossible; we first declare them impossible, and that's why they never happen.

表达提示logic chain → 逻辑链；declare sth impossible → 先认定做不到；willing to try → 愿意尝试

02
中文

关键理解：把问题从"能不能做到"改写成"如果能做到，我该怎么做"，这一问法适用于每个人。所谓"不可能""做不到"，很多时候是未经验证的自我判断，是一种懒惰的惯性，也是避免失败和丢脸的托词。万事开头难，一旦跨过"从不可能到可能"这个开头，后面的问题都能靠实际行动一一化解

EN

Rewrite the question from "can I do it?" to "if I could, how would I?" — and it applies to everyone. So-called "impossible" or "can't be done" is often an untested self-judgment, a lazy habit, and an excuse to avoid failure and embarrassment. The beginning is the hardest; once you cross the gap from impossible to possible, the rest gets solved one action at a time.

表达提示rewrite the question → 改写问题；self-judgment → 自我判断；the beginning is the hardest → 万事开头难

03
中文

典型场景：主播当年求职时没有海投，因为 Dream Company 很清晰，于是直接找到老板联系方式发邮件，竟然真的得到回复和面试机会，一待就是十年。讽刺的是，这个"敢想敢做"的开局，十年后却迟迟没能给人生划上新的结局——年轻时敢做的本能，长大后变成了畏手畏脚

EN

When the host was job hunting, she didn't mass-apply, because her dream company was clear. She found the boss's contact and emailed him directly — she actually got a reply and an interview, and ended up staying ten years. The irony: the bold opening she managed at twenty-something couldn't, ten years later, write a new ending for her life — the instinct to dare she had when young turned into hesitation as she grew older.

表达提示mass-apply → 海投简历；dream company → 理想公司；irony → 讽刺之处；hesitation → 畏手畏脚

04
中文

（引用）作者现身说法（Nina 的瑜伽老师梦）：原本"希望自己有一天成为瑜伽老师"只是模糊愿望；改成"我要在今年年底前成为"之后，她凌晨4点起床去健身房教课、每周给军营战友上课、周末去工作室带课。真正改变的不是外部条件，而是思维方式——从"我希望这件事发生"变成"我要想办法让它发生"

EN

Nina's yoga-teacher dream: "I hope to become a yoga teacher one day" was just a vague wish. After she changed it to "I will become one by the end of this year," she got up at four a.m. to teach at the gym, taught military trainees every week, and took weekend classes at the studio. What really changed wasn't the external conditions — it was the mindset: from "I hope this happens" to "I'll find a way to make it happen."

表达提示vague wish → 模糊的愿望；mindset → 思维方式；make it happen → 让它实现

05
中文

（引用）目标的正确打开方式：根据现有能力制定的"目标"够得着、最安全，但从设立一开始就在能力范围内，那只是计划。Think Big 的目标应带一点引领性、超越性——大到今天需要垫脚、费点劲、还够不到，不得不再成长一下才能接住它

EN

A goal based on your current ability is reachable and safe, but if it's within your ability from day one, it's just a plan. A Think Big goal should lead and stretch you — big enough that today you need to stand on tiptoe, struggle a little, and still can't reach it, so you have to grow a bit more to catch it.

表达提示stand on tiptoe → 踮脚；stretch sb → 让人成长；reachable → 够得着的

06
中文

主播做品牌时，供应商对"这种材质能不能做、压模塑形能不能打孔"之类的新设想，通常秒回"不行"。但他们的产品负责人有一种"打破砂锅问到底"的精神：为什么不行？为什么做不到？技术难点到底在哪里？有没有办法绕过这个难点达成目的？在一连串拆解问题的追问下，很多原本的难题真的迎刃而解

EN

When the hosts build their brand, suppliers answer new ideas like "can this material be made, can this mold be punched" with a quick "no." But their product lead has a keep-digging spirit: why not? Why can't it be done? Where exactly is the technical difficulty? Is there a way to work around it and still reach the goal? Under that chain of questions, many original problems simply dissolve.

表达提示keep digging → 打破砂锅问到底；work around → 绕过难点；dissolve → 迎刃而解

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「做不到往往是先入为主的自我设限」

    decided it was · self-imposed limits
    decided it was 更口语，self-imposed limits 更书面

  2. 想说「从希望到行动」

    stop wishing, start planning · turn I hope into I will
    两者都是口号式短句，适合励志场景

三、Ignore the Haters：别让泼冷水的人替你决定

Ignore the Haters: Don't Let the Cold-Water People Decide for You

情境泼冷水的人未必是坏人，关键是别让别人的判断替你决定；引用"少听建议"的受访者与主播好友的例子。语域：casual 播客讨论 + 名言引用。

01
中文

关键理解：这些人并不代表"坏人"，可能只是天生保守或消极的思维模式，甚至"对待自己时更加苛刻"。主播有位好友常把"不行""但是"挂嘴边，但那是从现实出发为她考量、怕她把事情想得太容易——而恰恰要反过来想：把事情想得容易一点，它才会真的变容易一点

EN

These people aren't necessarily bad — they may just have a naturally cautious or negative mindset, and are sometimes even harsher on themselves. The host has a close friend who always says "no way" and "but," yet that friend was genuinely looking out for her, afraid she'd take things too lightly. And the trick is to think in reverse: treat things as a little easier, and they really do become easier.

表达提示cautious mindset → 保守思维；look out for sb → 为某人着想；in reverse → 反过来

02
中文

（引用）"如果你能把80%的时间花在积极思考上——哪怕只是中立思考——那就是一件再好不过的事。"

EN

"If you could spend 80% of your time thinking positively — or even just neutrally — that would be a wonderful thing."

表达提示spend time doing → 花时间做…；neutrally → 中立地；positively → 积极地

03
中文

面对同一件事，一定有人说"千万别干，我就是被它耽误的"，也有人说"它彻底改变了我的人生"。这时该想的是：当下自己的处境、自己的经验，而不是直接听由任何一方帮你做决定

EN

Facing the same thing, someone will always say "don't do it, it ruined my life," and someone else will say "it completely changed my life." At that moment, think about your own situation and your own experience — don't just let either side decide for you.

表达提示ruin one's life → 毁掉人生；changed my life → 改变我的人生；let sb decide for you → 让别人替你决定

04
中文

一位受访者被记者问"有什么建议给年轻人"，回答只有四个字：少听建议。这不代表周围都是噪音，而是提醒你：别人的经验和生存方式与你不同，别人的终点未必是你的终点，别人遇到的坎坷未必是你会遇到的

EN

One interviewee, asked what advice he'd give young people, answered in four words: take less advice. That doesn't mean everyone around you is noise — it reminds you that other people's experience and way of living differ from yours; their finish line may not be yours, and the bumps they hit may not be yours.

表达提示finish line → 终点；bump → 坎坷；take less advice → 少听建议

05
中文

（引用）Think Big 在此处的含义：始终愿意相信——别人走过的路所到的终点，未必就是我的终点。我有能力去相信"自己故事版本"的走向，这是一种难得的 think big

EN

This is what Think Big means here: always be willing to believe that the destination of the road others walked isn't necessarily my destination. I have the power to believe in the direction of my own version of the story — that's a rare kind of thinking big.

表达提示destination → 终点/目的地；my own version of the story → 自己版本的故事；a rare kind of … → 一种难得的…

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「少听别人的建议」

    take less advice · outsource your decisions
    outsource your decisions 很地道，形容把决定权外包给别人

  2. 想说「别人的终点不是我的终点」

    finish line · what stopped them won't stop me
    第二句更有力量，适合宣言式表达

四、One Step at a Time：想得大，但还是一步一步来

One Step at a Time: Think Big, but Walk Step by Step

情境Think Big 需要 Do Small 配合：拆解目标、触发机制、二战战地记者徒步丛林的例子，以及拆解的适用边界。语域：casual 播客 + 故事叙述。

01
中文

核心机制：真正的 Think Big 离不开 Do Small——把一个大的目标拆解成一个又一个具体的小行动。如果你要写一本25万字的小说，每天告诉自己"我要写一本25万字的小说"，大概率会被挑战吓到然后放弃；但只告诉自己"今天要写800字"，就更容易开始做这件事

EN

Real thinking big can't work without doing small — break a big goal into one concrete small action after another. If you want to write a 250,000-word novel and tell yourself every day "I'm going to write a 250,000-word novel," you'll probably be scared off and quit; but if you only tell yourself "write 800 words today," starting becomes much easier.

表达提示break … into → 拆分成；scare off → 吓退；250,000-word → 25万字的

02
中文

关键理解：完成目标的第一步其实是拆解目标——拆到你觉得第一步自己可以轻松完成的时候，就够了。Think Big 给的是方向，Do Small 给的是临门一脚的路径，从方向到路径，是难走的第一步

EN

The first step to finishing a goal is actually breaking it down — until the first step feels easy enough to do, that's enough. Think Big gives you the direction; Do Small gives you the path for that final push. From direction to path is the hardest first step.

表达提示break it down → 拆解目标；the final push → 临门一脚；from direction to path → 从方向到路径

03
中文

典型场景：主播曾约定"每晚九点半读书两小时"，结果经常没开始就直接放弃。后来改成触发机制："只要开始玩手机超过半小时，就该去读书。"启动动作变得极其具体，就更容易开始，而一旦开始，后面的小行动会顺利展开

EN

The host once planned to "read for two hours at nine-thirty every night" and usually gave up before even starting. Later she switched to a trigger: "once I've been on my phone for more than half an hour, it's time to read." The starting action became extremely concrete, so it was easier to begin — and once begun, the following small actions flow smoothly.

表达提示trigger mechanism → 触发机制；concrete → 具体的；flow smoothly → 顺利展开

04
中文

适用边界：拆解适合"大而模糊"的目标。若目标本身就小，过度拆解反而徒增管理成本；拆解也不解决"方向错了"的问题，它只负责让"开始"变轻

EN

The boundary: breaking down works for big, vague goals. If the goal is already small, over-breaking it only adds management cost. And breaking down doesn't fix a wrong direction — it only makes "starting" lighter.

表达提示boundary → 适用边界；vague → 模糊的；management cost → 管理成本

05
中文

（引用）二战战地记者的故事：飞机失事被迫跳伞到缅甸与印度边境的丛林，必须徒步穿越140英里山地才能获救。有人脚被钉子扎伤、有人满脚血泡，光想到140英里就已绝望。后来他们不再想140英里，只想翻过眼前这座山、走到下一个村庄——最后真的一步一步走出了丛林

EN

A WWII war correspondent's story: his plane crashed and he had to parachute into the jungle at the Burma–India border, then hike 140 miles through the mountains to be rescued. Some got nails in their feet, some were covered in blisters; just thinking about 140 miles was despair. So they stopped thinking about 140 miles and only thought about climbing the hill in front of them and reaching the next village — and finally, step by step, they walked out of the jungle.

表达提示war correspondent → 战地记者；hike 140 miles → 徒步140英里；blister → 血泡；one step at a time → 一步一步来

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「只关注眼前这一步」

    focus on the next hill · reach the next village
    第一句适合总结，第二句带故事感

  2. 想说「让启动变轻」

    embarrassingly small · lower the bar
    embarrassingly small 是英文里很地道的说法

五、Do More：想得大，更要做得多

Do More: Think Big, Then Do Even More

情境忙碌反而让人更高效，但也要避开"能者多劳"和"瞎忙"两个陷阱，落点是 Do More Life 而非 Do More Work。语域：casual 播客 + 方法论。

01
中文

核心机制：当一个人真正忙起来，反而更清楚什么事情值得做、什么事情可以不做——因为忙碌逼着你学会极致的效率，也逼着你做取舍。Nina 发现一旦把课程、任务、兴趣全部塞进 to-do list，工作效率立马提升：工作量增加意味着必须停止把时间浪费在刷手机、随机刷到的短文等"时间黑洞"上

EN

When a person is truly busy, they become clearer about what's worth doing and what can be skipped — because busyness forces you to be extremely efficient and to make trade-offs. Nina found that once she packed her courses, tasks, and hobbies into a to-do list, her efficiency instantly improved: more work meant she had to stop wasting time on "time sinks" like scrolling through her phone.

表达提示make trade-offs → 做取舍；time sink → 时间黑洞；pack into → 塞进

02
中文

关键理解：时间好像还够用时，人会不断给自己留余地、留退路；一旦资源（时间、精力、注意力）变有限，反而会认真思考"当下真正重要的事是什么"。Do More 就是主动把自己的资源变得有限，从而无法无意识浪费，逼自己为每一件事判断"值不值得"

EN

When time seems abundant, people keep leaving room and a way out; once resources — time, energy, attention — become limited, you seriously ask what truly matters right now. Do More is about actively making your resources finite, so you can't waste them unconsciously and have to judge whether everything is worth it.

表达提示leave a way out → 留退路；finite → 有限的；unconsciously → 无意识地

03
中文

（引用）"If you want something done, ask a busy person to do it." （想让事情做成，就去找一个忙人来做。）——越忙的人脑中规划越清晰，新增任务也会根据难易被迅速安排进既有规划，完成后还有"爽感"驱动下一件事；而空白的清单反而需要热身、缺乏紧迫感，拖延成为常态

EN

"If you want something done, ask a busy person to do it." Busier people have clearer mental plans, so new tasks get quickly slotted in by difficulty, and completing them gives a satisfaction boost that drives the next thing; an empty list, by contrast, needs warm-up and lacks urgency, so procrastination becomes the norm.

表达提示mental plan → 脑中规划；slot in → 安排进；satisfaction boost → 成就感加成；procrastination → 拖延

04
中文

陷阱1：能者多劳的绑架。被"能者多劳"的说辞绑架，承担了许多本不属于自己的工作，在别人越干越轻松时自己忙上加忙。职场里很难完全避免，因此一定要明确工作的边界，并且敢于说不

EN

Pitfall one: the "able people do more" trap. Bound by that saying, you take on plenty of work that isn't yours, and while others get lighter, you get busier. It's hard to fully avoid at work, so define your boundaries and dare to say no.

表达提示the able-people-do-more trap → 能者多劳的绑架；boundary → 工作边界；say no → 说不

05
中文

陷阱2：瞎忙。忙着忙着忘了为什么开始忙。在"及时回复、唾手可得"的 AI 时代尤其危险——为了搭上时代的便车而冲动开启事情，如果只是跟风，到头来只会给自己徒增负担

EN

Pitfall two: busy-busy with no point. You forget why you started. In the AI age of instant replies and everything at your fingertips, it's especially dangerous — starting things on impulse to ride the wave, but if it's just following the trend, you only add burdens to yourself.

表达提示at your fingertips → 唾手可得；ride the wave → 搭上时代的便车；follow the trend → 跟风

06
中文

（引用）解法：Do More Life，而不是 Do More Work。不要给人生增加更多任务或打卡项，而是增加更多维度——do more exercise、do more experiences，从没尝试过的爱好或体验开始。新鲜的体验能在某种程度上让你的思维突然变得截然不同、更宽广

EN

The fix: do more life, not more work. Don't add more tasks or check-ins to your life — add more dimensions. Do more exercise, do more experiences, starting with a hobby or experience you've never tried. Fresh experiences can, in some way, suddenly make your thinking completely different and wider.

表达提示dimension → 维度；fresh experience → 新鲜的体验；check-in → 打卡项

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「忙人反而高效」

    busy people get things done · already busy
    第二句是英文谚语变体，很地道

  2. 想说「别瞎忙」

    confuse activity with progress · for the right reasons
    第一句适合正式点评，第二句适合日常建议
S6

六、玫瑰岛：敢想敢做到底意味着什么

The Rose Island: What Thinking Big Truly Means

情境玫瑰岛共和国的真实故事——敢想敢做的极致样本，以及"所有伟大的事一开始都像胡闹"的收尾。语域：casual 播客 + 电影推荐。

01
中文

主播推荐电影《玫瑰岛的不可思议的历史》：一位郁郁不得志的意大利工程师，突然萌生一个近乎异想天开的念头——在离意大利不远的公海上建一座属于自己的岛，成立一个不受任何国家管辖的国家。离谱的是，他真的开始做了：不先想"会不会成功"，而是先研究能不能建、怎么建、需要什么材料、怎么把立柱运过去、又怎么让它在海里立起来。他一步一步把一个别人眼里的幻想变成了现实

EN

The hosts recommend the film The Incredible Story of Rose Island: a frustrated Italian engineer suddenly gets a wild idea — build his own island on international waters near Italy and found a nation not governed by any country. Absurdly, he actually started: instead of asking whether it would succeed, he studied whether it could be built, how, what materials were needed, how to ship the columns, and how to make them stand in the sea. Step by step, he turned a fantasy in other people's eyes into reality.

表达提示frustrated → 郁郁不得志的；wild idea → 异想天开的念头；international waters → 公海；found a nation → 建国

02
中文

关键理解：这不是虚构，而是半个多世纪前真实发生的"玫瑰岛共和国"。小岛最终被政府炸毁，从世俗意义上算不上成功——但它证明：很多事情并不是因为一定会成功才值得去做，而是因为有人愿意相信它有可能，世界才因此多了一种新的可能性

EN

The key: this isn't fiction — the Republic of Rose Island really existed half a century ago. The government eventually blew it up, so by worldly standards it wasn't a success. But it proves that many things aren't worth doing because they will surely succeed — they create a new possibility in the world simply because someone was willing to believe they could.

表达提示by worldly standards → 按世俗标准；blow up → 炸毁；a new possibility → 一种新的可能性

03
中文

（引用）"所有伟大的事，一开始都挺像在胡闹的，但不知怎么着，他就成了。"

EN

"Everything great looks like fooling around at first — but somehow, he just made it work."

表达提示fool around → 胡闹；somehow → 不知怎么地；make it work → 让它成了

04
中文

（引用）2017年，卓尔·之尔（Giorgio Rosa）逝世前夕，一群来自里米尼的潜水员潜入海底，从废墟中打捞起一块当年的砖块送给他，上面写着： "里米尼的潜水员，很荣幸把梦的碎片，还给做梦的人。"

EN

In 2017, before Giorgio Rosa passed away, a group of divers from Rimini dived to the seafloor and salvaged one of the island's original bricks to give him. Written on it: "The divers of Rimini are honored to return the fragments of a dream to the dreamer."

表达提示salvage → 打捞；fragments of a dream → 梦的碎片；be honored to → 很荣幸做…

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「伟大起初像胡闹」

    looks ridiculous · looks like madness · becomes legend
    第一句直白，第二句更有修辞

  2. 想说「值得做不是因为一定会成功」

    not because guaranteed · belief is the reason to start
    第二句适合做宣言金句

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

用「think big」说：目标可以想得很大，但行动要落在眼前这一步。

You can think big, but your action has to land on the step right in front of you.

用「rewrite the question」说：把"能不能做到"改写成"该怎么做到"。

Rewrite the question from "can I do it?" to "how would I do it?"

用「one step at a time」说：不要想140英里，只想翻过眼前这座山。

Don't think about the 140 miles — just climb the hill in front of you, one step at a time.

用「take less advice」说：别人的终点未必是你的终点。

Take less advice — other people's finish lines aren't yours.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Think big is just to think positive.
✓ Thinking big means refusing to limit yourself early, not just staying positive.

think big 不是单纯积极心态；动词短语作主语要用动名词 thinking，且要点出"不设限"的落点，信息才完整。

✕ He is a person who always sprinkles cold water.
✓ He's the type who always pours cold water on new ideas.

泼冷水是固定搭配 pour cold water on sth（对象是想法/计划），不是 sprinkle；也不用硬译"person"，用 the type who 更自然。

✕ Break the big target into small targets.
✓ Break the big goal down into small, doable steps.

人生/学习目标英文用 goal，target 多指业绩或射击目标；拆解用 break down into，且落在 doable steps 更具体。

✕ Ignore the haters is the third lesson.
✓ Ignoring the haters is the third lesson.

动词原形不能直接作主语，作主语要用动名词 ignoring 或不定式 to ignore。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

把目标设得小一点才现实、才不容易失败 → 目标可以想得很大，只要把注意力和行动放在眼前的一小步上
做不到就是做不到，别再想了 → 把"做不到"改写成"该怎么做到"，拆开问题逐一解决
忙起来容易瞎忙，能闲则闲 → 主动让自己资源变有限，逼自己判断每件事值不值得；Do More Life 而非 Do More Work

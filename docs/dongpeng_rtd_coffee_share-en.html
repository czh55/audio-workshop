How Dongpeng, an Energy-Drink Maker, Overtook Starbucks in Ready-to-Drink Coffee

This episode reads MaShangYing's retail data showing Dongpeng climbing to second place in offline ready-to-drink (RTD) coffee sales, barely edging past Starbucks — while Nestlé still holds nearly half the market. The hosts break down how Dongpeng, born as an energy-drink maker, copied its winning playbook: big bottles, low prices, deep lower-tier distribution, and a "one-yuan happiness" giveaway strategy. They also flag that the RTD coffee category is nearly stagnant, with fresh-brewed coffee steadily siphoning demand.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Ready-drink coffee is bottled coffee.
✓ Ready-to-drink (RTD) coffee is bottled or canned coffee you can sip on the go.

ready-drink 是漏词；正确术语是 ready-to-drink（RTD），并补一句开盖即饮的解释让定义完整。

✕ Dongpeng's share rises to second, higher than Starbucks.
✓ Dongpeng moved to second in offline sales share, slightly edging past Starbucks.

「份额升到第二」要说明是什么口径（线下销售额）；edge past / slightly ahead 比 higher than 更贴切份额语境。

✕ The coffee market's position may continue to move.
✓ The industry ranking will likely keep shifting.

「位置波动」直译成 position moves 生硬；排名/名次用 ranking、shift、swing 更地道。

✕ Dongpeng aims at truck drivers and other blue collars.
✓ It targeted blue-collar workers like truck drivers.

blue collar 是名词短语要加 worker；介词用 target + 宾语，instead of aim at 也可但 target 更常用。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为东鹏只是个能量饮料品牌 → 东鹏把能量饮料的「大容量低价+下沉渠道」打法复制到即饮咖啡，份额已超星巴克
以为份额第二 = 赛道格局已定 → 差距不足 1%、雀巢占近半，单月排名只是波动，不是咖啡战争终局
以为即饮咖啡是增长蓝海 → 即饮赛道已接近停滞，现磨咖啡持续分流，增长要靠打法而非赛道红利

FIFA expects about $13 billion in revenue over the four-year cycle, driven by three engines: broadcast rights at 40 percent, sponsorship at 25 percent, and tickets at 28 percent. Meanwhile, the nonprofit based in Switzerland holds the executive, legislative, and judicial powers of global football all in one hand.

01
中文

核心机制：从 1998 年的 32 队 64 场扩至 48 队 104 场（+63%），同时美加墨三国联办。更多比赛 = 更多转播内容 + 更多赞助露出 + 更多门票可售

EN

The mechanism: from 32 teams and 64 matches in 1998 to 48 teams and 104 matches — a 63 percent increase — with three host countries this time. More matches equals more broadcast content, more sponsor exposure, and more tickets to sell.

表达提示broadcast content → 转播内容；sponsor exposure → 赞助露出；host country → 主办国

The key insight: FIFA's bet is on total volume. Even if the value per match drops from dilution, the total revenue from 104 matches could still beat 64. It contrasts with the underwhelming joint hosting by South Korea and Japan in 2002 — but the money smell behind 2026 is much stronger.

表达提示value per match → 单场价值；dilution → 稀释；joint hosting → 联办

情境信号制作、套餐拆分与「价格歧视」定价逻辑；中国市场为何低价。语域：formal 商业机制讲解。

01
中文

核心机制：FIFA 统一拍摄制作信号（慢动作、航拍、数据画面），按平台（电视/流媒体）、赛事范围（104 场/64 场/淘汰赛）、语言（英语/西班牙语）拆分成不同套餐，逐国逐台谈判定价

EN

The mechanism: FIFA produces the unified broadcast feed itself — slow motion, aerial shots, data graphics — then splits it into packages by platform, by match range, and by language, negotiating prices country by country, broadcaster by broadcaster.

表达提示broadcast feed → 转播信号；package → 套餐；broadcaster → 电视台

02
中文

EN

The key insight: this is classic price discrimination — the same product sold at different prices based on each market's willingness to pay, like an airline charging different fares on the same flight.

表达提示price discrimination → 价格歧视；willingness to pay → 支付意愿；fare → 票价

03

A typical scene: IP licensing, SaaS pricing, and content distribution can all borrow the strategy of splitting rights and differentiating prices.

表达提示IP licensing → IP 授权；differentiated pricing → 差异化定价

Tickets: Dynamic Pricing and Resale Commissions (28%, About $3.6 Billion)

情境动态定价首年启用；官方转售抽成几乎零成本；门票商业化争议。语域：formal 机制讲解 + 争议讨论。

01
中文

原因：2026 年首次采用动态定价，综合热门程度、抢票人数、城市、阶段等因素实时调价。美国队小组赛已破 1000 美元/张，决赛最低 4000 美元起

EN

The reason: 2026 is the first year of dynamic pricing, which adjusts prices in real time based on popularity, demand, city, and stage. US team group-stage tickets already top $1,000 each, and finals start at $4,000.

表达提示dynamic pricing → 动态定价；group-stage → 小组赛；finals → 决赛

02
中文

解法：对 FIFA 而言，Hospitality VIP 套餐（单场 2000-数十万美元）才是利润主力，普通门票与 VIP 收入 55 开但利润悬殊

EN

The solution, for FIFA: hospitality VIP packages — from $2,000 to hundreds of thousands per match — are the real profit driver. Regular tickets and VIP bring in roughly even revenue, but the profit margins are worlds apart.

表达提示hospitality package → 高端观赛套餐；profit driver → 利润主力；profit margin → 利润率

03
中文

严重程度：商业上合理，但偏离「促进足球运动发展」章程宗旨，引发过度商业化争议

EN

表达提示charter → 章程；over-commercialization → 过度商业化

04
中文

（引用）越来越贵的门票可能把真正热爱足球但收入不高的球迷挡在球场外——电视看和现场感受完全是两回事

EN

The mechanism: resale used to be capped at face value — buy at $60, sell at $60. In 2026, sellers can set their own price, and both sides pay a 15 percent service fee. On a $1,000 resale, FIFA takes $300, almost entirely free money.

表达提示face value → 原价/面值；service fee → 服务费；free money → 零成本进账

06

EN

The key insight: dynamic pricing plus resale commissions push this tournament's ticket revenue to four times the previous edition — a vivid sign of FIFA's increasingly aggressive commercialization.

表达提示previous edition → 上一届；aggressive commercialization → 激进的商业化

A New Experiment: Partnering with TikTok Creators

01
中文

核心机制：FIFA 与 TikTok 合作，非完整转播权，但允许精彩片段、采访、花絮二创传播。选拔 30 位全球创作者获得幕后采访权，内容仅限 TikTok 发布，FIFA 不抽成

EN

The mechanism: FIFA partners with TikTok without selling full broadcast rights — highlights, interviews, and behind-the-scenes clips can be re-created and spread. 表达提示highlights → 精彩片段；behind-the-scenes → 幕后花絮；revenue cut → 抽成

02

The key insight: it's a proof of concept. Top-tier sponsorships cost tens of millions, which small and mid-size brands can't afford; but creators can reach the long tail of brands through single-video deals, potentially widening FIFA's revenue frontier.

表达提示proof of concept → 模式验证；long tail → 长尾；revenue frontier → 收入边界

03
中文

表达提示headline sponsor → 头部金主；creator economy → 创作者经济

六、130 亿去哪了：非营利组织的资金循环

Where the $13 Billion Goes: A Nonprofit's Money Cycle

情境非营利不等于不赚钱；协议+分期+验收的拨款机制。语域：formal 机制讲解。

01
中文

关键理解：非营利 ≠ 不赚钱。限制的是利润分配（无股东分红），剩余资金必须用于章程规定用途——促进全球足球发展

EN

The key insight: nonprofit does not mean not profitable. What's restricted is profit distribution — no shareholder dividends — and surplus must go to the charter's purpose: developing football worldwide.

表达提示profit distribution → 利润分配；dividend → 分红；surplus → 剩余资金

02
中文

每个会员协会与 FIFA 制定四年发展协议（类似中国十四五），约定项目、KPI、预算。协会提交 proposal 审批后分期拨款，有完整监督和验收机制——验收不通过则停止拨款

EN

Each member association signs a four-year development agreement with FIFA — similar to China's Five-Year Plans — specifying projects, KPIs, and budgets. The association submits a proposal; once approved, funds are released in installments, with full monitoring and review — fail the review, and the funding stops.

表达提示development agreement → 发展协议；in installments → 分期拨款；review → 验收

03
中文

（引用）怎么落地：大额基金/公益拨款可借鉴「协议+分期+验收」机制，避免撒钱无监管

EN

How to apply it: large funds and grant programs can borrow the agreement-plus-installments-plus-review model to avoid handing out money with no oversight.

表达提示grant program → 公益拨款；oversight → 监管

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「非营利不等于不赚钱」

    第一句通俗，第二句准确

中文

原因：2015 年瑞士警方逮捕 7 名 FIFA 高官，美国司法部 47 项指控涉及卡塔尔为获 2022 主办权贿赂数百万美元。部分官员同时收受多個竞标方贿赂

The cause: in 2015 Swiss police arrested seven FIFA executives; the US Justice Department filed 47 counts involving millions in bribes paid by Qatar to secure the 2022 hosting rights. Some officials took bribes from multiple bidders at once.

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

用「price discrimination」说：转播权按市场支付意愿卖不同价。

Broadcast rights are sold at different prices based on each market's willingness to pay — classic price discrimination.

用「category exclusivity」说：有可口可乐在，百事永远进不来。

With Coca-Cola signed up, Pepsi will never get category exclusivity.

用「face value」说：旧规则里转售只能按原价。

Under the old rules, resale was capped at face value.

用「separation of powers」说：FIFA 一家握着行政、立法、司法三权。

FIFA holds executive, legislative, and judicial power in one body — no separation of powers.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The ticket money is 4 times of the last time.
✓ Ticket revenue is four times that of the previous edition.

times of 是中式搭配；比较结构用 four times that of / four times as much as。

✕ FIFA is a non-profit organization, so it doesn't make money.
✓ Being a nonprofit doesn't mean FIFA doesn't make money — it just can't pay out dividends.

非营利常被误解为不赚钱；正确理解是利润不分红、剩余用于章程用途。

✕ The broadcast right sells different countries different prices.
✓ Broadcast rights are sold at different prices to different markets.

中式语序「卖出不同价格」需用被动语态加介词结构 at different prices to。

✕ The creator can make money by the video, FIFA no cut.
✓ Creators monetize their videos, and FIFA takes no cut.

no cut 缺动词且中式拼句；用 takes no cut / takes no commission。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

世界杯是体育赛事，跟商业没关系 → 扩军、转播、门票都是生意，商业化越来越激进
非营利组织=不做生意 → 非营利限制的是分红，不是赚钱
足球规则由「足球圈」说了算 → FIFA 一家集行政立法司法于一身，治理仍不透明

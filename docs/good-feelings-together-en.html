一、三点一线的枯燥里，队友是"调味剂"

In the Monotony, Teammates Are the Seasoning

情境外界只看到赛场上的一两分钟，背后是三点一线的枯燥；快乐是队友之间主动制造出来的。语域：casual 播客座谈 + 训练日常。

01
中文

核心机制：外界只看到赛场上短短一两分钟的表现，背后是日复一日、三点一线的训练生活，相处环境简单到"除了教练就是队友"

EN

The outside world only sees the one or two minutes in the pool — behind it is a day-in, day-out three-point-one-line training life, with a social circle so simple that "it's just coaches and teammates."

表达提示three-point-one-line → 三点一线；day in, day out → 日复一日；social circle → 相处环境

02
中文

关键理解：枯燥是常态，快乐不是等来的，而是队友之间主动制造出来的；判断一支队伍好不好，看它怎么面对日常的重复

EN

The key insight: monotony is the norm, and happiness isn't something you wait for — it's something teammates actively make. You can judge a team by how it handles the daily repetition.

表达提示monotony → 枯燥；judge a team by → 判断队伍好坏看…；daily repetition → 日常重复

03
中文

典型场景：认真训练时大家都很沉默；但在准备活动和放松阶段，水里会"整出一些花火"——玩笑和捉弄就成了生活乐趣的来源

EN

A typical scene: everyone is silent during serious sets, but during warm-up and cool-down, they "stir up some fireworks" in the water — jokes and pranks become the source of fun in daily life.

表达提示warm-up / cool-down → 准备活动/放松；stir up some fireworks → 整出一些花火；prank → 捉弄

04

EN

Related concept: → psychological recovery — the more monotonous it gets, the more you need off-field seasoning.

表达提示psychological recovery → 心理恢复；off-field seasoning → 场外调剂

05
中文

（引用）原文依据："大家可能更多的其实是看到我们在赛场上……短短的一分钟、两分钟……但是在这背后的话，我们一直都是三点一线，日复一日的这样的一个训练方式。"

EN

"People mostly see us on the pool deck… that short one or two minutes… but behind that, we were always three-point-one-line, training day in and day out."

表达提示pool deck → 赛场/泳池边；behind that → 背后

The mechanism: they create connection through sudden appearances, scaring people, and pranks — popping into a gaming screen out of nowhere, or quietly standing behind someone and suddenly joining the group chat.

表达提示pop into → 突然入镜；out of nowhere → 突然/不知从哪；join the group chat → 加入群聊

S2

二、一起练：良性竞争与"捞刀续刀"的推动

Training Together: Healthy Rivalry and Passing the Torch

情境队友在极限时的鼓励与"续刀"、力量训练中不自觉的互相比较。语域：casual 座谈 + 训练细节。

01
中文

核心机制：精力更旺的人在自己完成训练之后，还能观察队友状态、给鼓励、给经验——像接力一样把队友"续"下去

EN

People with more energy, after finishing their own sets, can still watch their teammates' state, offer encouragement, and share experience — passing the torch on, like a relay.

表达提示pass the torch → 把接力棒传下去；offer encouragement → 给鼓励；relay → 接力

02
中文

关键理解：到身体极限时，队友的一句鼓励有真实效力：让你感到"被支撑"；激励方式要因人而异，有人吃鼓励、有人吃刺激

EN

The key insight: at your physical limit, a teammate's one line of encouragement has real power — it makes you feel "held up." Motivation has to be tailored: some people respond to encouragement, others to a challenge.

表达提示at your limit → 到极限；feel held up → 感到被支撑；tailor motivation → 因人施激

03
中文

典型场景：强度游到最后的几个来回，汪顺永远是那句"come on yeah"；穿连体衣做模拟比赛时，他一个人就是全场的气氛担当，像一支啦啦队

EN

A typical scene: in the last few laps of an intensity set, Wang Shun always says that one "come on, yeah." And when they do mock races in bodysuits, he alone is the whole atmosphere — like a one-man cheer squad.

表达提示intensity set → 强度游；mock race → 模拟比赛；cheer squad → 啦啦队

04
中文

相关概念：→ 氛围质量→训练质量→更好的结果

EN

Related concept: → atmosphere quality → training quality → better results.

表达提示atmosphere quality → 氛围质量；training quality → 训练质量

05
中文

（引用）原文依据："就其实在你最想放弃的时候，有队友给你这么一句鼓励，你真的会觉得有被安慰到，然后有人在支撑你的感觉。"

EN

"At the moment you most want to give up, a teammate throwing you that one line of encouragement — you really feel comforted, like someone is holding you up."

表达提示give up → 放弃；feel comforted → 被安慰到；hold sb up → 支撑着某人

06
中文

核心机制：力量训练（深蹲、卧推、爆发力）中大家不自觉比较——我加重一点、我多做两个，比着比着就把潜力逼出来了

EN

The mechanism: in strength training — squats, bench press, explosive power — everyone unconsciously compares: I add a bit more weight, I do two more reps, and the comparing pushes out their potential.

表达提示strength training → 力量训练；bench press → 卧推；unconsciously compare → 不自觉比较；push out potential → 逼出潜力

三、一起扛：从被点燃到被托举

Bearing It Together: From Being Ignited to Being Carried

情境队友的拼劲会点燃你、小叶2013年全运会的紧张转化，团队战绩是定心丸。语域：casual 座谈 + 赛场回忆。

01
中文

核心机制：队友在赛场上的拼搏会在你身上引爆能量——汪顺称之为"士气会高涨"

EN

A teammate's all-out fight on the race course ignites energy in you — Wang Shun calls it "morale surging."

表达提示all-out fight → 拼尽全力；ignite energy → 点燃能量；morale surges → 士气高涨

02
中文

关键理解：力量不必靠语言传递，看见别人完成一次极限冲刺、一场反超，你身体里的一部分就会被重新点燃；这解释了"开个好头"为什么重要

EN

The key insight: strength doesn't need words — watching someone complete a limit sprint or a comeback rekindles a part of your body. That's why "getting off to a good start" matters.

表达提示limit sprint → 极限冲刺；comeback → 反超；rekindle → 重新点燃；get off to a good start → 开个好头

03
中文

典型场景：小叶2013年全运会赛前紧张到发抖，检录前看到汪顺在400混最后50米瞬间反超夺冠——"一下子热血沸腾，把紧张转化成了能量和勇气"

EN

A typical scene: before the 2013 National Games, Ye Shiwen was so nervous she was shaking; at the call room she saw Wang Shun overtake in the final 50 meters of the 400 IM to win — "my blood boiled instantly, turning the nerves into energy and courage."

表达提示the call room → 检录处；400 IM → 400米混合泳；blood boils → 热血沸腾；nerves → 紧张

04
中文

相关概念：→ 团队战绩是个人最好的"定心丸"

EN

Related concept: → the team's results are the individual's best "reassurance pill."

表达提示reassurance pill → 定心丸；team results → 团队战绩

05
中文

（引用）原文依据："那种斗志，它会一下子就像点燃我身体里的某一种能量一样……紧张一下子就转化成了一种能量、一种勇气。"

EN

"That fighting spirit suddenly ignites some energy inside me… the nerves instantly turned into energy and courage."

表达提示fighting spirit → 斗志；ignite energy → 点燃能量；turn into courage → 转化成勇气

EN

The mechanism: from March's meet to June's championship is only 12 weeks — your body can handle the training alone, but the psychological hurdle has to be "carried" by teammates.

表达提示championship → 冠军赛；psychological hurdle → 心理难关；carry sb through → 托举着走过

四、大年龄运动员：接受当下，重建心理弹性

Older Athletes: Accepting the Present and Rebuilding Resilience

情境身体恢复力下降与"找回当年的自己"的执念、心理恢复大于体能休息。语域：casual 座谈 + 运动员心法。

01
中文

原因：身体恢复力随年龄客观下降——以前能连续六天高质量完成，现在完成一天要花两三天恢复；执着于"找回当年的自己"会陷入持续焦虑

EN

The reason: recovery capacity objectively declines with age — she used to complete six high-quality days in a row; now one hard day takes two or three days to recover from. Obsessing over "getting back the old me" leads to constant anxiety.

表达提示recovery capacity → 身体恢复力；in a row → 连续；obsess over → 执着于

02
中文

解法：接受当下、向前看：不再追求长续航，把"单堂课质量做高"，该训练就训练、该吃饭就吃饭、该休息就休息，顺着身体状态把当下该做的事做好，把焦虑慢慢消化掉

EN

The solution: accept the present and look forward. Stop chasing long endurance; make each "single session high quality." Train when it's time to train, eat when it's time to eat, rest when it's time to rest — go with your body's state, do the present well, and let the anxiety dissolve gradually.

表达提示look forward → 向前看；single session → 单堂课；go with the body's state → 顺着身体状态

03
中文

（引用）原文依据："我一直想要去寻找当年的自己，我想要一口气练六天，但是时间告诉你，你再也回不去了……这段时间我持续了非常非常久。"

EN

"I kept trying to find the old me — I wanted to train six days straight, but time tells you, you can never go back… that period lasted a very, very long time."

表达提示six days straight → 一口气练六天；go back → 回到过去

04
中文

原因：大训练量之后人会失去"原始的欲望和动力"——不是身体累，是对训练本身的期待值跌了，这时候光靠躺平回不来

EN

The reason: after heavy training loads, you lose "original desire and drive" — it's not physical fatigue, it's that your expectation of training itself dropped, and lying flat won't bring it back.

表达提示heavy training load → 大训练量；original desire and drive → 原始的欲望和动力；lying flat → 躺平

05
中文

解法：主动做"和目标无关的事"：看风景、喝咖啡、尝当地的茶、哪怕发呆换个环境；让期待值和欲望值重新回到高位。小和的经验是"我会更自我放过自己"——心情好，对工作状态和成果有隐形加成

EN

The solution: actively do things "unrelated to the goal": look at scenery, drink coffee, taste local tea, even just space out somewhere new. Let expectation and desire climb back to a high level. Xiaohe's experience: "I let myself off more easily" — a good mood gives an invisible boost to your work state and results.

表达提示unrelated to the goal → 和目标无关的事；space out → 发呆；let oneself off → 放过自己；invisible boost → 隐形加成

06
中文

（引用）原文依据："以前觉得这个东西不太重要，我可能在房间里好好休息就行了……但是在体能休息之外，更重要的其实是心理上的一个恢复。"

EN

"I used to think this wasn't important, that I could just rest well in my room… but beyond physical rest, what matters more is psychological recovery."

表达提示physical rest → 体能休息；psychological recovery → 心理恢复

  1. 想说「别再执着找回当年的自己」

    第一句平实，第二句更豁达

五、给普通人的启示：把"一起"带进生活

Lessons for Everyday People: Bring the "Together" Into Life

情境找运动搭子最简单的方法是先迈开腿，邀约降低启动成本，紧张不必当成敌人。语域：casual 座谈 + 大众启发。

01
中文

核心机制：想找一起运动的搭子，最简单的方法就是先迈开腿——愿意一起运动、热爱运动的人必然是"同频"的，不用说太多就知道彼此在想什么

EN

The mechanism: if you want a workout partner, the simplest way is to start moving first — people willing to exercise together and who love it are bound to be "on the same wavelength," and you know what each other is thinking without saying much.

表达提示workout partner → 运动搭子；on the same wavelength → 同频；start moving → 先迈开腿

02
中文

关键理解：运动不必一个人完成：先向伙伴发出邀约，共同完成一个目标，就会忘记身体上的酸痛，让训练不再是"坚持"而更像"一起玩"

EN

The key insight: exercise doesn't have to be done alone — reach out to a partner first, complete a goal together, and you'll forget the body's aches; training stops being "persistence" and becomes more like "playing together."

表达提示reach out to → 发出邀约；body aches → 身体酸痛；persistence → 坚持

03
中文

典型场景：夏日乐挑战用团队方式完成项目，让专业运动员也感到"很自在"；小叶小时候最快乐的事，就是下学后去游泳馆和小伙伴们先玩游戏再训练

EN

A typical scene: the summer fun challenge runs projects in a team format, which makes even professional athletes feel "at ease." And Xiao Ye's happiest childhood memory was going to the pool after school to play games with friends before training.

表达提示team format → 团队方式；at ease → 自在；childhood memory → 童年回忆

04
中文

相关概念：→ 邀约=降低自己启动成本的第一步

EN

Related concept: → an invitation is the first step to lowering your own startup cost.

表达提示invitation → 邀约；startup cost → 启动成本

05
中文

（引用）原文依据："你只要迈出了那一步，你的队友已经在跟你同频的路上了。"

EN

"Once you've taken that step, your teammates are already on the same wavelength as you, on the way."

表达提示take that step → 迈出那一步；on the way → 在路上

06
中文

核心机制：紧张源于你想变得更好、想达成预期——它本身是美好的期待，不必当成敌人

EN

The mechanism: nerves come from wanting to be better and meet expectations — it's a beautiful anticipation in itself, not something to treat as an enemy.

表达提示nerves → 紧张；meet expectations → 达成预期；beautiful anticipation → 美好的期待

  1. 想说「先迈开腿，同频的人自然会来」

    第一句口语，第二句更有画面

  2. 想说「邀约=降低启动成本」

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ We played jokes and made the water very lively.
✓ They stirred up some fun in the water — jokes and pranks everywhere.

make the water lively 是直译；stir up some fun / fireworks 是地道表达"制造欢乐气氛"。

✕ He is a person who always says come on yeah.
✓ He's always the one throwing out that "come on, yeah."

a person who always says 太啰嗦；the one throwing out 更口语紧凑。

✕ When I was nervous, my whole body was trembling.
✓ She was shaking with nerves before the race.

trembling 常用于寒冷/疾病；比赛前紧张用 shake with nerves 更地道。

✕ Recovery of psychology is more important than resting.
✓ Psychological recovery matters more than physical rest.

recovery of psychology 是中式名词堆叠；用形容词 psychological recovery 作主语更地道。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

一、TPU挑战GPU：架构根本差异

TPU vs GPU: The Fundamental Architectural Difference

情境用厨房比喻解释 GPU 与 TPU 的架构差异，以及 TPU 高利用率的原因。语域：casual 技术科普 + formal 分析。

01
中文

核心机制：GPU源于游戏显卡，SIMT多线程架构——像厨房里安排很多大厨，每人独立从冰箱取料、做菜、上菜，并行算力极强。TPU是面向矩阵计算的定制加速器，像流水线接力：第一人取菜、第二人加工、第三人装盘，中间调度极少，设备利用率更高

EN

The core mechanism: GPUs come from gaming graphics cards and use a SIMT multi-thread architecture — like a kitchen full of independent chefs, each grabbing ingredients, cooking, and plating on their own, with massively parallel compute. The TPU is a custom accelerator built for matrix computation, like an assembly-line relay: the first person grabs ingredients, the second processes, the third plates, with minimal scheduling in between and higher device utilization.

表达提示SIMT（Single Instruction, Multiple Threads）→ 单指令多线程；parallel compute → 并行算力；device utilization → 设备利用率

02
中文

关键理解：现代预训练从compute bound转向memory bound，GPU逐粒计算时会有idle period等待数据搬运；TPU通过软硬件协同保证满功率运行，要么复用缓存数据，要么跑满内存带宽

EN

The key insight: modern pretraining has shifted from compute-bound to memory-bound. GPUs idle while waiting for data movement when computing grain by grain; TPUs use software-hardware co-design to stay at full power — either reusing cached data or saturating memory bandwidth.

表达提示compute-bound / memory-bound → 算力受限/带宽受限；idle period → 空闲期；saturate memory bandwidth → 跑满内存带宽

03
中文

典型场景：大规模Transformer预训练、Gemini级自有模型定制训练

A typical scene: large-scale Transformer pretraining and Gemini-class custom model training.

表达提示pretraining → 预训练；custom training → 定制训练；Gemini-class → 双子星级别

When deeply customized for Google's own Gemini or frontier models from big labs, once all assumptions are fixed, the TPU beats the GPU in training efficiency and total cost of ownership. The XLA Black Box and the Ecosystem War

01
中文

核心机制：XLA是静态编译器，当Workload已知时可在全局TPU Pod做系统级优化：算子融合、中间态不反复存取内存、按硬件架构定制内存分配。上层支持PyTorch/JAX/TensorFlow，翻译成TPU汇编指令

EN

The core mechanism: XLA is a static compiler. When the workload is known, it can do system-level optimization across the global TPU pod — operator fusion, avoiding repeated memory round-trips for intermediate states, and customizing memory allocation for the hardware architecture. The upper layers support PyTorch, JAX, and TensorFlow, all translated into TPU assembly instructions.

表达提示static compiler → 静态编译器；operator fusion → 算子融合；TPU pod → 全局TPU集群

The key insight: compared with CUDA's open ecosystem, XLA is a black box — it can squeeze out performance, but developers can't easily control it; debugging requires understanding both hardware and software, and external customers can hardly fix bugs independently.

表达提示black box → 黑盒；squeeze out performance → 榨干性能；fix bugs independently → 独立修bug

四、大模型时代：MoE优化与方向押注

The LLM Era: MoE Optimization and Direction Bets

情境TPU 如何通过 3D 拓扑解决 MoE 痛点，以及激进定制的风险。语域：formal 芯片技术 + 战略判断。

01
中文

早期MoE在TPU上跑不好，因为2D拓扑下芯片只能与邻居通信，routing到不同专家需要绕很多环节。V4推出3D Torus+OCI光交换机，软件可编程配置通信路径，一次性解决MoE all-to-all痛点，效率明显提升

EN

Early MoE didn't run well on TPUs, because in a 2D topology a chip can only talk to its neighbors, and routing to different experts required many detours. The V4 introduced a 3D Torus with an OCI optical switch, letting software program the communication paths, solving the MoE all-to-all pain point in one shot with a clear efficiency gain.

表达提示MoE（Mixture of Experts）→ 专家混合模型；3D Torus → 三维环面拓扑；all-to-all → 全互联

02

五、供应链命门：博通与数据中心成本

The Supply-Chain Lifeline: Broadcom and Datacenter Costs

情境博通在 TPU 供应链中的角色、GPU 与 TPU 的数据中心成本结构差异。语域：formal 供应链 + 成本分析。

01
中文

核心机制：TPU团队设计芯片前端（图纸），Broadcom负责ICI芯片互联、后端信号布局、混合信号（模拟+数字）——这是技术壁垒极高的「脏活」。最终交给TSMC量产

EN

The core mechanism: the TPU team designs the chip's front end — the schematics; Broadcom handles the ICI interconnect, back-end signal layout, and mixed-signal work combining analog and digital — extremely high-barrier 'dirty work.' Everything goes to TSMC for mass production.

表达提示ICI（Inter-Chip Interconnect）→ 芯片间互联；mixed-signal → 混合信号；dirty work → 脏活（高壁垒苦功）

02
中文

关键理解：Broadcom像2B生意锁定最大客户，帮争取CoWoS/TSMC产能；但溢价权越来越大，TPU margin被挤压。没有backup方案（类似英伟达对台积电的议价）成本难控

EN

The key insight: Broadcom runs like a 2B business locked to its biggest customer, helping secure CoWoS and TSMC capacity; but its pricing power keeps growing, squeezing the TPU's margin. Without a backup plan — like Nvidia's bargaining position with TSMC — costs are hard to control.

表达提示pricing power → 溢价权；margin → 利润空间；bargaining position → 议价地位

03
中文

典型场景：GPU集群需大量NVSwitch交换机（贵）；TPU用桶线芯片直连+少量光交换，数据中心建设成本已拉开差距

EN

A typical scene: GPU clusters need a lot of expensive NVSwitch switches; the TPU uses barrel chips with direct connection plus a small amount of optical switching, and the datacenter build-out cost gap has already widened.

表达提示NVSwitch → 英伟达交换芯片；optical switching → 光交换；build-out cost → 建设成本

04
中文

表达提示liquid cooling → 液冷；SerDes → 串行解串器；core link → 核心环节

六、TPU十年进化史与Groq新星

A Decade of TPU Evolution and the Groq Rising Star

情境TPU 从 V1 到 V7 的进化脉络与 Groq 的差异化路线。语域：formal 技术史 + casual 故事。

01
中文

V1：仅Inference芯片，针对搜索推荐CNN加速（Jeff Dean、David Patterson参与）。V2：旗舰训练芯片，AlphaGo/AlphaFold/早期Transformer均用之。V4：加入Sparse Core服务推荐算法。V5/V6：进入大模型时代，V6分训练/推理两版本，押注Transformer预训练。V7 Ironwood：Henry参与，Gemini实现100% TPU训练

EN

The V1 was an inference-only chip targeting CNN acceleration for search and recommendations, with Jeff Dean and David Patterson involved. The V2 became the flagship training chip, used for AlphaGo, AlphaFold, and early Transformers. The V4 added Sparse Cores for recommendation algorithms. The V5 and V6 entered the large-model era; the V6 split into training and inference versions, betting on Transformer pretraining. The V7 Ironwood, which Henry worked on, achieved 100% TPU training for Gemini.

表达提示flagship training chip → 旗舰训练芯片；CNN（Convolutional Neural Network）→ 卷积神经网络；Jeff Dean → 杰夫·迪恩

02
中文

（引用）起源故事：2013年Jeff Dean演示深度学习突破后，Jonathan Ross（现Groq联合创始人、英伟达首席软件架构师）放两页PPT——「GPU能工作了（好消息）」「我们付不起这钱（坏消息）」，若用户发3分钟语音数据中心成本翻倍，由此催生TPU自研

EN

The origin story: in 2013, after Jeff Dean demonstrated a deep learning breakthrough, Jonathan Ross — now a Groq co-founder and Nvidia chief software architect — put up two slides: 'GPUs work, the good news,' and 'we can't afford this, the bad news.' If a user sent a three-minute voice message, datacenter costs would double — and that sparked the TPU self-research effort.

表达提示the good news / the bad news → 好消息/坏消息；spark → 催生；datacenter cost → 数据中心成本

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ GPU is like a kitchen with many cooks, TPU is like water flowing in pipes.
✓ GPUs are parallel chefs working independently; TPUs are an assembly-line relay with minimal scheduling.

流水线比喻要说 assembly-line relay；water flowing 不准确，没有表达接力与调度。

✕ TPU is faster than GPU in everything.
✓ TPU beats GPU on TCO only under specific conditions — large-scale, stable models, shared costs.

绝对化表达不对；要强调限定条件 limited conditions。

✕ XLA is very difficult to use for outside developers.
✓ XLA is a black box — outsiders can hardly debug it and must rely on Google engineers.

difficult to use 太委婉；用 black box + can hardly debug 才说透痛点。

✕ Groq is a chip company like Nvidia.
✓ Groq is essentially a compiler company — the LPU's every cycle is determined by its compiler.

Groq 本质是编译器公司；把它当芯片公司会误解其核心竞争力。

The fix: a three-stage verification. First, wait for authoritative teardown reports to inspect the chassis materials. Second, check complaint rankings to see if there's a cluster of complaints. Third, six months to a year later, search social platforms for 'Huajing S complaints, quality, gripes' and look for common issues — it only counts as confirmed when a dozen posts all mention the same thing, like a noisy window regulator. Wait until monthly sales steady past ten thousand and cumulative deliveries reach 80,000 to 100,000, and only then is the owner sample real enough.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The car is a three-fold discount AITO M9.
✓ It's the AITO M9 at a third of the price.

「三折」是 30%，即三分之一价格；three-fold 意思是「三倍」，方向正好反了，是数字相关的经典错误。

✕ The sales are raised by the factory capability.
✓ Sales follow production capacity as the factory ramps up.

「销量跟着产能走」不是被动语态；用 sales follow production / sales are tied to output 更自然。

✕ I will pinch my thigh if I buy the mid trim.
✓ I'll be kicking myself for not getting the high trim.

「掐大腿」是中文意象；英文对应是 kick myself 或 regret it badly，不能逐字翻译。

✕ The car uses no-name battery.
✓ The battery isn't a no-name brand — it's just weaker in brand power than CATL.

no-name 指完全没有名气的杂牌；瑞浦赛克有合资背景，更准确的表达是 lesser-known / weaker in brand power。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为月销量低 = 车不行 → 要看上市时间和产能爬坡曲线，销量被产能卡住不等于需求差

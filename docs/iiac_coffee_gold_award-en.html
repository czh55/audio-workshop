下半段：IIAC 金奖咖啡豆深度拆解

Part 2: The IIAC Gold-Award Coffee Bean, Explained

情境The hosts unpack what the IIAC gold award actually means. 语域：casual 科普拆解 + 消费提醒。

01
中文

核心机制：IIAC（International Institute of Coffee Tasters，国际咖啡品鉴协会）1993 年成立于意大利，非营利，靠会员费运营。其主办的 ICT（International Coffee Tasting）自 2006 年举办，采用匿名盲测打分

EN

The mechanism: the IIAC — the International Institute of Coffee Tasters — was founded in Italy in 1993, is non-profit, and runs on membership fees. Its event, the ICT, or International Coffee Tasting, has run since 2006 and scores coffees in anonymous blind tastings.

表达提示blind tasting → 盲测；membership fees → 会员费；non-profit → 非营利

02
中文

关键理解：金奖需同时满足两个条件：① 满分 100 分中得分 ≥ 85 分；② 排进当场品鉴会前 25%。前 25% 中的前 20% 可角逐铂金奖。只设金/铂两档，无银铜奖

EN

The insight: the gold award needs two conditions at once — a score of 85 or higher out of 100, and a finish in the top 25% of that session's tasting. The top 20% of that top 25% can compete for platinum. There are only gold and platinum tiers; no silver or bronze.

表达提示top 25% → 前25%；gold and platinum tiers → 金/铂两档；meet two conditions → 满足两个条件

03
中文

典型场景：品牌提交报名信息 → 每款产品约 2000 元参赛费 → 寄送样品。每年最多 10 款产品参赛，阶梯制报名费使多产品参赛更划算。评选范围含咖啡豆、胶囊、挂耳等

EN

Typical process: a brand submits registration, pays around 2,000 yuan per product, and ships samples. Up to ten products per brand per year, with tiered entry fees that make entering more products cheaper. The scope covers beans, capsules, drip bags, and more.

表达提示entry fee → 参赛费；tiered fees → 阶梯制收费；drip bags → 挂耳咖啡

04
中文

（引用）原文依据：「任何品牌只要缴纳了报名费、寄送样品就能够参赛」——这是金奖遍地开花的制度根源

EN

As the show says: 'Any brand that pays the entry fee and ships samples can compete' — that's the institutional root of gold awards blooming everywhere.

表达提示institutional root → 制度根源；bloom everywhere → 遍地开花；pay the entry fee → 缴纳报名费

05
中文

原因：「金奖」二字在中文语境自带稀缺感，但 ICT 不设淘汰赛、不设参赛门槛，85 分 + 前 25% 的门槛在品鉴会规模扩大后并不严苛。2024 年 8 月起国内累计举办 10 多场，单场 40 多款获奖

EN

Why: the word 'gold' carries built-in rarity in Chinese, but the ICT has no knockout rounds and no entry barrier, and the 85-point plus top-25% bar isn't strict once tasting sessions grow large. Since August 2024, more than ten sessions have been held in China, with over 40 entries winning at a single event.

表达提示built-in rarity → 自带稀缺感；knockout rounds → 淘汰赛；entry barrier → 参赛门槛

06
中文

解法：购买咖啡时区分「认证达标」与「赛事冠军」——前者代表「还不错」，后者才代表「万里挑一」

EN

The fix: when buying coffee, tell 'certification pass' apart from 'competition champion' — the first means 'pretty good,' the second means 'one in ten thousand.'

表达提示certification pass → 认证达标；one in ten thousand → 万里挑一；tell A apart from B → 区分A和B

Paraphrase & Chunks 3 组表达

  1. 想说「金奖≠第一名」

    passed the bar · certification, not a championship
    对比句适合揭穿营销

  2. 想说「门槛很低」

    entry bar · can enter
    entry bar 指门槛

  3. 想说「遍地开花」

    blooming on every shelf · ubiquitous
    ubiquitous 偏书面

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

用「blind tasting」说：ICT采用匿名盲测打分。

The ICT scores every coffee in anonymous blind tastings.

用「one in ten thousand」说：赛事冠军才是万里挑一。

A competition champion means one in ten thousand — a gold label just means 'pretty good.'

用「tell A apart from B」说：要区分认证达标和赛事冠军。

Buyers need to tell a certification pass apart from a competition champion.

用「hit its ceiling」说：中国家电市场触顶。

China's home-appliance market has hit its ceiling, forcing Samsung to retreat.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The coffee won the first place in IIAC.
✓ The coffee won an IIAC gold award — but that's a pass-line certification, not first place.

IIAC金奖不是「第一名」；用 pass-line certification / qualified 表达才准确。

✕ Gold award is very rare in China.
✓ Gold labels are blooming everywhere in China, precisely because the entry bar is low.

事实与直觉相反——正因为门槛低才遍地开花；要纠正「金奖=稀缺」的错误认知。

✕ The coffee bean is very delicious, so it won the award.
✓ The brand paid an entry fee and scored above 85 in a blind tasting.

「好喝才获奖」的因果是错的；获奖流程是报名+寄样+盲测打分，与主观口味无关。

✕ Samsung stopped selling TV in China.
✓ Samsung stopped selling TVs and other home appliances in mainland China — but it's keeping phones.

「停止销售」用 stop selling；且要带上「保留手机业务」的对比，事实才完整。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为「IIAC金奖」=万里挑一的好豆 → 金奖只是「85分+前25%」的合格线认证，参赛即有机会
以为金奖豆一定比普通豆好喝 → 获奖是流程+分数的结果，与你的口味偏好没有直接关系
以为大品牌摘金是实力象征 → 低门槛+低成本营销+示范效应，才是金奖遍地开花的真实原因

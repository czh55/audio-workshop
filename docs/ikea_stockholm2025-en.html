避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Nobody buys a rug for using.
✓ Nobody buys a rug to use it — it's about atmosphere.

for using 是中式表达；用 to use it 才自然。

✕ Nordic design is more human smell.
✓ Nordic design is Bauhaus with a human touch.

human smell 是直译错误；human touch（人情味）才是地道说法。

✕ The color ratio is more important than colors.
✓ The ratio matters more than the specific colors.

more important than colors 可理解但生硬；ratio matters 更简洁有力。

✕ Designers fight with the client.
✓ Designers 'clash' with clients — really brainstorming and spark collisions.

fight 带敌意；设计语境用 clash/pushback 或 brainstorm 更准确。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为地毯不实用就没价值 → 地毯是客厅氛围的稳定器——触感会以偏概全影响整个家的认知
以为配色靠眼睛喜欢 → 自然系配色靠大脑联想——温度湿度而非颜色本身
以为复古就是买旧单品 → 复古是设计回到美好年代——宜家用数据考古捕捉30年循环信号

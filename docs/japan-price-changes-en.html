一、汇率与身份：钱的"度量衡"变了

Exchange Rate & Identity: The "Measuring Stick" for Money Has Changed

情境汇率从 6.7 跌到 4 点几，直接改写以人民币计价的"身价表"；还聊到留学与毕业后的身份税制差异。语域：casual 亲历分享 + 数字对比。

01
中文

核心机制：初到日本留学时汇率约6出头（高至6.7），买什么都自动换算成人民币；如今汇率跌到4点几，甚至一度逼近3字头。同样是25万日元月薪，按6.7算是1.5万人民币，按4点几只有1万出头

EN

When the hosts first arrived in Japan as students, the exchange rate was around 6 — even up to 6.7 — so they automatically converted every purchase into yuan. Today it's fallen to the low 4s, and briefly approached the 3s. A monthly salary of 250,000 yen was about 15,000 yuan at 6.7, but barely more than 10,000 yuan at 4-point-something.

表达提示exchange rate → 汇率；convert into yuan → 换算成人民币；the low 4s → 4点几

02

The exchange rate isn't an abstract macro number — it directly rewrites the "value chart" priced in yuan. You did nothing, and your salary automatically shrank 30%. For people who earn and spend yen, daily life is unaffected, but the moment you go back home or exchange currency, the gap becomes visible.

表达提示priced in yuan → 以人民币计价；shrink → 缩水；the gap becomes visible → 落差立刻显形

01
中文

核心机制：5公斤大米从约2000日元涨到4000多日元，超市货架搬空、一个家庭限购一袋。政府放储备米应对，农协（JA）却极力反对——农协不只是农产品组织，还有银行、医院、保险，是垄断级存在

EN

A five-kilogram bag of rice went from about 2,000 yen to over 4,000 yen. Supermarket shelves got cleaned out, and each household was limited to one bag. The government released reserve rice, but the agricultural cooperative, JA, fiercely opposed it — JA isn't just a farm organization; it also runs banks, hospitals, and insurance, making it a monopoly-level player.

表达提示reserve rice → 储备米；agricultural cooperative → 农业合作社（农协）；monopoly → 垄断；clean out the shelves → 搬空货架

表达提示supply and demand → 供需；monopoly pricing → 垄断定价；import substitution → 进口替代；be locked out → 被挡在外面

After the bubble burst, prices stayed flat for over thirty years, but in the last three to five years they've risen about 100% — the average price of a new Tokyo home has broken past 100 million yen. The weak yen has pulled in global property speculators, and new units sell out almost instantly.

01
中文

核心机制：日元贬值对"赚外币花日元"的游客/代购是折扣（省30-40%），对"赚日元花日元"的居民没有实际好处；物价上涨对游客短期无感，对居民是每月账单。汇率+通胀叠加，在日外国人和日本本地人一起被挤压

EN

Yen depreciation is a discount for tourists and daigou who earn foreign currency and spend yen — saving 30% to 40% — but for residents earning and spending yen it brings no real benefit. Price rises feel invisible to tourists in the short run, but for residents they show up in every monthly bill. Exchange rate plus inflation stack up, squeezing both foreigners and locals in Japan.

表达提示depreciation → 贬值；discount → 折扣；stack up → 叠加；squeeze → 挤压

02
中文

（引用）"现在的东京就有一种特别荒诞的撕裂感：对于游客来说，日本的东西好便宜呀；对于咱们生活在日本的外国人来说，生活成本和压力都在上升。"

EN

"Tokyo today has this surreal rift: for tourists, things in Japan are so cheap! But for us foreigners living here, the cost of living and the pressure are both rising."

表达提示surreal rift → 荒诞的撕裂感；cost of living → 生活成本

03
中文

（引用）相关概念：日本人也在用脚投票——国内游转向冲绳、九州、北海道等低消费地，替代出境游

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

The exchange rate fell from 6.7 to the low 4s — my salary automatically lost 30%.

JA isn't just a farm group — it operates like a monopoly that controls pricing.

Once the price passes your anchor point, it stops feeling worth it.

Japanese people are voting with their feet, turning to lower-cost destinations.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The price of rice rose very fast, my heart feels broken.
✓ The price of rice doubled — and shoppers felt the sting immediately.

中式口语硬译情绪；英文里价格暴涨用 soared / doubled，配合 felt the sting 表达感受更地道。

✕ JA is a monopolistic organization, very strong.
✓ JA operates like a monopoly — it runs banks, hospitals, and insurance too.

monopolistic organization 太干瘪；用 operate like a monopoly 并补具体证据，才有说服力。

✕ The exchange rate is very low, I am very sad.
✓ With the exchange rate in the low 4s, my salary is worth a third less in yuan.

直译情绪词很空；把情绪转成事实对比（worth a third less）才地道。

✕ I want to buy clothes but the price is too expensive.
✓ The price has passed my anchor point, so it no longer feels worth it.

too expensive 是中式直译；用价格锚点（anchor point / not worth it）表达消费心理判断更地道。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

汇率是新闻里的宏观数字，跟自己没关系 → 汇率直接改写你的身价表——没做什么，工资就自动缩水30%
物价涨就是供需失衡 → 要看到垄断定价、储备受阻、进口关税三重结构，涨价背后也有"制度"

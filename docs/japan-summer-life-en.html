Slowly Living Out Our Own Summer in Japan

Two Chinese long-term residents of Tokyo, Lao Fan and Shirley, start from "midsummer day" heat and unfold the differences between how China and Japan get through summer — watermelon freedom versus air-conditioning freedom, indoor cooling systems, summer food, cicada song, wind chimes, and fireworks festivals. They land on a shared conclusion: Japan's seemingly superfluous rituals turn summer from a piece of weather into a rhythm of life with colors, tastes, and sounds — and turn them from merely living into actually starting to live.

一、高温之下：西瓜自由与空调自由

Under the Heat: Watermelon Freedom and Air-Con Freedom

情境酷暑日、骤热、梅雨结束后气温跳变，以及"失去西瓜自由，获得空调自由"的金句。语域：casual 生活闲聊 + 体感对比。

01
中文

核心机制：日本把30度以上的天气命名为"酷暑日"。今年梅雨季特别长，7月初还只有23-24度，梅雨一过直接跳到35-36度——中间的过渡气温"被吃掉了"，人们几乎来不及适应

EN

Japan officially names days above 30 degrees "midsummer days." This year the rainy season dragged on, so early July was still 23 to 24 degrees — but the moment the rain ended, it jumped to 35 to 36. The transitional temperatures in between "got eaten up," and people barely had time to adapt.

表达提示midsummer day → 酷暑日；rainy season → 梅雨季；transitional temperatures → 过渡气温

02
中文

关键理解：岛国四季分明，真正的夏天只有7月初到8月底（比国内"晚一个月"，也短得多）；骤热比持续高温更难防——7月全国半个月就有1万多人因中暑被救护车拉走

EN

The key insight: on this island nation, real summer only runs from early July to late August — about a month later than in China, and much shorter. Sudden heat is harder to guard against than a long hot stretch — nationwide, more than 10,000 people were taken to hospital by ambulance for heatstroke in half a month in July.

表达提示island nation → 岛国；sudden heat → 骤热；heatstroke → 中暑；guard against → 防范

03
中文

典型场景：7月初晚上还得盖被子睡觉，中旬开始不吹空调就活不下去；同一屋檐下，天津的湿热像"蒸笼"，日本的干热则是另一种体感

EN

A typical scene: in early July you still sleep under a blanket; by mid-month you can't survive without air conditioning. Under the same roof, Tianjin's humid heat feels like a steamer, while Japan's dry heat is a completely different sensation.

表达提示humid heat → 湿热；like a steamer → 像蒸笼；dry heat → 干热

04
中文

EN

"This year, early July was 23, 24 degrees; then mid-month, as soon as the rainy season ended, it went from 23, 24 to 35, 36 — it feels like the temperatures in between got eaten up."

表达提示as soon as → 一…就…；get eaten up → 被吃掉

05
中文

（引用）相关概念：酷暑日 → 骤热预警 → 中暑风险管理（气象与健康的连接点）

Related concept: midsummer day → sudden heat warnings → heatstroke risk management — the link where weather meets health.

表达提示heat warning → 高温预警；risk management → 风险管理；weather meets health → 气象与健康的交汇

中文

The gem: "You may have lost watermelon freedom, but you gained air-conditioning freedom." Two Chinese in Japan traded ludicrously expensive watermelons for the freedom to run the AC without being nagged.

S2

二、室内避暑系统：空调 · 风扇 · 遮阳

Indoor Cooling Systems: AC, Fans, and Shade

情境除湿模式的妙用、一直开反而省电、木造房保温差与"结界"现象。语域：casual 生活经验 + 家电使用技巧。

01
中文

核心机制：日本的空调除湿模式=降低湿度+轻微降温，湿度降下来后体感清爽，甚至比设定26度的制冷"还冷一点"，且不会像制冷那样忽冷忽热

EN

Japan's dehumidify mode lowers humidity while gently cooling — once the humidity drops, the body feels fresh, sometimes even "colder" than cooling mode set at 26 degrees, and without the alternating hot-cold swings.

表达提示dehumidify mode → 除湿模式；cooling mode → 制冷模式；hot-cold swings → 忽冷忽热

02
中文

关键理解：老范坦言"来日本之后才学会用除湿"——因为在国内没有空调自由，根本不知道这个功能是干嘛的；而制冷模式恒温差（设26度有时越吹越热，吹着吹着就变成了"制热"），除湿反而更稳定

EN

The key insight: Lao Fan admits he only learned to use dehumidify after coming to Japan — because back home, without air-conditioning freedom, he never knew what the function was for. Cooling mode is also unstable — set at 26 degrees, it sometimes blows hotter and hotter, turning into "heating" — while dehumidify stays steadier.

表达提示learn to use → 学会用；turn into heating → 变成制热；steady → 稳定

03
中文

典型场景：夏天家里上下两层空调一直开着除湿；老范以前的"老式用法"——开最低温最大风，吹冷了再关、热了再开——既费电又不舒服

EN

A typical scene: both floors of their home run dehumidify all summer. Lao Fan's old habit — cranking the AC to the lowest temperature and highest fan, then turning it off when cold and on again when hot — wasted power and felt terrible.

表达提示crank up → 开到最大；waste power → 费电

04
中文

（引用）原文依据："一直開除濕，我會覺得它要比這個製冷26度還要冷一點"；"我也是最近才學到的，一直開著空調相對來說是省電的。"

EN

"With dehumidify running constantly, I feel it's a bit colder than cooling mode at 26 degrees." "I only recently learned that keeping the AC running all the time is actually more power-efficient."

表达提示power-efficient → 省电；keep running → 一直开着

05
中文

（引用）相关概念：空调病观念 → 温度自由 → 除湿模式（观念的转变带来工具的效率跃升）

EN

Related concept: the air-conditioning-illness mindset → temperature freedom → dehumidify mode — a shift in mindset that brings a leap in how efficiently you use the tool.

表达提示mindset shift → 观念转变；leap in efficiency → 效率跃升

06
中文

核心机制：日本木造房保温隔热差，二楼白天可达32度；空调装在哪间屋，凉气就"困"在那间——即使开着门，门口也像有个"结界"，凉气只在屋内自己循环，不会流到隔壁

EN

The mechanism: Japanese wooden houses insulate poorly, so the second floor can hit 32 degrees by day. Wherever the AC is installed, the cool air stays "trapped" in that room — even with the door open, it's like there's a barrier at the doorway; the cool air circulates inside but never flows to the next room.

表达提示wooden house → 木造房；insulate → 保温；trapped → 被困住；barrier → 结界/屏障

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「一直开着反而省电」

    keep it on · more energy-efficient · on and off
    第二句更口语，blasting 很形象

  2. 想说「凉气只在屋里自己循环」

    cycles inside · stuck there
    第一句中性描述，第二句更口语
S3

三、夏天的味道：从凉面到鳗鱼

The Taste of Summer: From Cold Noodles to Eel

情境中日凉面的调味差异、土用丑日吃鳗鱼的习俗三层逻辑。语域：casual 美食闲聊 + 民俗讲解。

01
中文

关键理解：主食逻辑相通（都是凉面），但调味完全不同——日式酸甜口让习惯麻酱的北方人吃不惯；日本的"清爽"更多靠味道寡淡+口感冷冽来达成，冷荞麦是最典型的代表

EN

The staple logic is the same — both are cold noodles — but the seasoning is completely different. Japan's sweet-and-sour taste is hard for northerners used to sesame sauce; Japanese "refreshing" comes more from plain flavor plus icy texture, and cold soba is the most typical example.

表达提示seasoning → 调味；sweet-and-sour → 酸甜口；sesame sauce → 麻酱；cold soba → 冷荞麦

02
中文

（引用）相关概念：凉面=东亚共同解暑密码，风味差异来自"盐糖酸碱"的调味哲学不同

EN

Related concept: cold noodles are East Asia's shared secret for beating the heat, and the flavor differences come from different seasoning philosophies of salt, sugar, acid, and alkali.

表达提示beat the heat → 解暑；seasoning philosophy → 调味哲学

03
中文

核心机制：立春/立夏/立秋/立冬之前各有半个月叫"土用"（季节交替的过渡期），每12天循环一个"丑日"，两者相遇就是"土用丑日"

EN

The mechanism: before each season's start — beginning of spring, summer, autumn, winter — there's a two-week window called "doyo," a transition period; every 12 days cycles an "ox day," and when the two meet, it's the "doyo ushi day."

表达提示seasonal transition → 换季过渡；doyo → 土用（日本传统节气）；ox day → 丑日

04
中文

关键理解：习俗是三层逻辑叠出来的：①换季没胃口，吃点滋补的；②"丑"的发音同"う"，吃"う"开头的食物（鳗鱼うなぎ、乌冬うどん、梅子うめ）被认为吉利又滋补；③商业宣传让鳗鱼从所有"う"字食物里胜出，绑定成夏日国民习惯

EN

The key insight: the custom stacks three layers of logic. One, during seasonal change you lose your appetite, so eat something nourishing. Two, "ushi" sounds like "u," and eating foods starting with "u" — unagi eel, udon, ume plum — is considered lucky and nourishing. Three, commercial marketing made eel win among all the "u" foods and bind it into a national summer habit.

表达提示stack three layers → 三层逻辑叠出；nourishing → 滋补的；lose one's appetite → 没胃口；marketing → 商业宣传

05
中文

典型场景：今年8月7日立秋，7月26日左右就是土用丑日，鳗鱼店门前大排长龙；老范调侃这和国内"吃肝明目、以形补形"的逻辑一样充满"民间智慧"

EN

A typical scene: this year's beginning of autumn is August 7, so around July 26 is the doyo ushi day, and long lines stretch out the eel shops' doors. Lao Fan jokes it's the same logic as China's "eat liver for your eyes" — folk wisdom everywhere.

表达提示long lines / queue → 大排长龙；folk wisdom → 民间智慧；eat liver for your eyes → 吃肝明目

06
中文

（引用）原文依据："醜日它這個發音是う，所以日本人會認為這一天吃う開頭的食物，會比較的吉利，同時比較的滋補。"

EN

"The ox day is pronounced 'u,' so the Japanese believe eating food that starts with 'u' on that day is lucky and nourishing."

表达提示start with → 以…开头；lucky and nourishing → 吉利又滋补

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「习俗是三层逻辑叠出来的」

    a stack of logics · layers appetite and marketing
    第一句概括，第二句把三层点出来

  2. 想说「和吃肝明目一样是民间智慧」

    folk wisdom · old wives' tale energy
    old wives' tale 是英文里对应的民间俗信表达

四、风物诗：蝉鸣、风铃与清凉包装

Seasonal Scenes: Cicadas, Wind Chimes, and Cool Packaging

情境风铃的"假清凉"心理、蝉鸣的"对比哲学"、全感官降温。语域：casual 生活美学 + 感官观察。

01
中文

核心机制：风铃不会真的降温，但清脆的声响会让人从心理上感到一丝凉意——"听到铃的响，会给你带来一种假装清凉的体感"

EN

Wind chimes don't actually cool anything down, but the crisp sound makes you feel a touch cooler psychologically — "hearing the chime gives you a pretend-cool sensation."

表达提示wind chime → 风铃；crisp sound → 清脆的声响；a touch cooler → 一丝凉意

02
中文

关键理解：风铃常挂在神社、水池边和饰品店门口；它把"夏天吹过的风"变成一个清晰的声音意象。对老范来说，风铃一响，就是"夏天来了"的信号

EN

The key insight: wind chimes often hang at shrines, by ponds, and at accessory shop doors. They turn "the wind blowing through summer" into a clear sound image. For Lao Fan, the moment a chime rings, it's the signal that summer is here.

表达提示shrine → 神社；sound image → 声音意象；the signal that … → …的信号

03
中文

典型场景：逛饰品店看到风铃挂出来=夏天到了；团地朋友家阳台挂上风铃+楼下祭典的灯光音乐，就像日剧里走出来的场景

EN

A typical scene: walking into an accessory shop and seeing wind chimes out means summer has arrived; a friend's balcony in the public-housing complex with a chime hanging, and festival lights and music downstairs, looks like a scene straight out of a Japanese drama.

表达提示public-housing complex / danchi → 团地（公租房小区）；festival → 祭典；straight out of a drama → 像从日剧里走出来

04
中文

（引用）原文依据："它不會真的讓你感覺到涼爽，但是你聽到那個清脆的聲音，就會讓你精神上感覺到了一絲的涼意。"

EN

"It won't really make you feel cool, but when you hear that crisp sound, you feel a trace of coolness in your spirit."

表达提示a trace of → 一丝；in your spirit → 精神上

05
中文

（引用）相关概念：听觉降温 → 视觉降温 → 心理降温（三位一体的"全感官降温"）

EN

Related concept: hearing-based cooling → visual cooling → psychological cooling — a trinity of all-senses cooling.

表达提示trinity → 三位一体；all-senses cooling → 全感官降温

06
中文

核心机制：蝉鸣是"室外温度高"的信号——听到蝉叫得越欢，越凸显屋内空调下的凉快。老范总结得十分"哲学"："对方越惨，你觉得自己越幸福；外边越热，你觉得自己越凉。"

EN

The mechanism: cicada song is a signal that it's hot outside — the more they sing, the more you appreciate the coolness of your air-conditioned room. Lao Fan sums it up almost philosophically: "The worse off they are, the happier you feel; the hotter it is outside, the cooler you feel."

表达提示cicada → 蝉；sum up philosophically → 总结得很有哲理；appreciate → 体会/感激

  1. 想说「假装的清凉」

    fake coolness · psychological chill
    第一句口语直白，第二句带点总结感

  2. 想说「外面越热，屋里越凉」

    第二句有画面感，适合俏皮总结
S5

五、花火大会与夏日祭：从游客到生活

Fireworks and Summer Festivals: From Tourist to Resident

情境免费场人挤人 vs 买票场/郊外场体验质变，社区祭典的参与感。语域：casual 亲历踩坑 + 攻略。

01
中文

核心机制：花火体验的差距不在规模，而在"打开方式"：免费场=人挤人+视野被挡；买票场=视野开阔、体验质变；郊外田野场=像包场一样自由

EN

The gap in fireworks experience isn't about scale — it's about how you "open" it: free venues mean crowds and blocked views; ticketed venues give you open views and a transformed experience; and rural field venues feel like your own private show.

表达提示free venue → 免费场；blocked view → 视野被挡；private show → 包场

02
中文

关键理解：老范第一次自己去看花火没买票，体验极差"我为什么要来"；去了隅田川（号称100万人）更是"来日本10年最糟糕的一次花火大会"。相反，茨城、富士山、长冈（日本规模第一，能放两小时）这类在田间地头的场次，视野开阔、交通不便反而挡住了人流

EN

The key insight: the first time Lao Fan watched fireworks on his own, he didn't buy a ticket and it was terrible — "why did I even come?"; the Sumida River show, supposedly a million people, was his worst fireworks night in ten years in Japan. Meanwhile, field shows in Ibaraki, at the foot of Mount Fuji, and in Nagaoka — the nation's largest, running two hours — have open views, and inconvenient transport actually keeps the crowds away.

表达提示the nation's largest → 全国规模第一；open view → 视野开阔；keep the crowds away → 挡住人流

03
中文

典型场景：搬着椅子坐在田间地头免费看，前面全是空地没人挡；长冈花火要开2小时（一般场1.5小时，短的半小时），节目上线的当天他们就身在长冈附近

EN

A typical scene: sitting on a chair in the middle of a field, watching for free with nothing blocking your view; Nagaoka runs two hours — most shows run 1.5 hours, short ones half an hour — and they happened to be nearby when it went on air.

表达提示nothing blocking your view → 没人挡视线；happen to be nearby → 恰好在附近

04
中文

（引用）踩坑实录："人太多樓太多啥也看不見，免費的地方前面肯定有很多遮擋"；"最後中暑了，在肉體的體驗上是非常遭罪的。"

EN

The pitfall diary: "Too many people, too many buildings, you can't see anything — in free spots there's always something blocking the view"; "I ended up with heatstroke, it was physically miserable."

表达提示pitfall diary → 踩坑实录；heatstroke → 中暑；physically miserable → 身体上遭罪

05
中文

（引用）策略：一年只挑1-2个精品场，买票或选郊外，把花火当成"给夏天一个交代"，而不是打卡数量

EN

The strategy: pick only one or two premium shows a year, buy tickets or choose the countryside, and treat fireworks as "a proper send-off for summer" rather than a checkpoint-counting game.

表达提示premium show → 精品场；a proper send-off for summer → 给夏天一个交代；checkpoint-counting → 打卡数量

06
中文

核心机制：社区祭典和公共祭典体验完全不同——社区祭典（在"团地"公租房小区里办的）街坊邻里互相认识，居民就是组织者、委员会成员，参与者有真正的"参与感"；公共祭典里你只是个外来观众

EN

The mechanism: community festivals and public festivals feel completely different. Community festivals — held in the public-housing complex — have neighbors who know each other, residents who are the organizers and committee members, and real participation. At a public festival, you're just an outside spectator.

表达提示community festival → 社区祭典；organizer → 组织者；spectator → 观众；participation → 参与感

  1. 想说「免费场人多什么都看不见」

    第一句更口语抱怨，第二句中性描述

  2. 想说「把花火当给夏天的交代」

    第二句是攻略式金句

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Today is very hot, I want to blow air-conditioner.
✓ It's scorching today — I need to run the air conditioning.

blow air-conditioner 是中式直译；英文用 run / turn on the AC，或用 blast the AC 表示开到最大。

✕ The cool wind can't go to other rooms.
✓ The cool air stays trapped in that room — it never reaches the next one.

cool wind 是中式搭配，空调凉气用 cool air；go 换成 stays trapped / never reaches 更准确。

✕ Eel shop has a very long dragon line.
✓ There's a huge queue stretching out the eel shop's door.

dragon line 是直译"长龙"；英文排队用 queue / line，stretch out the door 描述队伍长度。

✕ The free fireworks place people too many.
✓ The free venue was packed — I couldn't see a thing.

中式语序 people too many 不成立；用 packed / crowded 表示人山人海。

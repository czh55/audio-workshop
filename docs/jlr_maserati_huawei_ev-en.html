Part 1: Maserati's Predicament and Huawei's Lifeline Deal

情境讲奇瑞捷豹路虎全面停产燃油车、发布Freelander电动品牌及销量雪崩数据。语域：formal 汽车产业分析。

01
中文

核心机制：2026年3月31日，最后一台燃油路虎揽胜极光在常熟基地下线；同时发布全新电动品牌 Freelander（神行者）。奇瑞捷豹路虎成为首个全面关停燃油车的合资品牌

EN

The core mechanism: on March 31, 2026, the last gasoline Range Rover Evoque rolled off the line at the Changshu base, while the company launched a new all-electric brand, Freelander. Chery Jaguar Land Rover becomes the first joint-venture brand to fully stop gasoline production.

表达提示reach shore or jump ship → 上岸或跳船；sink money into → 砸钱进；extended-range → 增程；plug-in hybrid → 插混

Part 3：合作新模式与中国汽车产业崛起

Part 3: A New Cooperation Model and the Rise of China's Auto Industry

情境总结新能源时代合资话语权转移，「用品牌换生存」的破局逻辑。语域：formal 产业评论。

01
中文

核心机制：新能源时代合资公司内中方话语权显著增强。外方最多主导设计外观，技术、运营、投资由国内车企主导。未来海外品牌在华新能源或仅「换标」

EN

The core mechanism: in the new-energy era, the Chinese side's voice inside joint ventures has grown significantly. The foreign side at most leads design; technology, operations, and investment are led by domestic automakers. In the future, foreign brands' new-energy cars in China may simply be 'rebadged'.

表达提示voice → 话语权；at most → 最多；rebadge → 换标；domestic automakers → 国内车企

02

EN

The key insight: this is a landmark moment for Chinese manufacturing going global — the inner soul of European luxury brands is being swapped for a Chinese soul.

表达提示landmark moment → 标志性事件；inner soul → 内心灵魂；swap A for B → 把A换成B

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

用「one car, two badges」说：一车两卖。

It's one car, two badges — Zunjie at home, Maserati abroad.

用「trade A for B」说：用品牌换生存。

They trade their brand names for survival in the Chinese market.

用「all-time low」说：销量史上最低。

The brand hit an all-time low last year, selling just over 20,000 units.

用「go all in」说：边缘品牌必须梭哈。

Marginal brands have no choice but to go all in on new energy.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Maserati sells the same car with two labels.
✓ It's one car, two badges — sold under different badges at home and abroad.

label 偏「标签」；汽车行业挂的标用 badge 更地道，且补上 at home and abroad 信息才完整。

✕ The sales fell to the lowest point in history.
✓ Sales hit an all-time low of just over 20,000 units.

lowest point in history 语法没错但冗长；hit an all-time low 是固定搭配，更简洁地道。

✕ The Chinese side controls the technology and the foreign side controls the design.
✓ The Chinese side leads technology and operations, while the foreign side leads design and appearance.

lead 比 control 更贴合「主导」的行业语境，control 太强且容易误解为管控。

✕ Mercedes and BMW still have oil car bonus to eat.
✓ Mercedes and BMW still have gasoline dividends to harvest.

oil car / bonus to eat 是中式直译；dividends to harvest 才是商业评论的地道说法。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为合资车里外方掌握核心技术、中方只是代工 → 新能源时代中方主导技术运营，外方只剩设计外观的话语权
以为百年品牌永远有品牌溢价护体 → 跟不上时代+新能源颗粒无收，百年品牌照样跌下神坛
以为国外豪华品牌来华是「降维输出」 → 现在是中方技术+外方车标，换标合作才是生存之道

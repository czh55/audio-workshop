005. What the Designer-Toy Elders Taught Labubu: Cuteness Is Sugar Coating, Not a Rice Bowl

Huisi and Jackie of the 'Yuguang Post' podcast skip the question of why Labubu got hot. Instead, they hold it up against Pokemon, Hello Kitty, the Bearbrick, and LinaBell to map the four long-term paths of designer-toy IP: a story universe, all-channel licensing, fashion scarcity, and persona-driven experience. They then point out the fundamental challenge for Pop Mart, a data-driven channel company trying to become a content company: cuteness is the entry point — the rice bowl comes from upgrading.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Labubu's price is very high now.
✓ The resale price spiked at 3,699 yuan, then collapsed below 2,000 after the restock.

very high 太笼统；用 spike/collapse 加上具体数字才符合本期的涨跌叙事。

✕ Hello Kitty has no mouth so she is very cute.
✓ Hello Kitty's mouthless design keeps her politically neutral — she can't smile, cry, or take sides.

把无嘴理解为「可爱」丢了关键信息；重点是政治中立 all-purpose neutrality。

✕ Buying a blind box is a good investment.
✓ At 99 yuan it's everyday spending; treating it as an investment follows Bearbrick logic — be careful.

investment 是危险话术；要区分日常消费与投机，点明风险才对。

✕ Pop Mart wants to be Disney.
✓ Pop Mart is benchmarking China Literature, not Disney — a channel company turning producer.

对标阅文而非迪士尼是本期关键论点；直接说 be Disney 是误读。

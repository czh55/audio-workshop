Ideal L9 Livis Launch Review: Without an Instant 'Wow' Moment, Can Emotional Value Be a Selling Point?

Four auto-industry guests on the Baba Wu podcast take apart the launch of the Ideal L9 Livis. The Apple-style recorded launch fell flat, and only the 50,000-yuan price cut lit up the comment feed. In an age of homogenized flagships, the L9 Livis fights for emotional value with a 'luxury without shouting' design language and consistent interior-exterior styling. But the 'embodied-intelligence flagship' ships only about 10% of its architecture, lacks the instant visual hit of an 'Ice-Crystal-Sand' paint job, and 70% of test-drive buyers are existing owners coming back to repurchase.

一、发布会：形式与传播的失速

The Launch: A Form and a Message That Stalled

情境The guests analyze why the recorded launch failed to generate excitement. 语域：播客点评 + 发布会传播分析。

01
中文

核心机制：理想延续纯线上录播发布，拒绝线下剧场效应；与小米/华为/蔚来的现场激情发布形成鲜明反差

EN

The mechanism: Ideal stuck with a fully online, pre-recorded launch, refusing the theater effect of a live event — a sharp contrast with the passionate on-stage launches from Xiaomi, Huawei, and NIO.

表达提示pre-recorded launch → 录播发布会；theater effect → 剧场效应；on-stage launch → 现场发布

02
中文

关键理解：任何车在发布会现场都会更「上头」，电脑前观看则冷静理性——这是传播层面的结构性劣势，而非产品本身问题

EN

The insight: any car feels more thrilling at a live launch, while watching on a laptop keeps you cool and rational — that's a structural disadvantage in communication, not a product problem.

表达提示structural disadvantage → 结构性劣势；thrilling → 上头/令人激动；cool and rational → 冷静理性

中文

典型场景：孙少军同时直播两场：老余（余承东）讲话时弹幕沸腾，切到理想画面瞬间安静，直到55.98万直降5万才再次引爆

EN

Typical case: the host Sun Shaojun streamed two launches side by side — when Richard Yu spoke, the comments exploded; the moment the feed cut to Ideal, they went silent, until a 50,000-yuan cut from 559,800 ignited them again.

表达提示side by side → 同时；the comments exploded → 弹幕沸腾；ignite → 引爆

二、产品力：50万的「豪而不登」

The Product: 500K Worth of 'Luxury Without Shouting'

情境The guests evaluate the L9 Livis's design language and interior. 语域：播客点评 + 产品评价。

01
中文

核心机制：理想将L9 Livis定位为「豪而不登」——80%做到了——提供奢华的松弛感，而非问界M9式霸气或极氪9X式张扬

EN

The mechanism: Ideal positions the L9 Livis as 'luxury without shouting' — 80% achieved — offering an opulent sense of relaxation rather than the Aito M9's bossy authority or the Zeekr 9X's showiness.

表达提示luxury without shouting → 豪而不登；opulent → 奢华的；bossy authority → 霸气；showiness → 张扬

02
中文

关键理解：50万级别该有的豪华感、松弛感、自然风格都做到了，外观比近期竞品换脸更优雅——但缺乏「一击命中」的辨识度

EN

The insight: it nails the luxury, relaxation, and natural style expected at the 500,000-yuan level, and the exterior is more elegant than the recent face-lifts of rivals — but it lacks that instant 'one-shot hit' of recognition.

表达提示one-shot hit → 一击命中；face-lift → 换脸/改款；recognition → 辨识度

03
中文

典型场景：女性用户对青绿配色接受度高；70%到店用户认可新内饰设计，形容为「更像客厅」而非「老登风」

EN

Typical case: female buyers respond well to the greenish color option; 70% of in-store customers approve the new interior, describing it as 'more like a living room' rather than 'old-school vibes.'

表达提示color option → 配色；more like a living room → 更像客厅；in-store customers → 到店用户

04
中文

表达提示stand out → 突出；details are done right → 细节到位；have this quality → 有这个质感

05
中文

（引用）与Mega对比：Mega造型过于独一无二排情绪价值第一；L9 Livis内外统一做得更好，排第二

EN

Compared with the Mega: the Mega's design was so unique it ranked first in emotional value; the L9 Livis ranks second because its interior-exterior consistency is better done.

表达提示emotional value → 情绪价值；interior-exterior consistency → 内外统一；rank first/second → 排第一/第二

中文

核心机制：自研马赫100走数据流架构（算力2056 TOPS双芯片）、UWB替代全部超声波雷达、8797支撑舱内视觉多模态交互——前排妈妈开车，后排小朋友想看二排电视，纯靠舱内视觉识别实现

EN

The mechanism: the in-house Mach 100 platform uses a data-flow architecture with 2,056 TOPS of dual-chip compute, UWB replacing all ultrasonic sensors, and the 8797 chip powering in-cabin multimodal vision — so when a mom drives up front and a kid in the back wants the second-row TV, it's handled purely through cabin vision recognition.

表达提示data-flow architecture → 数据流架构；multimodal vision → 多模态视觉；in-cabin → 舱内的

三、争议焦点：双色车身与情绪价值

The Debate: Two-Tone Paint and Emotional Value

情境The guests debate whether the L9 Livis has the emotional 'wow' factor that 500K+ buyers pay for. 语域：播客点评 + 销售一线观察。

01
中文

原因：50万以上用户买单靠情绪价值的一击命中——极氪9X靠「冰彩沙」外观让路虎/保时捷用户瞬间下单；问界M9靠黄尾+商务名片；L9 Livis没有这种「超级喜欢的一个点」

EN

Why: buyers above 500,000 yuan commit on an instant hit of emotional value. The Zeekr 9X's 'Ice-Crystal-Sand' paint makes Land Rover and Porsche owners swipe immediately; the Aito M9 wins with its rear-end presence and business image. The L9 Livis has no such 'one thing to fall in love with.'

表达提示instant hit → 一击命中；fall in love with one thing → 超级喜欢的一个点；rear-end presence → 车尾气场

02
中文

解法：理想押注老车主复购（目前70%–80%）+ 价格直降换量，等新客户数据再验证破圈效果

EN

The fix: Ideal bets on repeat buyers — currently 70% to 80% — plus the price cut to drive volume, waiting for new-customer data to prove whether it breaks out beyond its base.

表达提示repeat buyers → 老车主复购；drive volume → 换量；break out → 破圈

03
中文

EN

Severity: be cautious — existing owners accept it well, but winning new customers outside the base is doubtful.

表达提示be cautious → 小心；win new customers → 拉新；outside the base → 破圈

表达提示hit the showroom → 亮相门店；trade in → 置换；experience the car → 试驾体验

四、具身智能：10% 的旗舰承诺

Embodied Intelligence: A 10% Flagship Promise

情境The guests challenge whether the L9 Livis really earns its 'embodied-intelligence flagship' title. 语域：播客点评 + 技术争议。

01
中文

L9从家庭旗舰变为「具身智能旗舰」，是公司代表未来方向的旗舰产品——但没有说服我它为什么配得上这个title。发布会全程在讲硬件，具身智能核心卖点缺席

EN

The L9 has gone from family flagship to 'embodied-intelligence flagship' — the company's statement of its future direction. But it never convinced me why it deserves that title. The whole launch talked hardware, and the core embodied-intelligence selling point was absent.

表达提示embodied intelligence → 具身智能；statement of direction → 方向宣言；selling point → 卖点

02
中文

（引用）「我非常想在这次发布会听到什么是具身智能……等了半天之后公布价格，就有一种很奇怪的感觉」

EN

'I really wanted this launch to explain what embodied intelligence is... after waiting the whole time, the price dropped and I had this very strange feeling.'

表达提示a strange feeling → 奇怪的感觉；explain what X is → 讲清什么是X

03
中文

核心机制：李想/老汤发布会后直播解释——这是起点，搭建了8797+多模态传感器架构，但目前仅交付约10%功能，其余为长期技术方向

EN

The mechanism: Li Xiang and Lao Tang explained in a post-launch stream — this is the starting point, with the 8797 plus multimodal-sensor architecture in place, but only about 10% of the features are delivered so far; the rest are long-term directions.

表达提示starting point → 起点；delivered features → 已交付功能；long-term direction → 长期技术方向

04
中文

关键理解：肉呆认为真正彰显具身智能的仍是自动驾驶本身——8797、舱内摄像头、车外麦克风能否超越或分担自动驾驶的50%价值？目前非常悲观

EN

The insight: host Roudai argues the thing that truly demonstrates embodied intelligence is still autonomous driving itself — can the 8797 chip, cabin cameras, and external microphones match or share the 50% of value that self-driving carries? Right now he's very pessimistic.

表达提示autonomous driving → 自动驾驶；carry the value → 承载价值；pessimistic → 悲观

05
中文

方法边界：适配新平台拉齐老平台同等体验已属不易；在Autopilot未上车前，其余传感器/执行器再强也难改变本质——「重于营销而轻于实际」

EN

Boundaries: adapting the new platform to match the old one's experience is already hard; until full self-driving actually ships, no matter how strong the other sensors and actuators are, they can't change the essence — it's 'heavier on marketing than on substance.'

表达提示heavier on marketing than on substance → 重于营销而轻于实际；actuator → 执行器；match the experience → 拉齐体验

06
中文

（引用）原文依据：「他认为真正彰显一台车代表具身智能的，依然是自动驾驶本身……所以我非常期待被打脸吧」

EN

As Roudai says: 'What really proves a car represents embodied intelligence is still autonomous driving itself... so I very much look forward to being proven wrong.'

表达提示be proven wrong → 被打脸；look forward to → 期待

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The launch meeting was flat.
✓ The pre-recorded launch fell flat.

「发布会」用 launch（发布），不是 meeting（会议）；fall flat 是固定搭配。

✕ The car is luxury but not deng.
✓ It's luxury without shouting — opulent but understated.

「豪而不登」不能直译；用 understated / without shouting 表达「不张扬」。

✕ The car lacks a point to super-like.
✓ It lacks that one thing buyers instantly fall in love with.

「超级喜欢的一个点」要意译成 one thing to fall in love with，直译不通。

✕ The marketing is heavier than the actual.
✓ It's heavier on marketing than on substance.

「重于营销轻于实际」用 heavier on A than on B 的平行结构。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为发布会只要产品好就不愁卖 → 情绪价值时代，现场氛围与「一击命中」的辨识度同样决定成败
以为50万的车堆配置就有豪华感 → 豪而不登的松弛感与内外统一，比配置堆砌更能打动人心
以为挂上「具身智能旗舰」标签就是旗舰 → 只交付10%功能的头衔是营销，真正的价值要等自动驾驶落地

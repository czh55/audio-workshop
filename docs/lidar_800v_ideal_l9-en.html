四、OTA「遥感」争议：备案与审查的边界

The OTA 'Remote Alteration' Controversy: Filing vs. Review

情境What OTA filing actually means legally, and why it's not substantive code review. 语域：casual 法规科普。

01
中文

核心机制：车企OTA升级需向主管部门备案，记录改动参数、功能调整内容和责任主体，便于事后溯源追责

EN

The mechanism: automakers must file OTA updates with the regulator, recording the changed parameters, the functional adjustments, and the responsible party, so liability can be traced after the fact.

表达提示file with the regulator → 向主管部门备案；liability → 责任；trace after the fact → 事后溯源

02
中文

关键理解：备案≠对底层代码的实质性技术审查。车机系统封闭非开源，监管无法配备足够人力逐车型逐版本排查代码

EN

The insight: filing is not a substantive technical review of the underlying code. The infotainment system is closed and not open source, and the regulator doesn't have enough manpower to check every model and every version line by line.

表达提示substantive technical review → 实质性技术审查；line by line → 逐行排查

03
中文

典型场景：若OTA后悬架/智驾参数异常导致事故，备案记录可精准定位责任主体和改动版本

EN

A typical case: if an OTA update leaves the suspension or driving parameters abnormal and causes an accident, the filing records can precisely locate the responsible party and the changed version.

表达提示responsible party → 责任主体；pinpoint → 精确定位

04
中文

（引用）听友原文：「备案本质是合规的信息登记，和实质性的技术审查完全是两回事」

EN

A listener's words: 'Filing is essentially compliant information registration — a completely different thing from substantive technical review.'

表达提示information registration → 信息登记

05
中文

（引用）相关概念：「遥感」标题戏谑——OTA可远程改变车辆行为，但法律框架靠备案登记+追责制度，非事前逐行审查

EN

Related concept: the 'remote alteration' headline is playful — OTA can change a car's behavior remotely, but the legal framework rests on filing plus accountability, not line-by-line pre-review.

表达提示remote alteration → 远程篡改/遥感；accountability → 追责制度

06
中文

为什么重要：800V 5C充电、420km CLTC续航、四激光雷达都是新上车技术，纸面参数需真实路况验证

EN

Why it matters: 800-volt 5C charging, 420 kilometers of CLTC range, and four LiDARs are all new tech landing on the car — the paper specs still need real-road verification.

表达提示5C charging → 5C超充；CLTC range → CLTC续航；real-road verification → 真实路况验证

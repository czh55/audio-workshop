EP01 Conversation with Liu Zhenyun | When Words Fail Me, I Stay Silent

In this conversation, writer Liu Zhenyun talks about his novel 'The Salty Joke'. He explains how he builds the character Du Taibai through nothing more than his three daily meals, and goes on to explore many-sided lives, the traps of the internet, and a philosophy of silence. Literature, he argues, must capture what philosophy cannot explain — the irregular, the disorderly. When truth is still lacing up its shoes, lies have already run around the world; and when words fail you, silence itself becomes an act. A literary master on why keeping quiet can be the most eloquent thing you do.

S1

一、用一日三餐写人物：文学的哲学

Writing a Character Through Three Meals a Day: The Philosophy of Literature

情境刘震云以《咸的玩笑》中杜太白的一日三餐为例，讲文学如何用细节而非结论来写人物。语域：formal 文学对谈。

01
中文

核心机制：杜太白为拉杂粥凑齐十几种原料、为饺子纠结混香还是浅菜馅、买肉途中改主意买羊肉——看似写吃，实则是写他认真生活的思维习惯与世界观

EN

The core mechanism: Du Taibai gathers a dozen kinds of ingredients for his mixed-grain porridge, agonizes over whether to mix two fillings for his dumplings, and changes his mind to buy mutton halfway to the butcher — it seems to be about eating, but it is really about his habit of living seriously and his worldview.

表达提示core mechanism → 核心机制；mixed-grain porridge → 拉杂粥；change one's mind → 改变主意；habit of living seriously → 认真生活的思维习惯

02
中文

关键理解：作者不能用理性语言直接描述「杜太白是什么样的人」，那会变成作者的创造；读者通过一日三餐的细节自己读出来，才是文学

EN

The key insight: the author cannot describe 'what kind of person Du Taibai is' in rational language — that would become the author's invention; literature happens when readers discover it themselves through the details of three meals a day.

表达提示key insight → 关键理解；rational language → 理性语言；the author's invention → 作者的创造

03
中文

典型场景：当杜太白遭遇困境，对吃不再上心；风波过去重开餐馆，又恢复对食物的认真——吃是守卫生活底线的态度

EN

A typical scene: when Du Taibai falls into trouble, he stops caring about food; once the storm passes and he reopens his restaurant, his seriousness about food returns — eating is an attitude that guards the bottom line of living.

表达提示fall into trouble → 遭遇困境；bottom line → 底线；guard → 守卫/守护

04
中文

（引用）原文依据：看似写的是一日三餐，其实它跟人物结合起来……乐趣产生在对这些食物结构安排的这样一种思考

EN

In the author's own words: it looks like writing about three meals a day, but actually it is tied to the character… the pleasure comes from this kind of thinking about how the food is structured.

表达提示tied to → 与…结合；pleasure comes from → 乐趣产生于；structure the food → 安排食物结构

05
中文

（引用）相关概念：与《追忆似水年华》玛德琳小蛋糕触发记忆、普鲁斯特时间拉长哲学一脉相承

EN

A related idea: this echoes Proust's madeleine cake in 'In Search of Lost Time', where a small cake triggers memory, and Proust's philosophy of stretching time.

表达提示echo → 呼应；madeleine → 玛德琳蛋糕；trigger memory → 触发记忆；stretch time → 拉长时间

06
中文

哲学用理性把世界说明白，文学恰恰要写那些用哲学说不清楚的东西——没有规律的东西。让康德分析杜太白的一日三餐，那是另一个话语系统；文学的理性埋得最深

EN

Philosophy uses reason to explain the world; literature writes exactly what philosophy cannot explain — the things without rules. Ask Kant to analyze Du Taibai's meals and you enter a completely different system of language; literature hides its reason the deepest.

表达提示what philosophy cannot explain → 哲学说不清楚的东西；system of language → 话语系统；hide the deepest → 埋得最深

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「看起来写吃，其实写的是人」

    look like A, but really B · the surface vs the subject
    第一句直白口语；第二句带对比更有文学味

  2. 想说「读者自己读出来才是文学」

    discover for oneself · earn the meaning
    earn it 很口语，适合点评时强调读者的主动性
S2

二、异面人生：A面之外的支撑

Many-Sided Lives: What Supports You Beyond Side A

情境从表哥牛顿到网上白领的B面生活，讲每个人不为人知的一面如何支撑主流生活。语域：casual 文学点评 + 生活观察。

01
中文

核心机制：每个人不只有A面（街上走的那个），还有B/C/D/E/F面——往往不为人知，甚至自己忽略，却支撑着A面

EN

The core mechanism: everyone has more than Side A — the one walking the street; there are Sides B, C, D, E, and F, which are often unknown to others, sometimes ignored even by yourself, yet they support Side A.

表达提示Side A → A面；unknown to others → 不为人知；support → 支撑

02
中文

关键理解：表哥牛顿是农民，却对高等数学物理痴迷，这种乐趣支撑着他枯燥的打工生活；杜太白小时候也叫牛顿——同一精神谱系

EN

The key insight: his cousin Newton is a farmer but is obsessed with higher mathematics and physics — that joy supports his dull working life; Du Taibai was also called Newton as a child, the same spiritual lineage.

表达提示be obsessed with → 痴迷于；spiritual lineage → 精神谱系；dull working life → 枯燥的打工生活

03
中文

典型场景：网上年轻人白天是白领，周末做博物馆文创、在便利店黑板上写「妈妈早点下班」——用B面应对无处落脚的焦虑

EN

A typical scene: young people online are white-collar workers by day, then make museum creative merchandise on weekends or write 'Mom, get off work early' on a convenience store blackboard — using Side B to handle the anxiety with no place to land.

表达提示white-collar → 白领；museum merchandise → 博物馆文创；no place to land → 无处落脚

04
中文

（引用）原文依据：他的乐趣支撑着他……没有乐趣的工作，甚至没有乐趣的生活，但一个人总有一个有乐趣的这种背后的支点

EN

In the author's own words: his joy supports him… a job without joy, even a life without joy, but a person always has one joyful fulcrum hidden behind.

表达提示fulcrum → 支点；hidden behind → 背后的；support sb → 支撑某人

05
中文

（引用）相关概念：「爱你老己」与书中「街上走的每一个人都辛苦了」形成时代共振

EN

A related idea: 'love yourself as an old self' resonates with the line in the book, 'everyone walking the street has worked hard.'

表达提示resonate with → 与…形成共振；work hard → 辛苦了；era resonance → 时代共振

06
中文

人物扁平、单一、只有A面，不是文学的范畴。每个农民、每个小人物都不一样——生活中有那么多值得尊重、值得展示的不为人知的一面，这是写作的重要动力

EN

Flat, one-sided characters are not the scope of literature. Every farmer and every little person is different — life holds so many unknown sides worth respecting and showing, and this is an important engine for writing.

表达提示flat character → 扁平人物；worth respecting → 值得尊重；engine for writing → 写作动力

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「每个人都有不为人知的一面」

    a side nobody sees · a hidden layer
    hidden layer 更书面，访谈场景两者皆可

  2. 想说「B面支撑着A面生活」

    hold sth together · do the heavy lifting
    do the heavy lifting 很地道口语，指承担主要作用
S3

三、作家进化与保持年轻

A Writer's Evolution: How to Stay Young

情境把作家的三个阶段与杜甫的诗路对照，谈认识不停、作家就不老的道理。语域：formal 文学评论。

01
中文

第一阶段：作者有话要说——自己当话筒（《一地鸡毛》时期）

EN

Stage one: the author has something to say — he is his own megaphone (the 'Ground Covered with Chicken Feathers' period).

表达提示have something to say → 有话要说；megaphone → 话筒；period → 时期

02
中文

第二阶段：书里人间有话要说——作者变倾听者（《我不是潘金莲》《一句顶一万句》）

EN

Stage two: the people in the book have something to say — the author becomes a listener ('I Am Not Madame Bovary' and 'One Sentence Is Worth Ten Thousand').

表达提示turn listener → 变成倾听者；the people in the book → 书中人物

03
中文

第三阶段：引于严金宇宙，对大家说「都辛苦了」——像杜甫从「会当凌绝顶」到「国破山河在」到「安得广厦千万间」

EN

Stage three: he turns to a universe and tells everyone 'you've all worked hard' — like Du Fu moving from 'I will climb to the summit' to 'the country broken, rivers and mountains remain' to 'how could I get ten thousand mansions to shelter the poor'.

表达提示turn to → 转向；from A to B to C → 从…到…再到…；summit → 绝顶

04
中文

适用边界：怕的是作家永远停在第一阶段；生活不停，认识若自己停了，就老了

EN

The boundary: the fear is a writer stuck forever in the first stage; life keeps moving, and if your understanding stops, you grow old.

表达提示boundary → 适用边界；stuck in → 停在…；understanding stops → 认识停止

05
中文

（引用）原文依据：你写的越来越老气，你的作品都有老人味，谁愿意爱你？

EN

In the author's own words: the more you write, the older it gets; your work smells of old age — who would love you then?

表达提示old age smell → 老人味；who would love you → 谁愿意爱你；the more… the more… → 越…越…

06
中文

读《咸的玩笑》的以二十多岁三十多岁为主，留言语言非常新鲜——「在水之下漂浮着我们全部的生活」。作品越写越年轻，作家才能长久

EN

Readers of 'The Salty Joke' are mostly in their twenties and thirties, and their comments are very fresh — 'under the water floats all our life.' The younger the writing, the longer the writer lasts.

表达提示the younger the writing → 作品越写越年轻；last → 长久；fresh language → 新鲜语言

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「认识若停了人就老了」

    stop growing · stagnation ages you
    第一句直白；第二句更像金句，适合引用

  2. 想说「作品越年轻，作家越长久」

    the younger… the longer… · lives as long as
    比较结构是本期核心句型，两者都好用
S4

四、互联网困境与沉默哲学

The Internet Trap and the Philosophy of Silence

情境讲杜太白被网暴后的「沉默哲学」，以及互联网如何放大人的阴暗面。语域：formal 点评 + 社会观察。

01
中文

原因：杜太白遭遇网暴，四字概括「动辄得咎」——大家相信的是情绪不是真相；越说越描越黑，往困境的人身上扔石头，人人都是慷慨的

EN

The reason: Du Taibai is attacked by an internet mob — 'blamed no matter what he does'; people believe emotion, not truth; the more you explain, the darker it gets, and everyone is generous when throwing stones at someone in trouble.

表达提示blamed no matter what he does → 动辄得咎；internet mob → 网暴；throw stones at → 往…扔石头

02
中文

解法：沉默——不是低头认输，而是无以自明时不说话；曹务车说「他不说话了，他的沉默震耳欲聋」

EN

The solution: silence — not lowering one's head in defeat, but refusing to speak when nothing can justify oneself; Cao Wuchen says, 'He stopped talking, and his silence was deafening.'

表达提示justify oneself → 自明/自辩；deafening → 震耳欲聋；admit defeat → 低头认输

03
中文

严重程度：致命（对知识分子社会性死亡级别）

EN

The severity: fatal — on the level of social death for an intellectual.

表达提示severity → 严重程度；social death → 社会性死亡；intellectual → 知识分子

04
中文

（引用）嘉宾说法：「当真相还在穿鞋的时候，这个谎言趁着互联网已经跑遍了全世界，无以自白。」

EN

As the guest puts it: 'When truth is still putting on its shoes, this lie, riding the internet, has already run around the whole world.'

表达提示put on shoes → 穿鞋（指准备不及）；ride the internet → 借助互联网；run around the world → 跑遍全世界

05
中文

核心机制：小林白天找杜太白改店名，晚上是网上大V——骂世界各地名人为脚气水和防臭鞋垫带货，破窗效应下全村一哄而上为流量

EN

The core mechanism: Xiao Lin asks Du Taibai to rename his shop by day, but at night he is a big online influencer — attacking celebrities worldwide for endorsing foot-water and stinky-proof insoles; under the broken-window effect, the whole village piles on for traffic.

表达提示influencer → 大V；broken-window effect → 破窗效应；pile on → 一哄而上；for traffic → 为流量

06
中文

关键理解：互联网放大了人的B面甚至Z面；善良铺就的地狱之路——平常扔石头的人都很善良

EN

The key insight: the internet magnifies people's Side B, even Side Z; the road to hell is paved with good intentions — the ordinary people who throw stones are kind.

表达提示magnify → 放大；the road to hell is paved with good intentions → 善良铺就地狱之路；throw stones → 扔石头

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「怎么解释都没用」

    no matter how much · make the painting darker
    第二句呼应原文「越描越黑」的意象，更有画面感

  2. 想说「沉默本身就是态度」

    silence is a stance · its own kind of speaking
    两句话都适合做金句，前者更简短有力
S5

五、慈悲与人物结构的力

Compassion and the Structure of Characters

情境讲小说里敌我关系的反转与慈悲主题，以及刘震云坚持跑步的真实动因。语域：casual 播客点评 + 文学分析。

01
中文

曹务车害了杜太白，最后却在城墙上与他抱头痛哭——过去的敌人成了最知心的人。前妻敌对了一辈子，杜太白崩溃时却给他洗澡、盖被。杜天威跋扈一辈子，醉后在猪圈哭、人死留君（晋明帝典故）

EN

Cao Wuchen harms Du Taibai, yet finally weeps on the city wall with him — the former enemy becomes the most intimate confidant. His ex-wife fought him all her life, yet bathed him and covered him with a blanket when he collapsed. Du Tianwei is arrogant his whole life, cries in a pigpen when drunk, and 'the dead leave their soul behind' (the Jin Emperor allusion).

表达提示confidant → 知心人；collapse → 崩溃；allusion → 典故；former enemy → 过去的敌人

02
中文

（引用）"虽然我跟杜太白有过节……把一个知识分子弄成了一堆垃圾……但他沉默的声音，震耳欲聋。"

EN

A quote: 'Even though Du Taibai and I had our differences… we turned an intellectual into a pile of garbage… but his silent voice is deafening.'

表达提示have differences with → 与…有过节；a pile of garbage → 一堆垃圾；deafening silence → 震耳欲聋的沉默

03
中文

核心机制：两人从未见面，却在认识上突然一致——小事可以指望别人，大事只能靠自己；干苦自知，一点误出来的

EN

The core mechanism: the two never meet, yet suddenly agree in understanding — small matters can be left to others, big matters only to yourself; you taste the bitterness yourself, a realization earned one mistake at a time.

表达提示agree in understanding → 认识上一致；taste the bitterness yourself → 干苦自知；one mistake at a time → 一点误出来的

04
中文

关键理解：致名和尚吃百家饭长大，「人情冷暖、众生共业，正是此辈子来源」；与杜太白在泰山遇绝境形成结构呼应

EN

The key insight: the monk Zhiming grows up eating from a hundred families — 'the warmth and cold of human relations, the shared karma of all beings, is exactly the source of this life' — structurally echoing Du Taibai's desperate situation at Mount Tai.

表达提示warmth and cold of human relations → 人情冷暖；shared karma → 众生共业；echo → 呼应

05
中文

（引用）相关概念：地理结构也讲究——一个事情泰安来到严谨，严谨又到了泰安

EN

A related idea: even geography follows a structure — a matter comes from Tai'an to Yanjin, and Yanjin returns to Tai'an.

表达提示geography → 地理（结构）；come from A to B → 从A到B；return → 回到

06
中文

为什么重要：很多人夸刘震云「很有韧劲能坚持跑步」——因果关系说反了：因为跑步带来多巴胺和内啡肽的乐，才每天跑；写书同理，乐趣是写书最重要的原因

EN

Why it matters: many praise Liu Zhenyun for the persistence to keep running — but the causality is reversed; it is the joy of dopamine and endorphins that makes him run every day, and writing is the same; joy is the most important reason he writes.

表达提示persistence → 韧劲；reversed causality → 说反了的因果关系；dopamine and endorphins → 多巴胺与内啡肽

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「敌人变成了最懂你的人」

    worst enemy → closest friend · ended up being
    第一句对比强，适合概括；第二句更适合叙事

  2. 想说「因乐而写，不是因为坚持」

    because of joy, not discipline · follows naturally
    第二句点破因果关系，适合做总结

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

用「side」结构说：每个人都有不为人知的一面。

Everyone has a side nobody ever sees, and that hidden side is often what keeps you going.

用「the more… the more…」说：越解释越黑。

The more you explain, the darker the picture gets — silence might be the smarter move.

用「deafening」说：沉默震耳欲聋。

He said nothing, and his silence was deafening.

用「joy comes first」说：写作/跑步源于乐趣而非毅力。

Joy comes first — it's the pleasure that keeps him running and writing every day.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ I can learn nothing from his meals.
✓ There's a whole character hidden in the details of his three meals.

learn nothing from 是负面判断；这里想表达的是「细节里藏着一整个人」，用 there's a whole character hidden in 更准确。

✕ His silence is very loud.
✓ His silence was deafening.

very loud 太平淡且搭配生硬；deafening 是固定的地道表达，专指「沉默到震耳欲聋」。

✕ He runs every day because he is very persistent.
✓ It's the dopamine and endorphins that make him run every day — the joy comes first.

把因果说反了；英文要明确「乐趣是因、坚持是果」，用 It's A that B 强调结构。

✕ Truth is wearing shoes but lies ran to the world.
✓ When truth is still putting on its shoes, lies have already run around the whole world.

英文需要用 when/while 连接两个时间线，且 put on its shoes 更贴合「正在穿鞋」的动作感。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为写人物要靠作者直接下结论、贴标签 → 用一日三餐这样的细节让读者自己读出来，才是文学
以为沉默就是认输、不吭声 → 无以自明时沉默本身就是做事，是另一种有力的发声
以为坚持跑步/写作靠毅力硬扛 → 乐趣在前、坚持在后，找到多巴胺与内啡肽才是长久之道

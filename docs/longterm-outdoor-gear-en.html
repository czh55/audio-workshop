Long-Term Outdoor Gear: Spending Every Yuan on Gear That Stays With You

Long-term gear buying isn't about buying expensive — it's four principles added up together: full use (it only pays off if you use it often), one item for many jobs (the more scenarios one piece covers, the more it's worth), real value for money, and eco-friendliness (stop creating more plastic waste). Lao Chai and Che'er Ge, a 20-year outdoor veteran, break down clothes, shoes, backpacks, accessories, sleep systems, and electronics — which categories deserve the one-shot upgrade, which ones you can just grab cheap, and how to dodge the three big traps: spec-sheet fanatics, influencer hype, and disposable gear.

03 衣服：三层穿衣逻辑（08:00）

Clothes: The Three-Layer System (08:00)

情境拆解户外穿衣三层逻辑与羊毛的优势，穿插瑜伽服与平价运动内衣的性价比路线。语域：casual 经验分享 + 穿搭建议。

01
中文

核心机制：切尔哥给出户外穿衣的完整逻辑：贴身层（速干、羊毛内衣）→ 保暖层（软壳、抓绒、棉服、羽绒服，替代毛衣那一层）→ 硬壳（防水冲锋衣）→ 极寒时最外层再压一件厚羽绒服（露营过夜场景）。滑雪、徒步、登山可以共用这一套，这就是「一物多用」在服装上的落地

EN

Che'er Ge lays out the full logic of outdoor dressing: base layer (quick-dry or wool underwear), then insulation (softshell, fleece, puffy, or down — the layer that replaces your sweater), then a hard shell (a waterproof jacket). In extreme cold, add a thick down jacket on top for overnight camping. Skiing, hiking, and climbing can all share this same system — that's the 'one item, many jobs' idea landing on clothes.

表达提示base layer → 贴身层；insulation → 保暖层；softshell / fleece → 软壳 / 抓绒；hard shell → 硬壳（防水冲锋衣）；down jacket → 羽绒服

02
中文

EN

For the base layer, wool beats cotton: merino wool is antibacterial and odor-resistant, dries fast and wicks sweat, so you don't smell even after working out. Look for fiber diameters under 20 microns for a softer, skin-friendly feel. Wool underwear is gentler on skin than compression wear — it won't dig into your joints — while compression gear hugs better and keeps you warmer.

表达提示beats cotton → 比棉更好；antibacterial & odor-resistant → 抑菌防臭；wick sweat → 排汗；fiber diameter → 纤维直径；micron → 微米；dig into joints → 硌关节

05 背包：单日轻量，重装看背负（63:00）

Backpacks: Light for Day Trips, Check the Suspension for Heavy Loads (63:00)

情境按单日包与重装包分拆购买要点，聊三大品牌与「买大牌」理念，附咸鱼捡漏与越野跑背包推荐。语域：casual 装备选购 + 亲测心得。

01
中文

核心机制：单日徒步包（25 到 30 升足够）基本不用考虑背负系统——背板柔软，胸带腰带只起稳定作用；看重量、材质做工、外形顺眼就行。40 升以上的重装包必须看背负系统：背板长度要与身高匹配（火柴棍有科普视频教测量）、看背板设计和重量

EN

For a day-hike pack (25 to 30 liters is plenty), you barely need to think about the suspension system — the back panel is soft, and the sternum strap and waist belt just keep things stable. Just check weight, build quality, and whether it looks right. For heavy-hauling packs over 40 liters, the suspension is everything: the back-panel length has to match your height (Haglöfs has an educational video on measuring), and you need to check the panel design and weight.

表达提示day-hike pack → 单日徒步包；suspension system → 背负系统；back panel → 背板；sternum strap → 胸带；waist belt → 腰带；heavy-hauling pack → 重装包

02
中文

关键理解：背包「三大」是 Osprey（小鹰）、Gregory（格里高利）、Deuter（多特）；切尔哥的理念是「买大牌包」——大部分国产包还在学习西方设计，「他们学谁，我就买谁」。她自用 33 升格里高利单日包（背板偏长、容量偏大、收纳挂点极多）+ 50 多升重装包（替代旅行箱，去海岛徒步汤水上岸都能用）

EN

The 'big three' of backpacks are Osprey, Gregory, and Deuter. Che'er Ge's philosophy is 'buy a big-brand pack' — most Chinese packs are still learning from Western design, so 'whatever they're copying, I buy that instead.' She uses a 33-liter Gregory day pack — longer back panel, roomier capacity, tons of attachment points — plus a 50-plus-liter heavy pack that replaces her suitcase, working for island hikes and even wading ashore from the water.

表达提示big three → 三大品牌；attachment points → 收纳挂点；heavy pack → 重装包；wade ashore → 从水里走上岸；replace one's suitcase → 替代旅行箱

03
中文

（引用）原文依据：「40 升以上的背包基本上都是有背负系统了，所以在有背负系统的背包的时候你就要考虑它的尺寸，就是它背板的尺寸是不是适用你的这个身高。」

EN

'Packs over 40 liters basically all come with a suspension system, so with those you need to consider size — whether the back-panel dimensions actually fit your height.'

表达提示come with → 自带；back-panel dimensions → 背板尺寸；fit your height → 匹配你的身高

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Long-term gear just means buying expensive stuff.
✓ Long-term buying isn't about price — it's about how much you'll actually use the thing.

长期主义不是「买贵的」，核心是使用率；直译容易误导，用 isn't about… it's about… 讲清对比。

✕ The sleeping bag is unhygienic.
✓ Renting a sleeping bag is unhygienic — it's personal gear, like underwear.

主语用 renting… 更自然；再补「私人用品」类比，理由才完整。

✕ These shoes are suitable for me.
✓ These shoes fit me — La Sportiva's lasts suit my narrow feet.

谈鞋用 fit 而不是 suitable for（偏正式生硬）；指出鞋楦 lasts 更显专业。

✕ Buy the newest model every year.
✓ Skip the newest model — grab last year's version and save three or four hundred yuan.

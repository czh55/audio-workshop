Qieqie was moved, but still wanted to trust her feelings — because they weren't just feelings; years of experience and rational judgment were inside them.

表达提示settle down → 安稳定居；run around → 到处跑；rational judgment → 理性判断

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ I was like the movie Groundhog day, every day the same.
✓ Life became a Groundhog Day — every single day the same.

Groundhog Day 是专有名词要首字母大写；用 become 而不是 be like 更简洁自然。

✕ My body issued a warning: I couldn't swallow down rice.
✓ Her body sent an alarm: she couldn't eat, diagnosed with acid reflux.

「吃不下饭」是 couldn't eat / lost her appetite，不是 swallow down rice 逐字直译。

✕ Renting house is like finding a job, very hard.
✓ Renting felt like job hunting — you need references and income proof.

补上「推荐信、收入证明」等细节，表达才完整生动，而非空洞的 very hard。

✕ The landlord gave no compensate.
✓ The landlord offered no compensation.

compensate 是动词，名词是 compensation；offer no compensation 是地道搭配。

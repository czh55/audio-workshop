2. Physics of AI：用物理学方法论理解AI

Physics of AI: Understanding AI Through the Methods of Physics

情境从空间、时间、平行宇宙三个维度讲 Physics of AI 方法论，并对比 Anthropic 的可解释性工作。语域：formal 学术访谈。

01
中文

空间上看得更细：不只看端到端行为，也看内部神经元、内部表征

EN

In space, look closer: don't just watch end-to-end behavior — look inside at the neurons and internal representations.

表达提示end-to-end behavior → 端到端行为；internal representation → 内部表征

02
中文

时间上演化：不只看最后的checkpoint，也看模型如何演化过来——"Nothing in biology makes sense except in light of evolution"

EN

In time, study evolution: don't just look at the final checkpoint — trace how the model evolved. As Dobzhansky said, nothing in biology makes sense except in light of evolution.

表达提示checkpoint → 检查点；in light of evolution → 从进化角度看；trace how something evolved → 追溯演化过程

03
中文

平行宇宙（控制实验）：不同超参数训练不同模型，绘制"相图"——每个trick只在特定的"相"里work

EN

Parallel universes, or controlled experiments: train different models with different hyperparameters and draw a 'phase diagram' — every trick only works in a specific 'phase.'

表达提示controlled experiment → 控制实验；hyperparameter → 超参数；phase diagram → 相图

04
中文

（引用）核心洞察：AI里有很多"相"（如水有固态液态气态）。大家混乱是因为所有人都在说"我的trick work"，但没人回答"在什么条件下work"。Physics of AI的任务就是理清适用条件

EN

The core insight: AI has many 'phases,' just as water has solid, liquid, and gas. Everyone is confused because people keep saying 'my trick works' — but nobody answers under what conditions it works. The job of Physics of AI is to clarify those conditions.

表达提示phase → 相；solid/liquid/gas → 固态/液态/气态；applicability conditions → 适用条件

05
中文

Anthropic的方法：把混合表征升到高维稀疏空间，提取原子概念（如"说脏话的neuron"）

EN

Anthropic's approach: project the entangled representations into a high-dimensional sparse space to extract atomic concepts — like the neuron that fires on profanity.

表达提示sparse space → 稀疏空间；atomic concept → 原子概念；entangled representation → 纠缠/混合表征

06
中文

EN

Ziming Liu's team did similar work extensively from 2022 to 2024, then realized these findings were like 'the biology of AI' — without automated tooling, it would take centuries of old-school science.

表达提示old-school science → 古法科研；automated tooling → 自动化手段；the biology of AI → AI的生物学

01
中文

（引用）刘子鸣对田渊栋路线的关键质疑：如果你的系统只记住了"昨天调学习率失败了"，但不理解为什么失败，那么今天失败可能是因为另一个完全不同的原因——"知道what不够，还需要知道why"

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Research is not as structured as coding, the language of research has not been made yet.
✓ Research isn't as structured as coding — the language of research hasn't been invented yet.

两句之间用破折号或分号连接更自然；「创造一门语言」用 invent / create，不用 make。

✕ Transformer, GAN and ResNet are all beaten out by head.
✓ Transformer, GAN, and ResNet were all made up on a whim.

「拍脑袋」是 make up on a whim / a shot in the dark，直译 beaten out by head 没人能懂。

✕ The system only remembers the failure of adjusting learning rate yesterday.
✓ The system only remembers that tuning the learning rate failed yesterday.

remember + that 从句更地道；「调学习率」是 tune / adjust the learning rate。

✕ Everyone is asking each other for news, the action is very fast.
✓ Everyone is fishing for intel, and the pace of writing checks is extremely fast.

「打探消息」是 fishing for intel；「动作快」结合投资语境用 pace of writing checks，而非直译 action is fast。

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The road is very bad, my body is shaking.
✓ The road was brutal — we bounced around for twelve hours.

body is shaking 是中式说法；bounce around / be jolted 更描述乘车颠簸。

✕ We hired a labor to carry our bags.
✓ We hired a porter through a proper agency.

背夫不是笼统的 labor；porter 是专有名词，且强调通过正规渠道预订。

✕ The food is the most difficult to eat in the world.
✓ Nepal wins the crown for the worst food on earth.

「难吃」不是 difficult to eat；win the crown for worst food 是地道吐槽。

✕ We changed 200 yuan money each person every day.
✓ We changed about 200 yuan per person per day.

中式语序；per person per day 是标准表达，money 冗余。

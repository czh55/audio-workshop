避坑 PITFALLS

✕ He uses study to cheat women to his house.
✓ He lures women to his apartment on the pretext of study discussions.

cheat 是「欺骗钱财」语义；诱骗约见用 lure / deceive into，pretext 点明借口。

✕ She drank too much and forgot everything.
✓ Her blackouts might not be the wine — suspect drug-facilitated assault.

直接归因喝多了很危险；英文要保留怀疑语气 suspect + might，避免误导。

✕ He was sentenced to jail for his whole life.
✓ He received a life sentence, with a minimum of 13 years before parole review.

jail for his whole life 不准确；life sentence 搭配 minimum term before parole 才是司法表述。

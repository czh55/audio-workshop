A 4-Hour Interview with Ming-Yu Liu, NVIDIA's VP of Research

Ming-Yu Liu, NVIDIA's first VP promoted from the research track, sits down with Zhang Xiaojun for a four-hour deep dive a month after Jensen Huang's China visit. He lays out NVIDIA's bet: Physical AI, the 'zero-billion-dollar business' (fine to make no money now, but it must be wildly influential if it succeeds), and building markets instead of fighting over existing ones. He closes with Chinese martial arts: 'the most important thing in kung fu is controlling yourself, not showing destructive power' — model competition shouldn't be a Squid Game.

NVIDIA's Bet and Its Boundaries

情境刘洺堉开篇定义英伟达的定位——运算公司而非 solution 公司，以及老黄「zero-billion-dollar business」的赌注哲学。语域：tech 高管访谈 + 商业哲学。

01
中文

核心机制：Cosmos 瞄准的不是视频创作市场，而是 Physical AI——刘洺堉眼中的「下一个 revolution」，因为它是钢需：人年纪大了没法开车，生命品质会大幅下降；很多工作劳力密集又危险；家庭琐事没人想做。这些「真正能改变物理世界状态的 physical actor」需要世界模型来赋能

EN

Cosmos isn't aimed at the video-creation market — it's aimed at Physical AI, the 'next revolution' in Ming-Yu's eyes, because it's a hard need: when you get old you can't drive and your quality of life drops sharply; many jobs are labor-intensive and dangerous; nobody wants to do household chores. These 'physical actors that can truly change the state of the physical world' need world models to empower them.

表达提示Physical AI → 物理世界 AI；a hard need → 钢需；labor-intensive → 劳力密集；world model → 世界模型；quality of life → 生命品质

表达提示computing company vs solutions company → 运算公司与解决方案公司；computer stack → 从底层芯片到软件的整套技术栈；scratch an itch through your boot → 隔靴搔痒；pain point → 痛点

Accidents, Epiphanies, and Pivots

情境刘洺堉的成长轨迹：台北长大、赴美读博转计算机视觉、三菱电机三年、2016 年加入英伟达，以及两个关键转变。语域：career 自述 + 学习心态。

01
中文

核心机制：台北长大 → 新竹交通大学学无线通讯 → 大三决定去美国（父母震惊）→ 马里兰大学读博转计算机视觉 → 三菱电机研究所三年（毕业那年 AlexNet 出现）→ 2016 年加入英伟达至今 10 年

EN

Grew up in Taipei, studied wireless communication at National Chiao Tung University in Hsinchu, decided in his junior year to go to the US (his parents were shocked), did a PhD at the University of Maryland switching to computer vision, spent three years at Mitsubishi Electric Research — the year he graduated, AlexNet appeared — then joined NVIDIA in 2016, ten years and counting.

表达提示junior year → 大三；switch to computer vision → 转计算机视觉；ten years and counting → 至今 10 年；AlexNet → 引爆深度学习的经典卷积网络

02
中文

关键理解：AI 对他是一场意外。在台湾英特尔实习时第一次看到「从像素判断物件」的语音/图像识别，觉得神奇；「因为不懂，所以很容易理解它的重要性」。博士期间学的 SVM、图形模型在他毕业那年突然「什么都不 work」了，但他早已养成「什么都学」的习惯。他 2016-2017 年就预判「真正产生突破的第一个领域是 language」，只是「比较喜欢视觉信号，所以一直留在这里」

EN

AI was an accident for him. Interning at Intel Taiwan, he saw speech and image recognition that could 'judge objects from pixels' for the first time and found it magical — 'because I didn't understand it, it was easy to understand its importance.' The SVM and graphical models he learned in his PhD suddenly 'stopped working' the year he graduated, but he'd long built the habit of 'learning everything.' As early as 2016-2017 he predicted that 'the first field to truly break through is language,' only 'he likes visual signals more, so he's stayed here.'

表达提示SVM（support vector machine）→ 支持向量机；graphical model → 图形模型；break through → 产生突破；visual signal → 视觉信号；judge objects from pixels → 从像素判断物件

Typical scenario: when a path dead-ends and the era shifts, how not to panic — treat 'everything I learned stopped working' as the norm, and make 'learning everything' a habit.

表达提示dead-end → 走到头；era shift → 时代切换；treat sth as the norm → 把…看成常态；make sth a habit → 养成…的习惯

三、如何在英伟达拿到 GPU（01:02:46）

How to Win GPUs at NVIDIA

情境聊研究如何争取算力：懂公司要解的问题、解释影响、对齐兴趣与公司利益，并用 DALL·E 后拿到 1000 张 A100 的故事佐证。语域：职场生存 + 内部决策故事。

01
中文

核心机制：「你要懂公司要解的问题是什么，然后你要想办法跟公司解释，为什么你做的东西会对公司产生影响。」研究员普遍 high ego、文人相轻，但公司的本质是为股东获利——老黄希望 take care of family，公司要赚钱

EN

'You have to understand the problem the company is trying to solve, then find a way to explain to the company why what you're doing will have an impact on it.' Researchers generally have high egos and look down on each other, but a company exists to make a profit for its shareholders — Jensen wants to take care of the family, and the company has to make money.

表达提示high ego → 自我很强；look down on each other → 文人相轻；take care of the family → 照顾家人（老黄常用语）；have an impact on → 对…产生影响

02
中文

关键理解：他亲眼见过三菱电机研究院的教训：90 年代它是和微软研究院并驾齐驱的机构，但当公司方向改变、开始怀疑 blue sky research 的价值时，「一堆重要的学者陆陆续续被 fired」。所以做研究「要让自己的兴趣跟公司的兴趣可以对齐」，尤其在 deep learning 这种极其烧钱的领域，更要解释「为什么这对公司是一个重要的投资」

EN

He witnessed the lesson at Mitsubishi Electric Research firsthand: in the nineties it was an institution on par with Microsoft Research, but when the company changed direction and started doubting the value of blue-sky research, 'a bunch of important scholars got fired one after another.' So when doing research, 'you need to align your interest with the company's interest' — especially in a money-burning field like deep learning, you need to explain 'why this is an important investment for the company.'

表达提示on par with → 与…并驾齐驱；blue-sky research → 蓝天/基础研究；get fired one after another → 陆陆续续被解雇；align A with B → 让 A 与 B 对齐；money-burning → 极其烧钱的

03
中文

怎么用：每次立项先回答「这对公司为什么重要、和我们的生意有什么关系」——这不是妥协，是生存技能

EN

How to use it: before every project, answer first 'why does this matter to the company, and how does it relate to our business?' — that's not compromise, it's a survival skill.

表达提示survival skill → 生存技能；not compromise → 不是妥协；matter to the company → 对公司重要

四、从研究员到英伟达 VP（01:21:57）

From Researcher to NVIDIA VP

情境聊晋升路径、带 Cosmos 团队的信任基础，以及「别跟底层脱钩」的管理自省。语域：career 复盘 + 管理心得。

01
中文

核心机制：「我可能是第一个（从研究员做到 VP）吧。」前几年每年升一级，一直跳到 VP。原因有三：早期英伟达还没有 AI 人才时，他帮助公司提升 AI 研究知名度、解释 AI 怎么影响公司；自己又很喜欢落地（research 变成产品）；一步一步获得公司信任，公司就愿意把越来越大规模的项目交给他

EN

'I might be the first one, yeah.' In the first few years he was promoted a level every year, all the way to VP. Three reasons: early on, when NVIDIA had no AI talent yet, he helped raise the company's AI research profile and explained how AI would affect it; he also genuinely loved shipping — turning research into products; and he earned the company's trust step by step, so it kept handing him bigger and bigger projects.

表达提示be promoted a level every year → 每年升一级；raise the profile → 提升知名度；shipping → 落地、把东西做出来；earn trust step by step → 一步步获得信任

02
中文

关键理解：现在整个 Cosmos 团队每天烧掉几百万美金的运算成本——「公司有一定的信任，才有这个机会去带这么大的 project。所以你跟公司 align 非常重要。」

EN

The whole Cosmos team now burns millions of dollars of compute every day — 'the company only gives you this chance to lead a project this big if it has a certain level of trust in you. So aligning with the company is very important.'

表达提示burn millions of dollars → 烧掉几百万美金；compute cost → 算力成本；align with the company → 与公司对齐

03
中文

EN

The risk warning: the thing he worries about most as a manager is 'losing touch with the fundamentals' — that's why he's still 'one of the few VPs who still read code', keeping on reviewing everyone's code, reading papers, and challenging each person's idea.

表达提示lose touch with → 与…脱钩；read code → 看代码；challenge an idea → 质疑某个想法；the fundamentals → 底层/基本面

EN

'What I worry about most is losing touch with the fundamentals and not understanding the core of the problem. I force myself to review everyone's code, read papers, and challenge each person's idea.'

表达提示the core of the problem → 问题的核心所在；force oneself to do → 强迫自己做

05
中文

（引用）落地建议：管理者的自我保鲜——保留一个「动手回路」：哪怕不做实现，也要定期读代码、读论文、参与技术辩论，保持对问题本质的触觉

表达提示hands-on loop → 动手回路；keep one's feel for → 保持对…的触觉；technical debate → 技术辩论

表达提示paper authorship → 论文署名；a tug between A and B → A 与 B 之间的张力；horse-racing → 赛马行为；progress crawls → 进展缓慢

五、Cosmos 的诞生（01:37:00）

The Birth of Cosmos

情境Cosmos 的起源：10 年前的 Job Talk、DALL·E 的刺激、Sora 后的立项，以及为什么从视频生成做进物理世界。语域：产品起源故事 + 决策复盘。

01
中文

核心机制：10 年前加入英伟达的 Job Talk 他就说：「有一天最主要生成视频的运算将会是 deep neural（深度神经网络），而不会是传统的 computer graphics。」之后在英伟达做了 GAN、GauGAN（取名自画家高更）、StyleGAN 团队合作，建立 Deep Imagination Research Group，又做了 Picasso（AI foundry，与 Getty Images、Shutterstock 合作）

EN

真实场景：疫情期间很多公司裁员，英伟达的选择是砍 travel、砍工具（考虑不用 Slack 来省钱）——「照顾员工，一起度过困难」

EN

A real example: during the pandemic, when many companies laid people off, NVIDIA's choice was to cut travel and cut tools (even considering not using Slack to save money) — 'take care of the employees and get through the hard times together.'

表达提示cut travel → 砍差旅；Slack → 企业协作软件；get through the hard times → 度过困难

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「任务导向而非老板导向」

    outranks · the mission is
    第二句是口号式转述，简洁有力；outrank 偏书面

  2. 想说「不裁员的组织靠信任」

    afford to speak up · security breeds honesty
    第二句对仗、适合做金句；第一句更平实
S10

十、模型竞争不是鱿鱼游戏（03:12:20）

Model Competition Isn't a Squid Game

情境从研究员的竞争心态到管理者的造市场心态，以及为什么历史上没有公司能垄断所有科技。语域：心态转变 + 行业判断。

01
中文

在讲什么：研究员的毕业路径是「打败别人的 SOTA」——review 会问你是不是 state of the art，所以研究员天生是 highly competitive 的个体，「我的东西要比你强」。但当他做了管理者、站在英伟达生态的位置上，心态变了：「我不太想竞争了，我已经过那个时期。」

EN

A researcher's career path is 'beating someone else's SOTA' — reviews ask if you're state of the art, so researchers are born highly competitive individuals: 'my thing has to be stronger than yours.' But once he became a manager, standing at NVIDIA's position in the ecosystem, his mindset changed: 'I don't really want to compete anymore — I've already been past that stage.'

表达提示SOTA（state of the art）→ 当前最好水平；highly competitive → 竞争心很强；past that stage → 过了那个时期

02
中文

关键理解：这不是隐藏，而是一种 confidence：「我们做出来的东西是可以帮助到大家的。我的目标并不是击败对手，我的目标是做出好成绩、提出更好的 foundation，让大家可以动得更快。」看到伙伴说「用你的模型之后效率提升了很多」，「这种肯定也是很好的一件事情」——「你不一定需要击败你的对手才能产生贡献」

EN

This isn't hiding — it's a kind of confidence: 'what we make can genuinely help everyone. My goal isn't to defeat rivals; my goal is to produce great results and put out a better foundation, so everyone can move faster.' When a partner says 'after using your model, our efficiency went up a lot,' 'that kind of recognition is also a great thing' — 'you don't necessarily have to defeat your rival to make a contribution.'

表达提示recognition → 肯定、认可；make a contribution → 产生贡献；defeat a rival → 击败对手；put out a foundation → 推出底座

03
中文

（引用）「什么叫战争呢？我没有打算把别人杀死。我们的心态不是想把别人给杀死，我们的心态是怎么去造市场。」

EN

'What does war even mean? I have no plan to kill others. Our mindset isn't about killing the others — our mindset is how to build the market.'

表达提示build the market → 造市场；not X … but Y → 不是…而是…；对比句式要完整

04
中文

（引用）与其他概念的关系：从「击败别人」到「帮助别人」的转变，正是从 researcher 到 VP、从个人竞争到生态经营的必然切换

EN

The shift from 'defeating others' to 'helping others' is exactly the necessary switch from researcher to VP, from personal competition to ecosystem stewardship.

表达提示ecosystem stewardship → 生态经营；necessary switch → 必然切换；personal competition → 个人竞争

05
中文

（引用）一句话结论：位置决定打法——英伟达不参与鱿鱼游戏，因为它要的是整片海域

EN

One-line takeaway: position dictates playbook — NVIDIA doesn't join the Squid Game, because what it wants is the whole ocean.

表达提示position dictates playbook → 位置决定打法；Squid Game → 鱿鱼游戏；the whole ocean → 整片海域

06
中文

核心机制：「纵观历史，从来没有一个公司掌握所有的科技、其他公司都无法追上——这从来没有在世界上发生过，我不觉得 AI 会是一个例外。」软件时代刚出现时会写软件的公司很少，现在大家都会写软件，「那些活下来的软件公司都是有区分方式的，没有理由模型会是例外」

EN

'Throughout history, no company has ever mastered all the technology with every other company unable to catch up — that has never happened in this world, and I don't think AI will be an exception.' When the software era first appeared, few companies could write software; now everyone can. 'The software companies that survived all found a way to differentiate — there's no reason models will be the exception.'

表达提示catch up → 追上；differentiate → 做出区分；no reason … will be the exception → 没有理由…会是例外

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「我不再以击败别人为目标」

    winner-takes-all · stopped being the point
    winner-takes-all 是行业术语「赢家通吃」；第二句更口语

  2. 想说「模型领域不会出现垄断」

    own AI · differentiation is how you survive
    第一句直白有力；第二句在判断之外给出生存解法
S11

避坑总结与方法边界

Pitfalls & Boundary Notes

情境本期避坑总结：与巨头硬拼的致命错误与研究员的 high ego 陷阱。语域：总结 + 方法论。

01
中文

原因：在已经拥挤的赛道上跟资源比你大十倍的公司硬拼——「你的资金绝对没有他们雄厚，他们没有客户壁垒，他们把东西做便宜，你不容易追上」

EN

Why: head-to-head with a company ten times your resources on an already crowded track — 'your funding will never be as strong as theirs; they don't have customer moats to protect, they make things cheap, and you can't easily catch up.'

表达提示head-to-head → 硬碰硬；crowded track → 拥挤的赛道；customer moat → 客户壁垒

02
中文

解法：去找「他们没办法解」的痛点——不是他们不想解，是他们不能 justify 为什么要去解

EN

Fix: find the pain points they 'can't solve' — it's not that they don't want to solve them, it's that they can't justify why they should.

表达提示pain point → 痛点；justify → 论证合理性；not that … it's that … → 不是…而是…

03
中文

严重程度：致命——方向错了，努力越大损失越大

EN

Severity: fatal — get the direction wrong, and the more you work, the more you lose.

表达提示fatal → 致命；the more … the more … → 越…越…

04
中文

（引用）嘉宾说法：「如果你现在开一间公司，要跟 OpenAI、Anthropic 做一样的事情，很危险。」

EN

'If you start a company today doing the same thing as OpenAI and Anthropic, that's very dangerous.'

表达提示do the same thing as → 做和…一样的事情；start a company → 开公司

05
中文

原因：「研究员某方面来讲都是 very high ego，每个人都觉得自己想法比别人好」——这让人沉迷于「我的算法比你强」，却忘了公司要的是「这对公司为什么重要」

EN

Why: 'in some sense researchers all have very high egos — everyone thinks their own idea is better than everyone else's' — that hooks people on 'my algorithm is stronger than yours', and makes them forget what the company wants: 'why is this important to the company?'

表达提示high ego → 自我很强；get hooked on → 沉迷于；algorithm → 算法

06
中文

解法：研究立项时同时回答两个问题：它是不是前沿？它和公司的生意为什么相关？——两个答案缺一不可

EN

Fix: when starting a research project, answer two questions at the same time: is it cutting-edge? And why is it relevant to the company's business? — both answers are indispensable.

表达提示cutting-edge → 前沿；relevant to the business → 与生意相关；indispensable → 缺一不可

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「别在别人的主场硬拼」

    on its own turf · pick a different fight
    fight on its own turf 是惯用比喻；pick a different fight 是行动建议，更口语

  2. 想说「研究要同时回答前沿与相关」

    just a hobby · a reason to exist
    hobby 的贬义对比很强；reason to exist 简洁准确

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

用「picks and shovels」说：英伟达卖铲子，不挖金矿。

NVIDIA sells the picks and shovels — it powers solution companies instead of being one.

用「zero-billion-dollar business」说：现在不赚钱，成功后极其有影响力。

Cosmos is a zero-billion-dollar business — no revenue today, wildly influential if it lands.

用「mission is the boss」说：任务比任何经理都大。

At NVIDIA, mission is the boss — the mission outranks any manager.

用「align」说：立项前先让兴趣与公司利益对齐。

Before starting any project, align your interest with the company's business.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ NVIDIA is a solution company.
✓ NVIDIA is a computing company — it helps the companies that build solutions succeed.

把英伟达说成 solution 公司就丢了核心定位；用 computing company + helps … succeed 的对照才准确。

✕ I want to ask my boss for 1000 A100 GPUs.
✓ To win GPUs, explain why your work impacts the problem the company is solving.

要 GPU 不是「要」而是「证明相关性」；用 explain the impact / align 说清机制，才能体现访谈精髓。

✕ NVIDIA has no cut person.
✓ NVIDIA never lays people off and has no stack-ranking.

「裁员」英语说 fire / lay off，不是 cut person；「末尾淘汰」是 stack-ranking，二者含义不同。

✕ Our mindset is not killing others, our mindset is making market.
✓ Our mindset isn't about killing competitors — it's about building the market.

make market 是中式说法；英语说 build / grow the market。并列对比用 it's not A — it's B 更地道。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为做研究就是比谁的算法在榜单上更强 → 研究员立项前也先回答「这为什么对公司重要」——兴趣与公司利益对齐
以为产品/模型的 SKU 越多越全能 → 备多则力寡——从 22 个模型收敛到一个 Omni 模型，少即是多
以为 AI 竞争是鱿鱼游戏式的零和博弈 → 造市场而非存量竞争——不击败对手也能产生贡献，英伟达要的是整片海域

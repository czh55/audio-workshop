Part 1 · 纽约印象：从锁着的柜子到一盘沙拉（02:00-10:10）

First Impressions of New York: From Locked Cabinets to a Salad

情境大卫翁从日本搬到纽约的第一观感，与完成 100 位听友 coffee chat 的收官时刻。语域：casual 现场漫谈 + 城市观察。

01
中文

在讲什么：大卫翁从日本直接搬来纽约，第一观感并不好——他感觉是从「一种极度秩序的运行模式」来到了「极度无秩序的运行模式」，这种 culture shock 甚至比从中国国内直接来还要明显

EN

He moved to New York straight from Japan, and his first impression wasn't great — it felt like going from one mode of the world, an extremely ordered one, to an extremely disordered mode, and that culture shock was even sharper than coming straight from China.

表达提示first impression → 第一观感；an extremely ordered / disordered mode → 极度秩序/无秩序的运行模式；culture shock → 文化冲击

02
中文

关键理解：最具体直白的体感是：走进 CVS 便利店，发现有些柜子是锁着的。他很多年没有在日本便利店或中国商店里见过上锁的柜子，这种不安感是「对一座城市的观感」里最直接的证据

EN

The most concrete, visceral sign: walking into a CVS and seeing some shelves locked up. He hadn't seen locked cabinets in Japanese convenience stores or Chinese shops in years — that unease is the most direct evidence of how he feels about a city.

表达提示visceral → 身体层面、直感的；locked cabinet → 上锁的柜子；unease → 不安感；evidence of how one feels about a city → 对城市观感的证据

03
中文

与其他概念的关系：这是「沙拉」比喻和三国内在对比的感性起点——先有身体层面的不适，才有后面结构层面的洞察

EN

This is the emotional starting point for the 'salad' metaphor and the China-Japan-US comparison — physical discomfort comes first, structural insight follows.

表达提示emotional / sensory starting point → 感性起点；physical discomfort → 身体层面的不适；structural insight → 结构层面的洞察；come first / follow → 先有…才有…

04
中文

怎么用：评价一座城市要把观感拆成三块：天气影响短期观感，人决定长期观感，基础设施是另一回事——他搬去上东区 co-op 后，因为认识了人，纽约才变得「可爱起来」

EN

To judge a city, split your feeling into three parts: weather shapes your short-term impression, people decide the long-term one, and infrastructure is its own thing. It was only after he moved to an Upper East Side co-op and got to know people that New York became 'lovable'.

表达提示split sth into → 把…拆成；short-term / long-term impression → 短期/长期观感；co-op → 合作公寓（纽约常见住房形态）；get to know people → 认识人

05
中文

（引用）原文依据：「你感觉你从世界的一种运行模式，一种极度秩序的运行模式，来到了一个极度没有秩序的运行模式。那种 culture shock，可能比你直接从中国国内来还要更加的明显。」

EN

'You feel like you've gone from one operating mode of the world — an extremely ordered one — to an extremely disordered one. That culture shock might be even more obvious than if you'd come straight from China.'

表达提示operating mode → 运行模式；go from A to B → 从 A 到 B；might be even more obvious → 可能还要更加明显

06
中文

在讲什么：来纽约前他立了个 flag——和 100 位听友 coffee chat，昨天宣布完成。9 个月里他走了十几个州，几乎每个城市都约听友聊天

EN

Before coming to New York he set a goal — coffee chats with 100 listeners — and yesterday he announced he'd done it. In nine months he traveled through more than a dozen states, meeting listeners in nearly every city.

表达提示set a goal / make a pledge → 立 flag；coffee chat → 约人喝咖啡聊天；more than a dozen states → 十几个州

  1. 想说「从极度有序到极度无序的冲击」

    hyper-ordered · whiplash · hit harder than
    whiplash 指被从一种状态猛甩到另一种状态，形容 culture shock 非常形象；hit harder than 日常口语常用

Part 2 · 中国、日本、美国：三个国家三种「时间感」（11:00-24:00）

China, Japan, and the US: Three Countries, Three Senses of Time

情境把三国比作分别活在「过去/未来/两边都有」三种时间感，用送报纸站、地铁等生活细节佐证。语域：文化对比漫谈 + 生活观察。

01
中文

在讲什么：大卫翁先后在日本住了两年多、又来美国，跳出「牛马身份」后再看中日美三国，发现三国的状态「非常非常的不同」

EN

After living in Japan for over two years and then coming to the US, once he stepped out of the daily-grind identity he found the three countries extremely, extremely different.

表达提示the daily grind → 牛马般的日常（忙碌打工状态）；step out of → 跳出；extremely different → 非常非常的不同

02
中文

关键理解：日本是个活在过去的社会：它提供的服务跟二三十年前变化不大，还保留着送报纸的站、报纸杂志仍是重要的信息来源。当地人会觉得「不如从前了、开始下降了」，但相对其他国家的变化，日本的节奏极其缓慢

EN

Japan is a society living in the past: its services have barely changed in twenty or thirty years, and there are still newspaper-delivery stands, with print papers and magazines remaining an important source of information. Locals feel things 'aren't what they used to be', but compared with how fast other countries change, Japan moves at a glacial pace.

表达提示live in the past → 活在过去；barely change → 变化不大；newspaper-delivery stand → 送报纸的站；aren't what they used to be → 不如从前；glacial pace → 极其缓慢的节奏

03
中文

与其他概念的关系：去日本旅游「很舒适」的原因恰恰在此——它没有那么多吵闹的新东西；这与中国的「未来感」形成两极对照

EN

That's exactly why traveling in Japan feels so comfortable — there isn't much noisy new stuff. It's the polar opposite of China's future-facing buzz.

表达提示noisy new stuff → 吵闹的新东西；polar opposite → 两极对照；future-facing → 面向未来的；buzz → 喧闹、活力

04
中文

怎么用：判断一个社会的性格，看它保留了多少旧生活方式：送报纸的站、报刊亭、纸质杂志的销量，都是时间感的温度计

EN

To read a society's character, look at how much of the old way of life it keeps: newspaper stands, news kiosks, and print-magazine sales are all thermometers of a society's sense of time.

表达提示read a society's character → 判断社会性格；the old way of life → 旧生活方式；thermometer of … → …的温度计（晴雨表）

05
中文

（引用）原文依据：「日本是一个活在过去的社会。」「保留了……甚至还有一个送报纸的站，每天早上还有人去那里把报纸拿来。」

EN

'Japan is a society that lives in the past.' 'It still keeps — there's even a newspaper-delivery stand, and every morning people still go there to pick up their papers.'

表达提示pick up one's papers → 取报纸；it still keeps → 它保留了…；daily life detail → 生活细节

06
中文

在讲什么：美国「有一部分在过去，有一部分在未来」：聊 AI，所有人都告诉你「下个礼拜又不一样了」；但聊地铁，答案可能是「十年以后还是这样」

EN

The US is 'partly in the past, partly in the future': talk AI and everyone tells you 'next week it'll be different again'; talk subway and the answer might be 'in ten years it'll look the same'.

表达提示partly in the past, partly in the future → 一部分在过去、一部分在未来；next week it'll be different → 下个礼拜又不一样了

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「日本变化极其缓慢」

    frozen in time · glacial pace
    frozen in time 很有画面感，口语和文章都适用；glacial pace 偏书面但更精确

  2. 想说「美国新旧并存」

    half stuck … half sprinting · live side by side
    第一句用对比修辞形容 AI 与地铁，很生动；第二句平实直白

Part 3 · 从金融白领到内容创作者（26:40-39:30）

From Finance White-Collar to Content Creator

情境聊离开金融行业的真实动机：不是想通的瞬间，也不是 FIRE 剧本，而是半年一年的反复拉扯。语域：personal 自述 + 职业反思。

01
中文

在讲什么：被问「为什么离开金融」，他澄清：不是一个「想通了的瞬间」，也不是攒够了钱的 FIRE 剧本——那是长达半年一年的「反复拉扯」

EN

Asked why he left finance, he clarifies: it wasn't a single 'moment of clarity', nor was it the FIRE script of having saved enough — it was a back-and-forth tug-of-war that lasted six months to a year.

表达提示moment of clarity → 想通了的瞬间；FIRE（Financial Independence, Retire Early）→ 财务独立提前退休；tug-of-war → 反复拉扯

02
中文

关键理解：他原本差点入职另一家公募基金（创始人在疫情期间和他视频聊了五六个小时），最后机缘巧合没去；于是把一直想做的「去日本学日语」执行了。做着做着发现：好像也没有那么需要回到金融机构做一份职场工作。确实有那个算账的瞬间——如果不再往上加一个零的欲望，当时的资产积累已经足够休息很长一段时间

EN

He almost joined another mutual-fund company — the founder video-chatted with him for five or six hours during the pandemic — but it fell through by chance. So he finally executed the thing he'd always wanted to do: learn Japanese in Japan. And as he went along, he found he didn't really need to go back to an institutional job. There was a genuine money moment: once he stopped wanting to add another zero, his accumulated savings were already enough to rest for a long, long time.

表达提示fall through → 泡汤/没成；by chance → 机缘巧合；execute the thing you always wanted → 把一直想做的事执行掉；money moment / do the math → 算账的瞬间；add another zero → 再往上加一个零

03
中文

与其他概念的关系：他不分享「FIRE 秘诀」，因为清楚自己享受了房地产时代红利：在懵懵懂懂、工作一两年时「租房和买房月供差不多，那就买了」，然后被时代裹挟着完成了资本原始积累——这让他对「红利」这个话题格外清醒

EN

He won't share a 'FIRE secret' because he knows he enjoyed the real-estate era's dividend: when he was young and clueless, barely a year or two into working, 'the monthly mortgage was about the same as rent, so he bought' — and he got carried along by the times into completing his original capital accumulation. That's why he's unusually clear-eyed about 'windfalls'.

表达提示monthly mortgage → 月供；get carried along by the times → 被时代裹挟着；original capital accumulation → 资本的原始积累；clear-eyed about … → 对…格外清醒

04
中文

怎么用：重大转型不是等一个「想通时刻」，而是把已有的小冲动（学一门语言、做一个项目）先执行掉，让现实慢慢告诉你答案

EN

Big transitions don't come from waiting for a 'eureka moment' — you execute the small urges you already have (learning a language, starting a project) and let reality slowly tell you the answer.

表达提示eureka moment → 想通的时刻；execute a small urge → 把小冲动执行掉；let reality tell you the answer → 让现实告诉你答案

05
中文

（引用）原文依据：「它是一个反复拉扯的关系。」「你是裹挟着，完成了一个资本的原始积累的……它是在你懵懵懂懂的时候。」

EN

'It was a relationship of constant back-and-forth tugging.' 'You were carried along and completed an original capital accumulation … it happened while you were young and clueless.'

表达提示back-and-forth tugging → 反复拉扯；carry along → 裹挟着；young and clueless → 懵懵懂懂

06
中文

在讲什么：离开主权基金后，他进了一家做「连接金融知识和普通人」的公司——实际上是管一个公众号，后来做播客。核心驱动力是即时反馈：每天都能看到阅读量、评论在变化

EN

After leaving the sovereign fund, he joined a company that 'connects financial knowledge to ordinary people' — really, he ran a WeChat public account and later started a podcast. The core driver was instant feedback: every day he could see the read counts and comments moving.

表达提示sovereign wealth fund → 主权基金；WeChat public account → 公众号；instant feedback → 即时反馈；read count → 阅读量

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「离职不是灵光一现，而是长期拉扯」

    epiphany · lightbulb moment · go back and forth
    lightbulb moment 比 epiphany 更口语；go back and forth 把「反复拉扯」说得很自然

  2. 想说「被时代红利裹挟」

    sweep along · the times did the accumulating
    swept along 形象表达被动裹挟；第二句是口语化的点睛说法

Part 4 · 关于「乱纪元」（42:07-58:10）

The 'Chaotic Era': Living in Unpredictability

情境借用《三体》「乱纪元」概念讨论宏观不可预测与普通人的应变结构。语域：philosophical 漫谈 + 个人经历。

01
中文

在讲什么：词出自《三体》：乱纪元里你永远不知道第二天会发生什么——太阳升不升、温度是 30 度还是零下 30 度；小说里的人必须把自己「变成一张纸」储存下来，等到恒纪元再复活

EN

The term comes from 'The Three-Body Problem': in a Chaotic Era you never know what tomorrow brings — whether the sun rises, or whether it's 30 degrees or minus 30. People in the book have to 'fold themselves into a sheet of paper' and hibernate until a Stable Era brings them back.

表达提示Chaotic Era → 乱纪元；Stable Era → 恒纪元（三体概念）；fold oneself into a sheet of paper → 把自己变成一张纸；hibernate → 休眠

02
中文

关键理解：他和朋友最早聊这个概念时主要指宏观，没想到宏观后来真能乱成这样。乱纪元的本质是不可预测性——地缘政治、经济事件无法预测。资本市场的反应很有趣：宏观不可预测，大家干脆不看它了（不看美联储降息加息了），转而只看个股的技术线和 AI——「AI 这个东西你怎么不会骗我吧，你肯定是在往前发展的」。但对普通人的影响是滞后感知的：没影响你之前，你觉得它只是新闻、是发生在远方的事；等零下 30 度的寒潮真的落到身上，再应对可能已经晚了

EN

When he and his friends first used the term, they mainly meant the macro picture — they never expected the macro to actually get this chaotic. The essence of a Chaotic Era is unpredictability: geopolitics and economic events can't be forecast. The market's reaction is funny: since the macro is unpredictable, everyone just stops watching it (no more tracking the Fed's rate moves) and turns to individual stock charts and AI — 'AI wouldn't lie to me, right? It has to be moving forward.' But for ordinary people the impact is felt late: until it touches you, it's just news, something happening far away; by the time the minus-30 cold snap actually lands on you, responding may already be too late.

表达提示the macro picture → 宏观面；geopolitics → 地缘政治；the Fed's rate moves → 美联储降息加息；individual stock charts → 个股技术线；cold snap → 寒潮；the impact is felt late → 影响是滞后感知的

03
中文

与其他概念的关系：乱纪元解释了他一系列选择：资产配置多元化、肉身选择权（在日本生活一段时间、保留能挪动的能力——他在上海完整经历了三个多月的封控，那种「不知道哪天突然完全无法挪动」的体验刻骨铭心）

EN

The Chaotic Era explains a whole series of his choices: diversifying asset allocation, and keeping physical optionality — living in Japan for a while, staying able to move. He lived through more than three months of lockdown in Shanghai, and that feeling of 'one day you might suddenly be completely unable to move' is seared into his memory.

表达提示asset allocation → 资产配置；optionality → 选择权（投资术语借用到人生）；lockdown → 封控；seared into one's memory → 刻骨铭心

04
中文

怎么用：在乱纪元里不要追求预测，追求应变结构：多币种、多地点、多技能，给「自己」保留选择权

EN

In a Chaotic Era, don't chase prediction — build a response structure: multiple currencies, multiple locations, multiple skills. Keep the option for yourself.

表达提示chase prediction → 追求预测；response structure → 应变结构；keep one's options open → 保留选择权

05
中文

（引用）「他（乱纪元）没有影响你之前，你都会觉得他跟你没有关系，他只是一个新闻报道，他只是一个发生在远方的事情。」

EN

'Before the Chaotic Era touches you, you always think it has nothing to do with you — it's just a news report, just something happening far away.'

表达提示have nothing to do with you → 与你无关；a news report → 一则新闻报道；happening far away → 发生在远方

06
中文

在讲什么：欧阳斌追问：父辈赶上改革开放、你们赶上房地产，普通人怎么不错过这样的机遇？

EN

Ouyang Bin presses further: our parents caught the reform-and-opening wave, your generation caught real estate — how does an ordinary person avoid missing the next opportunity like that?

表达提示reform and opening-up → 改革开放；catch the wave → 赶上浪潮；avoid missing the opportunity → 不错过机遇

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「宏观不可预测」

    a coin flip · call the big picture · unforecastable
    a coin flip 很口语；call 有「预判走势」之意，call the big picture 是老外常说的

  2. 想说「乱纪元里要保留选择权」

    keep exits open · stay flexible
    keep exits open 把选择权说成「留后门」，很有画面感；第二句更直白

Part 5 · 问答环节：汇率风险 · 底层能力 · 深度学习（60:10-67:00）

Q&A: FX Risk, Core Skills, and Deep Learning

情境听友问答环节：汇率风险怎么管、机构怎么做、普通人该怎么办。语域：专业答疑 + 大白话翻译。

01
中文

在讲什么：有听友问：你自己怎么做资产配置里的汇率风险？机构工作里又是怎么处理汇率波动率的？

EN

A listener asks: how do you personally handle FX risk in your asset allocation, and how did you deal with currency volatility in your institutional work?

表达提示FX / foreign-exchange risk → 汇率风险；asset allocation → 资产配置；currency volatility → 汇率波动

02
中文

关键理解：他在主权基金工作时，投资大几十种货币，结论是：汇率是世界上最难判断的价格指标，没有之一——它是两个东西的结合（如人民币对美元），一个已经难判断，两个根本判断不出来。机构做法是「不考虑汇率」：不纳入月度考核与归因；到年底结算，研究组会给出展望——但「几乎所有的对汇率的预测，是错得最离谱的」（没人预测到人民币去年跌到 7.3，一年后回到 6.8）

EN

At the sovereign fund he invested across dozens of currencies, and his conclusion: the exchange rate is the single hardest price to judge in the world, bar none. It's a combination of two things (like RMB against the dollar) — one is already hard to judge, two is hopeless. Institutions simply 'don't consider FX': it's kept out of monthly reviews and attribution; at year-end settlement the research team gives an outlook — but 'almost every forecast about the exchange rate is the one that's most badly wrong' (nobody predicted the yuan falling to 7.3, then back to 6.8 a year later).

表达提示bar none → 没有之一；RMB against the dollar → 人民币对美元；attribution → 归因；year-end outlook → 年底展望；badly wrong → 错得最离谱

03
中文

核心原则（本币需求原则）：选一个本币——在美国生活美元就是本币。配置外币资产的前提是真实需求：父母在中国养老、未来可能回国养老，才需要配人民币资产；没有生活需求，就不要对某种货币有执念判断。「日元一定会涨已经坑死我了」，但因为在日本生活，账面损失折算未来五年十年的花销就不存在

EN

The home-currency principle: pick one base currency — if you live in the US, the dollar is your base. The only premise for holding foreign-currency assets is real need: parents retiring in China, or a possible move back to retire — only then do you need yuan assets. Without a life need, don't hold a stubborn view on any currency. 'The yen will definitely rise' is a call that's killed me — but because he lives in Japan, the book loss, converted into what he'll actually spend over the next five or ten years, basically doesn't exist.

表达提示base currency → 本币；hold foreign-currency assets → 配置外币资产；a stubborn view → 执念判断；book loss → 账面损失；spread across future spending → 折算未来花销

04
中文

与其他概念的关系：机构可以用衍生品对冲汇率，但成本要占几个点收益，普通人没必要承担——「对普通人来说，你考虑太多没有意义，你考虑不清楚」

EN

Institutions can hedge with derivatives, but the cost eats a few percentage points of return — ordinary people don't need to take that on. 'For ordinary people, thinking about it too much is pointless; you can't reason it out anyway.'

表达提示hedge with derivatives → 用衍生品对冲；eat a few points of return → 占掉几个点收益；reason it out → 想清楚

05
中文

（引用）「最怕的就是你把汇率这件事情当作是你投资指标之一。」「没有人预测得到……所以就不要把这个作为你的预测之一。」

EN

'The scariest thing is treating the exchange rate as one of your investment metrics.' 'Nobody can predict it … so don't make it one of your predictions.'

表达提示investment metric → 投资指标；the scariest thing → 最怕的；make sth one of your predictions → 把它作为你的预测之一

06
中文

（引用）怎么用：问自己一句「我未来在哪里花钱？」，基于需求配置，不做货币投机

EN

How to use it: ask yourself 'where will I actually spend money in the future?', allocate based on that need, and skip currency speculation.

表达提示allocate based on need → 基于需求配置；currency speculation → 货币投机；skip → 别做

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「汇率最难预测」

    can't call a price · guaranteed to be wrong
    call 有「预判走势」的意思；第二句用夸张说法，更适合口头交流

  2. 想说「按需配置，不做货币投机」

    where your future spending is · allocate by needs
    第一句把原则口语化（模仿 put your money where your mouth is）；第二句更直白
S6

避坑总结与方法边界

Pitfalls & Boundary Notes

情境本期避坑总结：线性外推的诱惑与信息流叙事的失真。语域：总结 + 方法论。

01
中文

原因：人天然相信「一件事情能够线性外推一直走下去」——资产一直涨、红利一直有、趋势永续。这与「万物皆有周期」的世界观直接冲突

EN

Why: humans naturally believe that 'one thing can be extrapolated linearly and keep going forever' — assets keep rising, tailwinds always last, trends never end. That directly clashes with the worldview that everything runs in cycles.

表达提示extrapolate linearly → 线性外推；tailwind → 红利/顺风；everything runs in cycles → 万物皆有周期；clash with → 与…直接冲突

02
中文

解法：每次感到「这次不一样」时，把它当成周期信号而不是趋势信号；用「这幕我见过」自检替代即时反应

EN

Fix: every time you feel 'this time is different', treat it as a cycle signal rather than a trend signal. Replace your instant reaction with a self-check: 'I've seen this scene before.'

表达提示this time is different → 这次不一样；cycle signal vs trend signal → 周期信号与趋势信号；instant reaction → 即时反应

03
中文

严重程度：致命——把波动当趋势，会在最高点接盘

EN

Severity: fatal — mistake a fluctuation for a trend and you'll be left holding the bag at the peak.

表达提示mistake A for B → 把 A 当成 B；hold the bag → 高位接盘（接最后一棒）；at the peak → 在最高点

04
中文

（引用）嘉宾说法：「你不相信一件事情能够线性外推、一直往下走下去。」「投资是反人性的事情，这个是板上钉钉的。」

EN

'You don't believe one thing can be linearly extrapolated and keep going forever.' 'Investing goes against human nature — that's set in stone.'

表达提示go against human nature → 反人性；set in stone → 板上钉钉

05
中文

原因：小红书上全是「我怎么在 190 块买入 SpaceX 涨到 1000 块」的帖子；但真实世界是时代广场上 SpaceX 上市海报与批判 Grok 的抗议人偶并存

EN

Why: Xiaohongshu is full of posts like 'how I bought SpaceX at 190 and it's now 1,000'. But the real world is Times Square, where a SpaceX IPO billboard and protest puppets criticizing Grok stand side by side.

表达提示IPO → 上市；protest puppet → 抗议人偶；stand side by side → 并存

06
中文

解法：重要判断前，先补「实感」——去现场、读一手材料、见当事人，再决定要不要相信信息流里的叙事

EN

Fix: before any important judgment, add 'ground truth' — go to the scene, read primary sources, meet the people involved, and only then decide whether to believe the narrative in your feed.

表达提示ground truth → 实感/真实基准；primary source → 一手材料；narrative → 叙事；in your feed → 信息流里

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「这次不一样是最贵的四个字」

    most expensive phrase · check your cycle meter
    第一句是投资界的名言式表达；第二句把道理说成仪表盘，更生动

  2. 想说「别只信信息流，要补实感」

    buy the feed · verify the narrative with reality
    buy the feed 带一点双关的调侃感；第二句更书面正式

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

用「culture shock」说：从极度有序的社会搬到极度无序的社会。

Moving from a hyper-ordered society to a chaotic one was a real culture shock.

用「glacial」说：日本的变化极其缓慢。

Compared with China and the US, the pace of change in Japan is glacial.

用「keep my options open」说：乱纪元里我保留选择权。

In a Chaotic Era, I keep my options open — multiple skills, multiple places, multiple currencies.

用「this time is different」说：把「这次不一样」当成周期信号。

Whenever I catch myself thinking 'this time is different', I treat it as a cycle signal, not a trend.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ New York gives me a bad feeling.
✓ My first impression of New York wasn't great — the disorder was a real culture shock.

bad feeling 太模糊；用 first impression + 具体原因（disorder / culture shock）才地道。

✕ Japan's change speed is very slow.
✓ Japan moves at a glacial pace — its services barely change decade to decade.

change speed 是中式表达；英语习惯说 pace of change，形容词用 glacial 更生动准确。

✕ The exchange rate is a price that is very hard to forecast.
✓ The exchange rate is the hardest price to forecast, bar none.

very hard 程度不够；要传达「最难且没有之一」用 the hardest … bar none。

✕ I want to leave myself the right to choose.
✓ I want to keep my options open.

right to choose 是「选择权（法律/权利）」语境；「给自己留余地」英语固定说 keep one's options open。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为对一座城市的好恶来自城市本身——天气、街道、设施 → 城市观感拆三块：天气管短期，人管长期；认识了人才让纽约可爱起来
以为离开金融是某个「想通的瞬间」决定的 → 重大转型是把小冲动先执行掉，让现实慢慢给答案——没有想通时刻，只有反复拉扯
以为投资的关键是预测（预测汇率、预测宏观） → 乱纪元不追求预测，追求应变结构——多币种多地点多技能；投资是对世界观的投票

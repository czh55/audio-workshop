Why Study English in the Philippines

情境两位嘉宾开场交代背景：菲律宾语培是成熟产业，把密集课时、一对一、住宿托管、全英文环境打包成远低于欧美的价格。语域：casual 播客开场 + 产业拆解。

二、菲律宾英语教育产业与择校

The Industry and Choosing a School: Spartan vs Semi-Spartan

情境嘉宾对比斯巴达与半斯巴达两种学校的纪律强度，并介绍 CIA 与 CPLS 两所代表学校的「人设」。语域：casual 亲历体验 + 产业观察。

01
中文

在讲什么：菲律宾语言学校按纪律强度分两类。斯巴达：早 8 点到晚 10 点，全天课程加三节自习，自习前不许外出，晚归一分钟扣分、扣到退学不退学费，「条款非常不近人情」。半斯巴达：六点下课，相对宽松

EN

Philippine language schools fall into two camps based on how strict they are. Spartan schools run from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. — full-day classes plus three self-study periods, no going out before study time, and if you're even a minute late coming back, points get deducted until they expel you without refunding tuition. 'The rules are brutally cold.' Semi-Spartan schools let you out at six, much more relaxed.

表达提示two camps → 两类/两大阵营；self-study period → 自习；deduct points → 扣分；expel → 退学/开除；refund tuition → 退学费；brutally cold → 不近人情

02
中文

关键理解：斯巴达是为「雅思冲刺型选手」设计的爆肝模式，适合短期、有明确分数目标、自律强的人；但「全天候 + 全英文 + 听不懂也得坐着」对基础差、原动力不足的人是「坐牢」——嘉宾自己的原话是「轻微坐牢的感觉」

EN

The key insight: Spartan mode is built for IELTS sprinters — short-term, goal-driven, disciplined types. But all-day, all-English, sit-there-even-if-you-don't-understand is basically prison for people with weak foundations and low motivation. The guest's own words: 'a faint sense of being in jail.'

表达提示goal-driven → 目标驱动的；sit there even if you don't understand → 听不懂也得坐着；faint sense → 轻微的感觉；feel like jail → 像坐牢；all-English environment → 全英文环境

03
中文

边界：适合短期冲刺（1-2 个月）；不适合长期（读到 5-6 个月以上容易崩溃，有人读一两年，大家都会惊叹「这个人耐力怎么这么强」）

EN

The boundary: it's great for a short push of one to two months, not for the long haul. Past five or six months people start to crack — anyone who lasts a year or two gets 'how do you have that much stamina?'

表达提示for the long haul → 长期（坚持）；start to crack → 开始崩溃/撑不住；last → 坚持/熬过；stamina → 耐力

EN

Quote: 'My first reaction was resistance, even though I really wanted to improve my English.' — the guest, a former teacher, instinctively recoiled at the idea of going back to school.

EN

How it connects: Spartan versus semi-Spartan is the first filter in choosing a school. It decides your schedule intensity, your freedom, and your mental budget for how much pressure you can take.

表达提示first filter → 第一道筛选；schedule intensity → 日程强度；mental budget → 心理承受预算；how much pressure you can take → 能承受多大压力

06
中文

在讲什么：两家学校代表两种极端「人设」。CIA：宿务硬件排名第一的新校区，五星级酒店式装修，健身房全宿务最顶尖（海景落地窗），泳池、KTV、台球桌、大头贴机一应俱全，「周末被禁足也不会无聊」；学生 500-600 人，营销做得好，韩国老板还配套胶囊酒店「把学生出校园后的生活也赚走」。CPLS：宿务第一家语言学校，年代久远，宿舍昏暗压抑、空调噪音大，「像城中村待拆楼」，市中心位置

EN

The two schools represent two extreme personalities. CIA: Cebu's top-ranked new campus for facilities — five-star hotel decor, the best gym in Cebu with ocean-view floor-to-ceiling windows, plus a pool, KTV, pool table, and photo booth, so 'even a weekend lockdown wouldn't be boring.' It has 500 to 600 students and strong marketing, and the Korean owner even runs a capsule hotel next door, 'squeezing money out of students' lives outside campus too.' CPLS: Cebu's very first language school, old and worn — dim, gloomy dorms, noisy AC, 'like a building slated for demolition in an old neighborhood,' right in the city center.

表达提示represent two extreme personalities → 代表两种极端人设；floor-to-ceiling windows → 落地窗；one-stop amenities → 设施一应俱全；lockdown / be confined to campus → 被禁足；slated for demolition → 待拆的；gloomy → 昏暗压抑

    night and day 形容天壤之别；condemned building 呼应「待拆楼」，很形象

Costs, Courses, and the Class-Swapping Hack

情境拆解学费构成、课程梯度（ESL→雅思→商务→NPMC），以及「一周换一次课表、指定老师」的换课攻略。语域：casual 攻略分享 + 信息整理。

01
中文

在讲什么：学费约 1.3 万人民币/月（CPLS），CIA 贵 1-2 千，通常含住宿（部分含餐），是「语培 + 住宿 + 管理」的打包价。课程梯度：ESL 生活英语（约 7 节/天）→ 雅思班（9 节 + 3 节自习 = 12 节）→ 商务英语 → NPMC 演讲自信表达课

EN

Here's the money part: tuition runs about 13,000 RMB a month at CPLS, and CIA costs 1,000 to 2,000 more — usually including housing, sometimes meals. It's a bundle price for classes plus room plus management. The course ladder: ESL everyday English at about seven classes a day, IELTS prep at nine classes plus three study periods, business English, and NPMC, a public-speaking confidence course.

表达提示run about … a month → 大约每月…；bundle price → 打包价；course ladder / curriculum track → 课程梯度；public speaking → 公开演讲/演讲表达

表达提示grind for a score → 为分数拼命；test-taking technique → 应试技巧；ease in with … → 先用…过渡；the safer path → 更稳的路径

表达提示only care about → 只看重；doesn't speak like a human → 不说人话（戏谑说法）；select and filter → 选拔；test-taking technique → 应试技巧

四、实际体验与收获

The Real Experience and What They Gained

情境嘉宾复盘一个月后的口语突破、仍存的阅读障碍，以及学校里遇到的各国同学。语域：casual 亲历复盘 + 人物观察。

01
中文

在讲什么：来之前「甚至对着 ChatGPT 都开不了口」，一个月后能把英语当成第二种语言自如表达——「基本上现在他们在说话，我都能听懂，我也能回复」

EN

Before coming, she 'couldn't even open her mouth to ChatGPT.' A month later she could use English as a second language, comfortably — 'basically now when people talk, I understand it all, and I can reply.'

表达提示couldn't even open my mouth → 开不了口；as a second language → 作为第二种语言；comfortably → 自如地；understand it all → 全都能听懂

02
中文

关键理解：口语突破的本质不是词汇量暴涨，而是「说出来」的心理阻力被高频环境磨掉了。但阅读仍是障碍：看到英文会直接翻译、连瞄都不想瞄，所以还想啃雅思

EN

The key insight: the real breakthrough wasn't exploding vocabulary — it was the psychological barrier to speaking that got worn down by constant high-frequency practice. Reading is still a wall: her eyes hit English and her brain auto-translates, and she doesn't even want to glance at it, which is why she's still thinking about tackling IELTS.

表达提示psychological barrier → 心理阻力；wear down → 磨掉；high-frequency practice → 高频练习；auto-translate → 直接翻译；tackle → 啃（攻克）

03

EN

The boundary: killing the shame equals boosting speaking confidence — it's not the same as raising your test score. Hard skills like reading and writing need a different path, which is exactly why they both later considered taking IELTS.

表达提示kill the shame → 消除羞耻；boost confidence → 提升自信；hard skills → 硬能力（阅读写作等）；a different path → 另外的路径

表达提示by the later stage → 到了后期；express and communicate → 表达和沟通；treat A as B → 把 A 当作 B；completely comfortably → 完全自如

五、安全与文化观察

Safety and Culture: What They Saw on the Ground

情境讨论「签证难办、危险」的焦虑 vs 实地感受，以及菲律宾老师乐观「懒散」的国民性格及其地理宗教根源。语域：casual 安全提醒 + 文化观察。

01
中文

在讲什么：来之前最大的心理障碍是「签证难办」和「危险」——朋友天天转发枪击案、绑架案。实地：问过在学校工作近一年的中国经理、长居的中介、中文潜水教练和本地老师，结合自身感受——「比来之前国内渲染的和自己想象中的要安全很多」，没感受到排华或歧视

EN

Before coming, the biggest mental blockers were 'getting a visa is a pain' and 'it's dangerous' — friends kept forwarding headlines about shootings and kidnappings. On the ground: she asked a Chinese manager who'd worked at the school for almost a year, a long-term resident agent, a Chinese-speaking dive instructor, and local teachers. Combined with her own feelings — 'it's much safer than what the Chinese media made it sound and what I'd imagined' — she felt no anti-Chinese sentiment or discrimination.

表达提示mental blocker → 心理障碍；a pain → 麻烦/难办；on the ground → 实地；make sth sound … → 把…渲染成…；anti-Chinese sentiment → 排华情绪

02
中文

关键理解：安全与否取决于你把自己置于什么环境：深夜独行、露财逛市区、缺乏风险预判，放哪个城市都危险。要警惕的硬现实：飞车抢劫、偷窃概率更高，持枪合法（保安抽屉里的枪、商超开包查枪）

EN

The key insight: safety depends on what environments you put yourself in. Walking alone late at night, flashing money around downtown, having no sense of risk — that's dangerous in any city. The hard realities to watch out for: snatch-and-grab robbery and petty theft are more common, and guns are legal — security guards keep guns in their desk drawers, and supermarkets check your bag for weapons at the entrance.

表达提示depend on … → 取决于…；flash money around → 露财；snatch-and-grab robbery → 飞车抢劫；petty theft → 小偷小摸；risk awareness → 风险预判；watch out for → 要警惕

03
中文

（引用）原文依据：「总的来说是没有这么吓人，但是肯定是要基于你本身有一定的那种风险预判意识，不要把自己置于那种危险系数很高的环境中。」

EN

Quote: 'Overall it's not that scary, but you definitely need a basic sense of risk awareness — don't put yourself in high-danger environments.'

表达提示not that scary → 没那么吓人；risk awareness → 风险意识；high-danger environment → 危险系数很高的环境；put yourself in … → 把自己置于…

04
中文

（引用）怎么用：行前把现金分开放、买旅行保险、夜间不出门、不去治安差的区域；把「概率事件」交给常识，而不是被新闻吓到放弃整趟行程

EN

How to use it: split your cash into separate spots, buy travel insurance, don't go out at night, and stay out of bad neighborhoods. Leave 'probability events' to common sense — don't let scary headlines talk you out of the whole trip.

表达提示split your cash → 把现金分开放；bad neighborhood → 治安差的区域；probability event → 概率事件；talk sb out of sth → 劝某人放弃某事

05
中文

在讲什么：老师会在课间五分钟走廊上即兴高歌合唱，「甚至厕所排队也想唱就唱」；老师自己也承认大部分菲律宾人「懒散」，一个家庭五六个孩子只靠一个人赚钱，升职加薪但要多担责任一般都会拒绝

EN

About the culture: teachers burst into impromptu sing-alongs in the hallway during the five-minute break — 'they'll even sing while queuing for the bathroom.' The teachers themselves admit most Filipinos are 'laid-back.' A family of five or six kids is often supported by one breadwinner, and people usually turn down a promotion with a raise if it means taking on more responsibility.

表达提示burst into an impromptu sing-along → 即兴高歌合唱；laid-back → 懒散/随性；breadwinner → 养家糊口的人；turn down a promotion → 拒绝升职；take on more responsibility → 多担责任

06
中文

关键理解：这种性格的底层可能是地理与宗教：天主教国家 + 地震台风洪灾频发，「努力了反正也没什么用」，于是把精力放在爱和关系上；家庭地位极高，「家庭快乐他们就快乐」

EN

The deeper story might be geography and religion: it's a Catholic country, and earthquakes, typhoons, and floods are constant — 'working hard won't change much anyway,' so energy goes into love and relationships. Family sits at the very top; 'when the family is happy, they're happy.'

表达提示the deeper story → 底层原因；Catholic country → 天主教国家；constant → 频发的；won't change much anyway → 努力了反正也没用；family sits at the very top → 家庭地位极高

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「安全取决于你把自己放在什么环境」

    less about A, more about B · stay out of · risk drops
    less about A, more about B 是澄清因果的常用结构；risk drops 口语自然

  2. 想说「菲律宾人懒散但乐天」

    to the point of · easygoing · laid-back
    to the point of 表示程度，如 easygoing to the point of seeming lazy；laid-back 是评价性格的常用词

六、对比分析：菲律宾 vs 国内 vs 欧美

Philippines vs China vs the West: Where Each Route Wins

情境嘉宾对比「提分」与「练口语」两条路线的最优解，指出它们不在同一个地方。语域：casual 结论复盘 + 路线对比。

01
中文

嘉宾亲测结论：提分这件事，「B站一个免费视频可能比这里老师教一个月更有用」；中国人在应试提分上世界范围内有优势

EN

The guest's firsthand conclusion on score improvement: 'a free video on Bilibili might be more useful than a month of classes here.' When it comes to test-score gains, Chinese learners have a worldwide advantage.

表达提示firsthand conclusion → 亲测结论；free video → 免费视频；score gains / raise your score → 提分；have a worldwide advantage → 在世界范围内有优势

02
中文

为什么重要：两条路线的最优解不在同一个地方。消除口语羞耻、敢开口、能聊天——菲律宾沉浸式最强；6 分左右想在 1 个月内快速提分——回国找机构（嘉宾点名学为贵）或看免费应试视频更快。把「提分」目标放进菲律宾，是把钱花错了地方

EN

Why it matters: the best answer for each route lives in a different place. For killing speaking shame, daring to open your mouth, and just chatting — the Philippines' immersion is unbeatable. For jumping from around Band 6 to a higher score within a month — a Chinese test-prep agency, like Guixue, which the guest named, or free exam videos will get you there faster. Taking a 'raise my score' goal to the Philippines is spending your money in the wrong place.

表达提示lives in a different place → 不在同一个地方；dare to open your mouth → 敢开口；immersion → 沉浸式；test-prep agency → 培训机构；Band 6 → 雅思 6 分（band score 计分）

03
中文

适用边界：如果你底子差到连开口都不敢（如嘉宾「高考 59 分」），那菲律宾的「消除羞耻」就是提分的前提——先解决敢不敢，再谈分不分的

EN

The boundary: if your foundation is so weak that you can't even speak up — the guest scored 59 on her gaokao English — then the Philippines' shame-killing is the prerequisite for raising your score. Solve 'dare you?' first, then talk about 'how many points?'

表达提示speak up → 开口；prerequisite → 前提；gaokao → 高考；solve X first, then talk about Y → 先解决…再谈…

04
中文

（引用）「如果你有很具体的提分的这个诉求的话，我会觉得这个钱花得不是特别值得。」

EN

Quote: 'If you have a very concrete goal of raising your score, I'd say this money isn't especially well spent.'

表达提示concrete goal → 具体诉求；raise your score → 提分；well spent → 花得值

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「不同目标的最优解在不同地方」

    point to · test-prep · go immerse
    point to 表示「指向」；immerse 直接用作动词（go immerse）很口语

  2. 想说「先解决敢不敢开口，再谈分数」

    fix the courage problem · before chasing · can't even say a sentence
    chase a score 指追分，很形象；fix … before … 是「先解决…再…」的万能结构

The Final Verdict: Who Should Go, Who Shouldn't

情境给出适合/不适合人群清单、一句话总结与三句嘉宾金句收尾。语域：casual 总结 + 金句回顾。

01
中文

适合来菲律宾学英语：基础很差、讨厌英语、想感受新教学方式的人；5 分左右想冲到 6 分的人；想短期密集开口、消除口语羞耻的人；想要「游学 + 潜水 + 度假」综合体验的人

表达提示weak foundation → 基础差；sit around Band 5 → 5 分左右；push up to → 冲到；intensive speaking practice → 密集开口；combo experience → 综合体验

02
中文

不适合：雅思 6 分左右、想在 1 个月内快速提分的人（回国报机构更快）；完全无法忍受纪律与强度的人；计划长期（5-6 个月以上）纯应试学习的人；对生活品质有强要求、又没看过校区的人

用「bundle」说：菲律宾语培把课时、住宿和全英文环境打包成一个价格。

Philippine language schools bundle class hours, housing, and a fully English-only environment into one price.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The Philippines is very cheap.
✓ The Philippines offers the same package at a much lower price — intensive hours and one-on-one classes for a fraction of the cost.

cheap 带「廉价低质」联想；谈性价比用 a fraction of the cost / much lower price / good value 更准确地道。

✕ I went to the Philippines to improve my oral English.
✓ I went to the Philippines to get more speaking practice and kill my fear of opening my mouth.

oral English 很「中式考试腔」；口语场景更常说 speaking practice，且点出具体目的（消除开口恐惧）更地道。

✕ The Spartan school is very strict, so I don't like it.
✓ Spartan mode is built for IELTS sprinters — intense and disciplined, and honestly a bit jail-like if you're not ready for it.

只说 strict 太干瘪；用 be built for / jail-like 表达「为冲刺设计」「坐牢感」这些播客里的具体语境才到位。

✕ The teacher teaches me IELTS.
✓ My teacher walks me through IELTS test-taking techniques.

teach me IELTS 是直译生硬；英文习惯说 teaches IELTS（教科目）+ walk sb through（带练），更自然。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为去菲律宾学英语是欧美留学的便宜平替 → 贵不贵是次要，关键是它卖的「组合拳」——密集课时 + 一对一 + 全英文，把沉浸感的单价打了下来
以为斯巴达越严越好，选最严的学校就能学好 → 斯巴达只适合短期冲刺型选手；对基础弱、动力不足的人就是「坐牢」，择校要先算心理承受预算
以为提升英语 = 报班花钱，花钱多进步就快 → 不同目标的最优解在不同地方：开口自信去菲律宾、提分回国、长期融入去欧美——先想清楚要买哪一种

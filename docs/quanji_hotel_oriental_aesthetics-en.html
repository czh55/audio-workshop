避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Quanji's target users are the workers who are sent out to work on business trips by the company.
✓ Quanji's target users are road warriors sent out by their departments.

road warrior（出差打工人）是地道名词；冗长的定语从句可以简化成一个短语。

✕ The light of the hotel is very dark and comfortable.
✓ The hotel arranges darkness on purpose — dim lighting that feels soothing, not gloomy.

dark 带负面联想；「布置暗」要用 arrange darkness，且强调 dim（柔暗）而非 dark（昏暗）。

✕ Quanji's aesthetic is very traditional Chinese style.
✓ Quanji reads as Chinese, Japanese, or Korean to foreigners — it's a Chinese-character-culture temperament, not heavy national style.

直译「中式风」太重；本期核心是白粥式符号，要说明它不是浓重国风。

✕ Ji Qi made the hotel have no theme, no center and no boundary.
✓ Ji Qi set Quanji's 'no theme, no center, no boundaries' principle — absence becomes its way of penetrating everywhere.

no theme, no center, no boundaries 是品牌专用短语；补一句「以无渗透」才说透哲学；made + have 结构很中式。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为出差酒店就是要豪华舒适 → 出差打工人要的是高效充电与秩序感——中性、留白、不打扰才是体面
以为东方美学=浓重国风 → 全季的白粥式符号走的是汉字文化圈气质——日/韩/中都能成立，轻盈流通
以为做大做强就是好事 → 做大=成为行业标准=变成平均值；全季接受这种「平均」，因为国民酒店本身就是消费民主

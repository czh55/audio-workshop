中文

EN

The core mechanism: Anthropic released 11 function plugins on its Cowork platform — covering legal, finance, sales, and data analysis — calling out nearly every profitable line of traditional SaaS, one by one. 二、SaaS 存活的三条护城河

Three Moats for SaaS Survival

情境数据护城河与复杂流程护城河，以及多步流程错误率累积的数学直觉。语域：formal 商业分析。

01
中文

核心机制：数据沉淀在特定领域，通用大模型（OpenAI、DeepSeek、Google Gemini）做不出同等质量的 Agent，因为缺乏你的数据

EN

The core mechanism: when data settles in a specific domain, general large models like OpenAI, DeepSeek, and Google Gemini can't produce agents of the same quality, because they lack your data.

表达提示data moat → 数据护城河；settle in a domain → 沉淀在某个领域；lack your data → 缺乏你的数据

02
中文

表达提示strongest barrier → 最强壁垒；the big players → 大厂

中文

04
中文

核心机制：传统软件沉淀了足够复杂的流程（如 20 步 ERP 流程），智能体是概率模型，每步 1-2% 出错率，20 步连乘后错误率不可接受

EN

表达提示probabilistic model → 概率模型；error rate → 出错率；multiply → 连乘

05
中文

关键理解：这给了传统 ERP/SaaS 公司约 3 年「救赎区」完成自我革命——但在今天 AI 变化速度下，3 年可能都嫌长

EN

The key insight: this gives traditional ERP and SaaS companies a roughly 3-year 'redemption zone' to complete their self-revolution — but at today's AI speed, 3 years may already be too long.

表达提示redemption zone → 救赎区；self-revolution → 自我革命；at today's speed → 以今天的速度

06
中文

（引用）数学直觉：0.99^20 ≈ 82%，0.98^20 ≈ 67%——多步流程的累积错误率让企业在容错度要求高的场景难以接受纯 Agent

EN

The math intuition: 0.99 to the 20th power is about 82%, and 0.98 to the 20th is about 67% — the cumulative error rate of multi-step processes makes it hard for enterprises in high-tolerance scenarios to accept pure agents.

表达提示cumulative error rate → 累积错误率；fault tolerance → 容错度；pure agents → 纯智能体

五、RaaS 商业模式：按结果收费

The RaaS Model: Charging by Results

情境SaaS/PaaS/RaaS 的比喻对比、市场规模差距与百融的定位。语域：formal 商业模式分析。

01
中文

核心机制：SaaS = 拎包入住的精装房；PaaS = 毛坯房+工具箱自己装修；RaaS（Results as a Service）= 直接交付结果，按岗位/按件/按整体外包收费

EN

The core mechanism: SaaS is a move-in-ready furnished apartment; PaaS is a bare-shell unit with a toolset for you to renovate; RaaS, Results as a Service, directly delivers results — charging by position, by piece, or by overall outsourcing.

表达提示move-in-ready → 拎包入住；bare-shell unit → 毛坯房；outsourcing → 外包

02
中文

关键理解：RaaS 市场规模远超 SaaS，不是一倍两倍而是几十倍——尤其在中国，SaaS 不能端对端交付结果，企业付费意愿弱；RaaS 直接交付岗位或结果，价值可量化

EN

The key insight: RaaS's market is far bigger than SaaS — not by two or three times but by dozens of times. Especially in China, where SaaS can't deliver end-to-end results and enterprises are weak on willingness to pay, RaaS delivers a position or a result directly, and the value can be quantified.

表达提示end-to-end → 端到端；willingness to pay → 付费意愿；quantified value → 价值可量化

03
中文

（引用）与 SaaS 的区别：SaaS 卖工具使用权，RaaS 卖「这个活干完了」

EN

The difference from SaaS: SaaS sells the right to use a tool; RaaS sells 'the job being done.'

表达提示the right to use → 使用权；the job being done → 活干完了

04
中文

OpenAI 的 GPTs 本质就是 Agent Store，但一年都没再提——没搞起来。2C 的 Agent Store（如 Claude Code）大厂会做，但 2B 企业级 Agent Store 他们没想清楚

EN

OpenAI's GPTs is essentially an Agent Store, but they didn't mention it for a year — it never took off. For consumer Agent Stores like Claude Code, big companies will build them; but for B-end enterprise Agent Stores, they haven't figured it out.

表达提示take off → 搞起来；figure it out → 想清楚；Agent Store → 智能体商店

05
中文

百融定位：不做 AGI/通用大模型，但做领域专属大模型（95% 问题用专属模型，边角 case 路由给通用模型）+ Agent Builder + Agent Store 开放生态

EN

BaiRong's positioning: it doesn't do AGI or general large models, but it builds domain-specific large models — 95% of problems are handled by its specialized models, with edge cases routed to general models — plus an Agent Builder and an open Agent Store ecosystem.

表达提示domain-specific model → 领域专属模型；edge case → 边角情况；route to → 路由给

06
中文

适用边界：Anthropic 以 2B 生产力大模型为主，做了 Agent Store 也不意味着垄断——垂直领域仍需 IAV（独立智能体供应商）

EN

The boundary: Anthropic focuses on B-end productivity large models, and even with an Agent Store it doesn't mean monopoly — vertical domains still need IAV, independent agent vendors.

表达提示monopoly → 垄断；independent agent vendor（IAV）→ 独立智能体供应商；vertical domain → 垂直领域

How Traditional Enterprises Survive

The fix: most traditional enterprises can't build it themselves — they only care about three things: quantity, quality, and cost. They find a partner who provides agents directly and pay by results, without even needing employee training.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Software industry in China is only 4% of America's output value.
✓ Software as a product industry in China is worth only about 4% of America's.

「软件作为产品产业」要说 software as a product industry 才准确；价值表达用 worth / output value。

✕ The agent has 1-2% error every step, so after 20 steps it is very bad.
✓ Each agent step has a 1-2% error rate — after 20 steps, errors compound to an unacceptable level.

表达累积误差用 compound；very bad 太笼统，用 unacceptable / untenable 更精准。

✕ The new king has no money, the old king has money, so the old king is afraid.
✓ The old king has existing revenue to protect, so it dare not disrupt itself.

核心是「存量利益」不是「有钱没钱」；用 existing revenue to protect 才说透。

✕ The silicon employee can write the report and the human sign name.
✓ Silicon employees draft the reports; senior partners review, sign, and take responsibility.

sign name 是中式表达，用 sign off / take responsibility；且要体现「审核+担责」两个动作。

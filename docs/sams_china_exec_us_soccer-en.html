01
中文

世界杯特调：足球为何在美国越来越受欢迎

World Cup Special: Why Soccer Keeps Winning America Over

情境足球在美国从不到 2% 升到第三大受欢迎运动，拉美裔人口增长是关键变量。语域：formal 数据解读 + 播客点评。

01
中文

盖洛普统计：1937–2004 年仅不到 2% 美国人把足球列为最爱运动。经济学人最新调查显示超 10% 美国人最爱足球，略超棒球，仅次于橄榄球和篮球，升至第三位

EN

Gallup data: from 1937 to 2004, fewer than 2 percent of Americans named soccer their favorite sport. A recent Economist survey shows more than 10 percent now do, edging past baseball to rank third behind American football and basketball.

表达提示Gallup → 盖洛普；edge past → 略超；rank third → 升至第三位

02

EN

A Johns Hopkins professor argues: American football, baseball, and basketball are firmly entrenched; Americans favor high-scoring sports and sports invented at home, while soccer has few goals and even draws, so it stayed marginalized for a long time.

表达提示firmly entrenched → 地位稳固；high-scoring → 高分；marginalized → 边缘化

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Yum China bought the Pizza Hut brand of China.
✓ Yum China bought the brand ownership for mainland China, not the global brand.

「拿下中国品牌所有权」不等于买品牌本身；用 ownership for mainland China 才准确。

✕ Sam's Club changed two high leaders.
✓ Sam's Club China replaced both its chairman and its chief purchasing officer.

high leader 是中式表达；executive / chairman / chief purchasing officer 才是地道职位说法。

✕ Football is very hot in America now.
✓ Soccer's popularity has even surpassed baseball in America.

football 在美国指橄榄球，足球要说 soccer；very hot 也不如 surpassed 表达准确。

✕ SpaceX used 60 billion dollars to buy Cursor.
✓ SpaceX bought Cursor with 60 billion dollars in stock — a stock deal, not cash.

是用股票而非现金收购；用 in stock / stock deal 强调交易结构。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为特许经营就是加盟开店 → 中国运营商正逐步掌握品牌所有权与主导权，省下的特许费投入本土化
以为 AI 企业烧钱做 C 端就够了 → 豆包的高日活低收入 vs Seedance 的 B 端变现，说明 C 端流量不等于盈利
以为美国人不看足球 → 拉美裔人口近乎翻番，把足球推上了美国第三大最受欢迎运动的位置

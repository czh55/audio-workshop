避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The mosquito bites me and it becomes a very big bag.
✓ The midge bite swells into a large welt, then oozes for weeks.

蚊子/蠓虫要区分 species；「包块」用 welt/swelling，不是 bag（袋子）；oozes 描述化脓流水更准确。

✕ We date at his office building and watch night view.
✓ Our dates were literally at his company — touring the office tower and watching the night view.

「去公司约会」在英文里要用 literally 强调反差感；tour the office 比 date at the building 更自然。

✕ Shenzhen's tolerance is that nobody manages you.
✓ Shenzhen's tolerance means nobody scrutinizes your background or ambitions.

「没人在意」不是「没人管理」；scrutinize（审视）或 care 更贴切，表达自由而非失管。

✕ The food at this restaurant is definitely not delicious.
✓ The tissue brand on the table is a dead giveaway that the food isn't worth it.

直接断言 not delicious 太绝对；用 dead giveaway 表达「民间避雷信号」，语气更符合分享语境。

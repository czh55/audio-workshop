避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Washing my face more times will clean it better.
✓ Washing more often doesn't mean cleaner skin — it strips your barrier and triggers more oil.

「过度清洁」英语对应 over-clean / strip the barrier，而不是 simple 表达；强调越洗越糟用 make things worse。

✕ I want to balance my skin's water and oil.
✓ I used to chase 'water-oil balance', but the real issue is a damaged barrier.

「水油平衡」是中文护肤圈说法，英文没有对应概念；地道说法是 barrier repair / oily on the outside, dry on the inside。

✕ SPF 100 is twice as strong as SPF 50.
✓ SPF 50 already blocks about 98% of UVB — SPF 100 adds almost nothing.

SPF 数值非线性叠加；SPF30→50 只多 2%，应说 blocks about 98%，而不是 twice as strong。

✕ I put an ice cube directly on my sunburned face.
✓ Don't ice a sunburn — use lukewarm saline or a cool towel instead.

晒伤直敷冰块会冻伤毛细血管（frostbite），正确急救是 cool compress/凉毛巾冷敷；用 make it worse 表达反效果。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为洗得越勤、越用力，脸越干净 → 温和且彻底——一天最多两次，洗掉脏东西即可，别把保护性油脂也洗光
以为「水油平衡」，疯狂补水就能压住油 → 缺水和出油没关系——外油内干是屏障受损，先修屏障，而不是狂喷水
以为防晒指数越高越好，SPF 100 最安心 → ABC 原则：A 避开、B 硬防晒、C 防晒霜兜底——SPF 50 以上几乎无增量，堆高反而闷痘

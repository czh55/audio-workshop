Hosts Hailu and Jingya round up the 2026 summer camera releases: Fujifilm's 40th-anniversary disposables, Sony's RX10 V and FX5, Panasonic's LUMIX L10, and the rumored Nikon fixed-lens camera. Their core take: in a camera-market winter, the "bridge camera" has become every maker's only lifeline, and new releases look less like innovation and more like feeding the AI — just to stay in the conversation. The only thing actually worth buying, they argue, is the pay-per-shot disposable film experience.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The new camera is more expensive than before 3000 yuan.
✓ The new camera is 3,000 yuan pricier than before.

中式语序；「贵3000」用 is 3,000 yuan pricier than。

✕ This camera can shoot all things but doesn't want to change lens.

混用主语；「不用换镜头」用 without changing lenses。

✕ The price is compared to a manicure.

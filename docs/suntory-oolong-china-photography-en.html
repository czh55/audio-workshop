Suntory Oolong's 30-Year Chinese Visual Love Affair: A Dreamland and Women's Growth

From 1984 to 2011, every Suntory oolong tea commercial was shot in China, used Chinese models, and told Chinese stories — yet was shown only to Japanese audiences. The program walks through six phases: the Chinese-style illustration period of 1984-1987, the tea-ism and Chinese symbols period of 1987-1990, the peach-blossom paradise period after Ueda Yoshihiko joined in 1991-1994, Chinese women going global in 1995-2005, the sisters' daily life period of 2006-2009, and the Chinese celebrity period of 2010-2011. The core theme: how one Japanese brand shaped a generation of Japanese impressions of China, through Ueda's photography philosophy of 'distance' and the brand spirit of always looking up to something.

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

用「settle for」说：拍不了实景，只能退而求其次用插画。

They couldn't shoot on location, so they settled for illustration.

用「sense of distance」说：上田的照片有独特的距离感。

Ueda's photos have a distinctive sense of distance.

用「run through」说：中国元素贯穿全部阶段。

Chinese elements ran through every single phase.

His standard of beauty overlapped with what Chinese people dreamed of.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The commercial uses China real location.
✓ The ads were shot on location in China.

「中国实景」是 on location in China；commercial 是名词形式，不能直接做定语。

✕ The models are from dancing institute, very tall and thin.
✓ The models came from dance academies — tall and slender.

舞蹈学院是 dance academy；tall and slender 比 tall and thin 更正面（thin 常带贬义）。

✕ The ads have Chinese dialogue but no Japanese letters.
✓ The ads use Chinese dialogue with no Japanese subtitles.

「字幕」是 subtitles，不是 letters（字母/文字）；屏幕字幕语境必须用 subtitle。

✕ The product is tied strong with China.
✓ The product was strongly tied to China.

tie to 用介词 to 而非 with；副词 strongly 修饰 tied，放在其前面。

Taipei's Witch House: Thirty Years of Indie Music and Women's Spaces

From a shoebox storefront beneath a women's bookstore beside NTU to the birthplace of Taiwan's indie music, the Witch House was never a carefully planned project: founder Peng Yujing was a pastry apprentice who opened the shop half-willingly; a truckload of gear rescued from a shut-down livehouse accidentally turned it into a stage; and noise complaints forced the 'unplugged' rule that made its name. Thirty years on, it's like an old banyan tree — not the flashiest thing around, but always there.

A Chaotic Beginning: From a Cooking Dream to the Witch House

情境The hosts set the scene: Taiwan lifted martial law in 1987, and by the early nineties society was restless for an outlet of expression — underground bands and rock livehouses were popping up. Then they introduce founder Peng Yujing, a sociology grad who couldn't find work and dreamed of running her own restaurant. 语域：casual storytelling + 历史背景讲解。

中文

核心机制：1987 年台湾正式解除戒严令，结束长达 38 年的军事管制。像一个人憋了很久没说话、终于得到可以喘气的机会，九十年代初整个社会蠢蠢欲动，都在寻找表达的出口——地下乐团出现，西朗、人狗、蚂蚁等为摇滚乐迷打造的 Livehouse 也慢慢出现

EN

In 1987, Taiwan officially lifted martial law, ending 38 years of military rule. Like someone who hasn't been allowed to speak for ages finally getting room to breathe, society in the early nineties was itching to move — everyone was looking for an outlet for expression. Underground bands appeared, and livehouses built for rock fans, like Xilang, Rengou, and Mayi, slowly came into being.

二、阿峰与 Scum：摇滚噪音付出的代价

A-Feng and Scum: The Price of Rock & Roll Noise

情境A-Feng of the indie band Boney Flesh opened Scum, a performance space near NTU with a rare rule: bands had to play original songs. Loud rock noise brought neighbor complaints, police fines, and finally the venue's death — but the borrowed gear became the Witch House's first stage. 语域：narrative storytelling + 人物故事。

01
中文

核心机制：独立乐团骨皮肉成员阿峰（不是苏打绿的阿峰）在台大附近创立展演空间 Scum。与其他允许全翻唱的 Livehouse 不同，Scum 几乎是最早一批规定「上台乐团必须演奏原创曲目」的地方

EN

A-Feng, a member of the indie band Boney Flesh — not the A-Feng from Sodagreen — founded Scum, a performance space near NTU. Unlike other livehouses that allowed full cover sets, Scum was one of the first venues to require bands on stage to play original songs.

表达提示cover set / cover song → 翻唱曲目；original song → 原创曲目；performance space / venue → 展演空间；band member → 乐团成员

02
中文

关键理解：「必须原创」埋下了台湾独立乐团发展的种子。但当时地下乐团多演摇滚，超高声贝的噪音引来居民投诉抗议，Scum 吃了大量警察罚单——阿峰把赚的倒贴进去，还赔掉一台奔驰，最终倒闭

EN

The key insight: that 'originals only' rule planted the seed of Taiwan's indie scene. But back then underground bands mostly played rock, and the ear-splitting volume drew neighbor complaints and police fines. A-Feng sank his own money in, lost a Mercedes in the process, and the venue finally went under.

表达提示plant the seed of → 埋下…的种子；ear-splitting → 震耳欲聋的；police fine → 警察罚单；go under → 倒闭；sink money in → 把钱倒贴进去

03
中文

典型场景：当「理念正确」撞上「物理现实」（噪音扰民），没有妥协机制的理念会被成本压垮——这是女巫店后来最重要的反面教材

EN

Typical scenario: when a 'righteous principle' collides with physical reality — noise complaints, in this case — an idea with no room for compromise gets crushed by cost. This is the cautionary tale the Witch House later took most seriously.

表达提示collide with reality → 与现实相撞；cautionary tale → 反面教材；no room for compromise → 没有妥协空间；get crushed by cost → 被成本压垮

三、女巫店三原则：被现状逼出来的定调 [08:26]

The Witch House's Three Rules: Principles Forced by Reality

情境The Witch House sits in a residential area near NTU, on the ground floor of a space too small to soundproof — it couldn't copy Scum's full underground-band model. So Peng made three decisions: unplugged, originals only, and female-first. The constraints weren't flaws; they were reframed as advantages. 语域：商业/定位分析 + 讲故事。

01
中文

女巫店位于台大附近的居民区、店面在一楼且空间小、很难装隔音设备；即使让 Scum 原班人马操持，也没法把地下乐团整套模式复制进这个新开不久的小空间，否则照样吃罚单关门。同期又出现 Vibes 等阳刚摇滚风格的 Livehouse。彭郁晶由此探索最适合女巫店的风格，做了一个三点决定：

EN

The Witch House sits in a residential area near NTU, on the ground floor, in a space so small that installing soundproofing was nearly impossible. Even with Scum's original crew running it, the full underground-band model couldn't be copied into this brand-new little room — or they'd get fined and shut down just the same. At the same time, more macho rock livehouses like Vibes were popping up. So Peng searched for the style that fit the Witch House best and made a three-point decision.

表达提示residential area → 居民区；soundproofing → 隔音设备；copy / transplant a model → 复制模式；macho → 阳刚的；get fined and shut down → 吃罚单关门

02
中文

关键理解：三条原则互相咬合成一个系统：不插电解决「能不能活」，原创解决「活成什么样」，女性优先解决「谁来定义」。限制没有被当成缺陷，而是被转念成可利用的优势

EN

The key insight: the three principles interlock into one system — unplugged solves 'whether we can survive', original music solves 'what we become', and female-first solves 'who gets to define us.' The constraints weren't treated as flaws; they were reframed into an advantage to be exploited.

表达提示interlock → 互相咬合；reframe → 重新框定/转念；an advantage to be exploited → 可利用的优势；constraint vs flaw → 限制 vs 缺陷

03
中文

（引用）原文依据：「后来她做了一个决定，包括三点……这些其实都不必成为限制，反而可以成为一种可利用的优势。」

EN

Quote from the show: 'Later she made a decision, including three points... none of these have to be limits — they can actually become an advantage you can use.'

表达提示None of these have to be limits → 这些都不必成为限制；turn into an advantage → 转化为优势

04
中文

核心机制：与其说 Unplugged 是一种音乐立场，不如说是担心邻居嫌吵、被现状逼出来的妥协。但它同时是鼓励——鼓励表演者在摇滚的喧嚣之外，去寻找「你和观众近距离时，怎么让他更投入」的技巧与法门

EN

Unplugged was less a musical stance than a compromise forced by worrying the neighbors would complain. But at the same time it was an encouragement — an invitation to look beyond rock's loudness and find the craft of getting the audience more involved when you're performing up close.

表达提示less A than B → 与其说是A不如说是B；stance / position → 立场；get sb involved → 让某人更投入；up close → 近距离

05
中文

关键理解：妥协不是退让，而是换一条路竞争。噪音限制把竞争维度从「音量」切换到了「亲密感与技巧」——这反而成为女巫店的差异化优势

EN

The key insight: compromise isn't retreating — it's competing on a different track. The noise restriction moved the competition from 'volume' to 'intimacy and skill', and that became the Witch House's differentiation advantage.

表达提示retreat → 退让；compete on a different track → 换一条赛道竞争；differentiation → 差异化；edge / differentiator → 优势

06
中文

典型场景：在资源、场地、合规都受限时，把「不能做的事」翻译成「可以做得更好的事」，是最高性价比的定位方法

EN

Typical scenario: when resources, space, and compliance are all constrained, translating 'what we can't do' into 'what we can do better' is the most cost-effective way to position yourself.

表达提示cost-effective → 性价比高的；position yourself → 定位自己；compliance → 合规；translate A into B → 把A转化成B

四、女性空间：从观众席走上舞台 [13:08]

A Women's Space: From the Audience to the Stage

情境The Witch House's website keeps a 'Time Tunnel' timeline of first performances since 1996 — Chen Shan-Ni, Huang Xiaozhen, Chen Qizhen, and Anpu all debuted there. One day a month, 'Witch Festival' features only female performers. The store's menu and decor openly embrace women's bodies and pleasure. 语域：文化观察 + 女性主义话题。

01
中文

核心机制：女巫店官网有名为「时光隧道」的年表，记录 1996 年以来许多乐团乐手的首次演出时间：开业第一年（1996）还是四分卫、骨皮这类摇滚乐队，年底才出现陈珊妮；从 1997 年开始，黄小桢、伊能静、陈绮贞陆续出现，1998 年巴奈，1999 年雷光夏和当时还未出道的安溥。此外，每个月有一天「女巫祭」，当天只有女性歌手可以演出

EN

The Witch House's website keeps a timeline called the 'Time Tunnel' recording the first performances of many bands and artists since 1996. In the opening year, 1996, the stage still belonged to rock bands like Quarterback and Boney Flesh; Chen Shan-Ni only showed up at year's end. From 1997, Huang Xiaozhen, Yi Nengjing, and Chen Qizhen followed one after another, then Panai in 1998, and Lei Guangxia plus a not-yet-debuted Anpu in 1999. Also, one day each month is 'Witch Festival', when only female singers can perform.

表达提示timeline → 时间线/年表；debut → 首次演出/出道；one after another → 陆续；show up → 出现

表达提示set apart from → 与…区分开；machismo → 阳刚气/大男子气；artistry → 文艺气息；a stage of one's own → 属于自己的舞台

五、独立精神不止于音乐 [16:32]

Independence Beyond Music

情境Poetry readings, glove puppetry, and stage plays — the Witch House's independent spirit welcomed any non-commercial expression, not just music. For its 20th anniversary it sold festival tickets by hand, one envelope at a time, cutting out ticketing middlemen. 语域：文化叙事 + 独立精神讨论。

01
中文

核心机制：女巫店倡导的独立精神不限于独立音乐，也欢迎各种独立于商业的创作与表达。1999 年，彭郁晶的德国丈夫悠悠主持诗歌发表活动，鸿鸿、夏宇（另一个名字李格蒂）都曾发表诗歌，还有人用接近摇滚的爆发力朗诵诗歌，模糊了诗歌与乐曲的界限。店里也上演过阿中偶戏布袋戏——布袋戏起源于福建、在台湾全面发展，靠手指和手掌操控木偶，无真人参与，依赖后场音乐配合，女巫店正是合适的场地；此外还有舞台剧等

EN

The independent spirit the Witch House championed was never limited to indie music — it welcomed all kinds of creation and expression that stood apart from commerce. In 1999, Peng's German husband Yo-Yo hosted poetry readings, where Hong Hong and Xia Yu — under her pen name Li Gedi — both read their work, and some people recited with an almost rock-like explosive force, blurring the line between poetry and music. The store also staged A-Chung's glove puppet theater — glove puppetry, which originated in Fujian and grew to full development in Taiwan, is operated by hand and fingers with no human performer, relying on the backstage band; the Witch House was exactly the right venue. Stage plays and other forms followed.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The show lifted martial law in 1987.
✓ Taiwan lifted martial law in 1987.

lift 的主语是政府/地区，不是节目；The show/host 只能做引述主体（the host says…），不能替代政策主体。

✕ A-Feng paid a Mercedes for the venue.
✓ A-Feng lost a Mercedes to the venue's debts.

pay 语义是「付钱购买」；「赔进去/倒贴」用 lose … to / sink money into 更准确。

✕ The unplugged rule is the shop's musical standpoint.
✓ Unplugged wasn't a musical standpoint — it was a compromise the noise forced.

把「被现状逼出来的妥协」说成主动立场（standpoint）丢掉了关键的被动性；用 not A but B 结构补出被动来源。

✕ Female artists can only play on the stage on Witch Festival day.
✓ Once a month, Witch Festival gives the stage to female artists only.

play on the stage 太直译；give the stage to sb 表达「让位」的机制感，once a month 才是准确的频次。

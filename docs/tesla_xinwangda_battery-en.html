Why Tesla Picked 'Second-Tier' Battery Maker Sunwoda

Three-Dao breaks down why Sunwoda became Tesla's fifth global battery supplier. From a new cooperation model where Sunwoda supplies only battery cells rather than finished modules, to the twin strategies of supply-chain diversification and de-empowering suppliers, he reveals how Tesla — facing falling revenue and halved gross margins — used a 'cheap and compliant' second-tier player to chip away at CATL's pricing power. He also maps the global battery landscape, where Chinese makers now hold a 70% share, and what that means for the balance of power in the industry.

Part 1：特斯拉的电池棋局

Part 1: Tesla's Battery Chessboard

情境The host explains the new supply model between Tesla and Sunwoda. 语域：casual 商业分析 + 供应链拆解。

01
中文

核心机制：欣旺达从浙江义乌工厂生产磷酸铁锂电芯，运至上海特斯拉超级工厂，由特斯拉自行完成模组/PACK组装、热管理、BMS与整包封装。类似宝马从宁德时代运电芯到沈阳工厂组装的模式

EN

The mechanism: Sunwoda produces LFP battery cells at its Yiwu factory in Zhejiang and ships them to Tesla's Shanghai gigafactory, where Tesla does the module and pack assembly, thermal management, BMS, and final packaging itself — a model similar to how BMW ships CATL cells to its Shenyang plant for assembly.

表达提示LFP cells → 磷酸铁锂电芯；gigafactory → 超级工厂；module / pack → 模组/电池包；BMS → 电池管理系统

02
中文

关键理解：特斯拉从"买成品电池包"转向"买原材料电芯"，对成本、性能与供应链话语权掌控更大。以前供应商卖成品，现在卖原材料

EN

The insight: Tesla is shifting from buying finished battery packs to buying raw cells, which gives it more control over cost, performance, and supply-chain leverage. Suppliers used to sell finished products; now they sell raw material.

表达提示finished battery pack → 成品电池包；raw cells → 电芯；supply-chain leverage → 供应链话语权

03
中文

典型场景：出口欧洲的Model Y等车型使用欣旺达电芯；宁德时代仍供应国内外大部分磷酸铁锂，占特斯拉全球采购四成到五成

EN

Typical case: models like the Model Y exported to Europe use Sunwoda cells, while CATL still supplies most of the LFP batteries at home and abroad — 40% to 50% of Tesla's global procurement.

表达提示global procurement → 全球采购；export to Europe → 出口欧洲；40% to 50% → 四到五成

04
中文

（引用）原文依据：「新旺达目前跟特斯拉的合作就是它只向特斯拉提供最最最基础的零酸铁里的电芯，大家注意是电芯不是电池。」

EN

As the host puts it: 'Sunwoda's current cooperation with Tesla is that it only supplies the most basic LFP cells — note, cells, not batteries.'

表达提示supply cells, not batteries → 供电芯不是电池；note → 注意；cooperation model → 合作模式

05
中文

（引用）相关概念：与宁德时代"直接供模组装车"是两种截然不同的合作深度；与4680自研电池是"自己种菜自己做饭"的终极理想

EN

Related concepts: this is a completely different depth of cooperation from CATL's 'direct module supply to the car line,' and a step toward the ultimate ideal of Tesla's in-house 4680 battery — 'growing your own vegetables and cooking your own meals.'

表达提示depth of cooperation → 合作深度；in-house → 自研；growing your own vegetables → 自己种菜（自给自足的比喻）

06
中文

三刀归纳两大核心原因：第一便宜，欣旺达走薄利多销路线，动力电池毛利率约9.77%，不到宁德时代23.84%的一半；第二听话，二线厂商合作不对等，条件放得低，愿意派团队驻场、让车企把控核心环节，甚至出让技术

EN

Three-Dao boils it down to two reasons. First, cheap: Sunwoda runs a small-margin, high-volume model, with a power-battery gross margin around 9.77% — less than half of CATL's 23.84%. Second, compliant: second-tier players negotiate from a weaker position, so they accept lower conditions, station teams on-site, let the carmaker control core processes, and even give up technology.

表达提示small-margin, high-volume → 薄利多销；gross margin → 毛利率；compliant → 听话的；station teams on-site → 派团队驻场

Paraphrase & Chunks 3 组表达

  1. 想说「从买成品到买原料」

    shift from A to B · buy the ingredients
    三明治比喻很口语生动

  2. 想说「二线厂商更听话」

    weaker position · be more accommodating
    accommodating 是「好说话、配合」的正式说法

  3. 想说「薄利多销」

    small margins, high volume · a low-margin play
    商业分析常用

Part 2：特斯拉业绩倒逼降本

Part 2: Tesla's Results Force a Cost Crackdown

情境The host connects Tesla's falling sales and margins to its aggressive cost-cutting. 语域：casual 财报分析 + 行业点评。

01
中文

核心机制：Model Y全球单一车型销量冠军（含燃油车），但公司成立以来首次营收下滑3%，净利润37.94亿美元同比暴跌46%，交付量连续两年下滑至163.6万辆

EN

The mechanism: the Model Y was the best-selling single model worldwide, gas cars included — yet revenue fell 3%, its first drop ever, net profit plunged 46% year over year to 3.794 billion dollars, and deliveries fell for two straight years to 1.636 million vehicles.

表达提示best-selling single model → 单一车型销量冠军；plunge → 暴跌；year over year → 同比；deliveries → 交付量

02
中文

关键理解：汽车毛利率从2021年约30%腰斩至15.4%（剔除碳积分后）；中国市场份额从6%降至4.9%，降价也难回高峰期。Optimus与Robotaxi持续烧钱，汽车业务是现金流"现金奶牛"

EN

The insight: auto gross margin was halved from about 30% in 2021 to 15.4% excluding carbon credits; China market share fell from 6% to 4.9%, and price cuts can't bring back the peak. Optimus and Robotaxi keep burning cash, so the car business is the cash cow that funds everything.

表达提示be halved → 腰斩/减半；carbon credits → 碳积分；cash cow → 现金奶牛；burn cash → 烧钱

03
中文

典型场景：砍掉不赚钱的Model S/X（曾具符号意义：展翼门创新、国内第一批名人车主背书），全面转向成本最低化

EN

Typical case: cutting unprofitable Model S and X — models with symbolic status, from falcon-wing doors to China's first celebrity owners — and turning fully to cost minimization.

表达提示falcon-wing doors → 展翼门；symbolic status → 符号意义；cost minimization → 成本最低化

04
中文

（引用）原文依据：「电池的成本相当于是占了它整个的这台车的成本的30%，30应该可能都不止30多。」

EN

As the host says: 'Battery cost is about 30% of the whole car's cost — probably more than 30%.'

表达提示battery cost → 电池成本；account for → 占…比例；the whole car's cost → 整车成本

05
中文

（引用）相关概念：电池成本占整车30%以上，是降本第一刀口；引入欣旺达是毛利率压力下的必然选择

EN

Related concept: battery cost at over 30% of the vehicle is the first cut for cost reduction; bringing in Sunwoda is the inevitable choice under margin pressure.

表达提示the first cut → 第一刀口；margin pressure → 毛利率压力；inevitable choice → 必然选择

06
中文

宁德时代2025年营收4237亿（+17%）、净利润722亿（+42%），全球市占率39.2%连续九年第一，装车量约为比亚迪2.4倍。动力电池毛利率23.84%，比同行高5到15个百分点，比特斯拉整车毛利还高

EN

CATL's 2025 results: revenue of 423.7 billion yuan, up 17%; net profit of 72.2 billion, up 42%; a global share of 39.2%, first for nine straight years, with installed capacity about 2.4 times BYD's. Its power-battery gross margin of 23.84% runs 5 to 15 percentage points above peers — higher than Tesla's whole-car margin.

表达提示net profit → 净利润；market share → 市占率；installed capacity → 装车量；percentage points → 百分点

Paraphrase & Chunks 3 组表达

  1. 想说「业绩腰斩」

    be halved · cut in half
    两词都可用，halved 更书面

  2. 想说「汽车业务是现金奶牛」

    cash cow · fund the moonshots
    cash cow 商业术语；moonshot 指烧钱的远期项目

  3. 想说「电池是第一刀口」

    the first cost to cut · the easiest savings
    分析语境适用

Part 3：全球电池市场大洗牌

Part 3: The Global Battery Market Reshuffles

情境The host maps the 2025 global battery landscape and why Japanese and Korean giants are losing ground. 语域：casual 行业数据解读 + 竞争分析。

01
中文

核心机制：2025年全球动力电池装车量1187GWh（+31.7%），前十中六家中国厂商（宁德、比亚迪、中创新航、国轩、亿纬锂能、蜂巢），合计市占率70.4%（2022年仅60.4%）

EN

The mechanism: global EV battery installations hit 1,187 GWh in 2025, up 31.7%. Six of the top ten are Chinese — CATL, BYD, CALB, Gotion, EVE Energy, and Svolt — together holding 70.4% of the market, up from 60.4% in 2022.

表达提示installations → 装车量；top ten → 前十；combined share → 合计市占率

02
中文

关键理解：中国厂商增速30-80%跑赢大盘，国轩高科增速82.5%最猛；日韩三巨头（LG/SK/三星）合计市占率降至15.3%（-3.4%），三星SDI是唯一负增长（-6.9%）

EN

The insight: Chinese makers grew 30% to 80%, outpacing the market, with Gotion the fastest at 82.5%. The Japanese-Korean trio of LG, SK, and Samsung fell to a combined 15.3%, down 3.4 points, and Samsung SDI was the only one in negative growth at minus 6.9%.

表达提示outpace → 跑赢；negative growth → 负增长；the Japanese-Korean trio → 日韩三巨头

03
中文

典型场景：国内新能源车不可能装日韩电池（意识层面+性价比+产业链完整）；日韩只能出海欧美，但中国厂商也在欧洲建厂抢份额

EN

Typical case: domestic EVs can't use Japanese or Korean cells — for reasons of sentiment, value, and supply-chain completeness — so Japan and Korea can only go to Europe and America, where Chinese makers are building plants to grab share anyway.

表达提示sentiment → 意识/心理层面；supply-chain completeness → 产业链完整；grab share → 抢份额

04
中文

（引用）原文依据：「全球动力电池市场的前十当中有六家都是中国的厂商……合计突破市占率70.4%。」

EN

As the host says: 'Six of the top ten in the global battery market are Chinese makers... together crossing 70.4% market share.'

表达提示cross a share → 突破市占率；top ten → 前十

05
中文

（引用）相关概念：宁德欧洲市占近40%、德国工厂满产；欣旺达匈牙利工厂推进本土化

EN

Related facts: CATL holds nearly 40% of the European market with its Germany plant at full capacity, while Sunwoda is pushing localization at its Hungary factory.

表达提示localization → 本土化；at full capacity → 满产；Hungary factory → 匈牙利工厂

06
中文

原因：①吃不到中国新能源红利；②欧美电动化降温（美国2025年电车销量十年来首次下降2%）；③押注三元锂高端路线，磷酸铁锂2024年渗透率突破50%后中低端失守；④过度依赖单一客户（松下/LG高度依赖特斯拉Model Y）

EN

Why they're falling: first, they miss out on China's EV boom; second, electrification is cooling in the West — US EV sales fell 2% in 2025, the first drop in a decade; third, they bet on the premium NMC route, losing the mid-and-low end once LFP passed 50% penetration in 2024; fourth, they depend too much on single customers — Panasonic and LG lean heavily on Tesla's Model Y.

表达提示miss out on → 错过/吃不到；NMC → 三元锂；penetration → 渗透率；lean heavily on → 高度依赖

    outpace the market · faster than the overall market
    outpace 更书面有力

  2. 想说「吃不到红利」

    miss out on · the windfall
    windfall 指意外红利

    rely on one customer · eggs in one basket

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Sunwanda is a second-line battery factory.
✓ Sunwoda is a second-tier battery maker.

品牌英文名是 Sunwoda；「二线」用 second-tier / second-tier player，second-line 是错误直译。

✕ The battery is 30% of the car's cost.
✓ Battery cost accounts for over 30% of the vehicle's total cost.

直接说 is 30% 显得口语；商业分析里用 account for 更正式地道。

✕ Tesla bought cheap battery to save money.
✓ Tesla brought in a cheaper supplier to relieve margin pressure.

买便宜货 ≠ 降本策略；要说出目的 relieve margin pressure / cut cost。

✕ The market share is 70.4% of China.
✓ Chinese makers hold a combined 70.4% of the global market.

是「全球市占率70.4%」，不是「中国的市场份额」；combined 表「合计」。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为特斯拉选电池只看技术最先进 → 成本与话语权才是关键——便宜+听话的二线厂商是撬动定价权的杠杆
以为供应商越强越好 → 供应商太强会反噬定价权，多供应商+自研才是车企的护城河
以为中国电池强只是性价比 → 70%全球市占率背后是产业链完整+规模效应+增速碾压

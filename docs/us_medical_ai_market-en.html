二、三巨头与黑马的商业模式

2. The Business Models of the Big Three and the Dark Horse

情境OpenEvidence 的 RAG 架构、Medical Coding 的市场机会与 Anthropic 的落地。语域：casual 播客访谈 + 商业模式分析。

01
中文

它不是问诊AI，而是高度优化的检索架构——所有回答必须来自授权期刊，禁止自由发挥。核心竞争力是数据授权和医生渗透速度，而非模型能力。赚钱靠药企广告预算（原本给医药代表的渠道预算分流）

EN

It's not a diagnosis AI — it's a highly optimized retrieval architecture. Every answer must come from authorized journals; no improvising. Its core advantage is data licensing and how fast it reaches doctors, not model capability. It earns from pharma advertising budgets, diverting the channel budgets once spent on pharma reps.

表达提示retrieval architecture → 检索架构；data licensing → 数据授权；pharma rep → 医药代表；divert → 分流

三、评估标准与创业机会

3. Evaluation Standards & Startup Opportunities

情境从选择题到真实对话场景的评估进化，以及医疗数据是否会被巨头垄断。语域：casual 播客访谈 + 创业讨论。

01
中文

旧方法：MedQA/PubMedQA选择题——ChatGPT早就能考90+分（美国医师执照考试），测的是书本知识

EN

The old way: multiple-choice benchmarks like MedQA and PubMedQA — ChatGPT was already scoring 90-plus on the US medical licensing exam, but those tests measure book knowledge.

表达提示multiple-choice benchmark → 选择题基准；medical licensing exam → 医师执照考试；book knowledge → 书本知识

02
中文

新方法：262位医生、60国26专科设计真实对话场景评分——涵盖是否安抚患者、是否覆盖关键医学要点；危险错误会扣分至负分

EN

The new way: 262 doctors across 26 specialties in 60 countries designed real conversation scenarios for scoring — covering whether the model reassures patients and hits key medical points, with dangerous errors deducting into negative scores.

表达提示reassure the patient → 安抚患者；hit key points → 覆盖关键要点；deduct into negative → 扣到负分

EN

The key data: OpenAI's O3 scored 60% on Health Bench, topping out at 32% on hard mode. It does well on emergencies and common conditions like hypertension, but clearly drops on complex and rare diseases.

表达提示Health Bench → 健康对话基准；hypertension → 高血压；complex and rare diseases → 复杂疑难病

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ The doctor works 62 hours every week, all the time sees patients.
✓ The GP works 61.8 hours a week but spends most of it on administrative processes, not patients.

「每周 62 小时」要接 but 转折说明大量时间在文书；直译 all the time sees patients 与原意相反。

✕ The insurance refused to pay, so the doctor must code perfectly.
✓ Only 10% of denied claims are appealed, yet 80% of appeals are overturned — the problem is paperwork, not medicine.

要传达「拒付大多因文书错误、申诉胜率高」的结构，而不是简单因果；补数据让逻辑成立。

✕ ChatGPT can get 90 points in medical exam, so it can see patients.
✓ A 90 on multiple-choice tests says little about real clinical conversation ability.

考试高分 ≠ 临床能力，正是本期观点；用 says little about 表达「说明不了什么」。

✕ AI can replace doctors in the future.
✓ AI assists; licensed doctors still make the final medical decisions.

「替代医生」是过度推断；用 assists / final decisions 表达 AI 的工具定位，符合本期结论。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为医疗 AI 高分=能看病 → 选择题 90 分不代表真实对话能力——新评估是医生设计的真实场景评分

以为 AI 可以替代医生决策 → AI 是工具、医生是主体，急救与用药必须由持证医生确认

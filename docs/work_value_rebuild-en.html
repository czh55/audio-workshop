故事一：编剧小洋 · 从工作狂到陪伴女儿

Story 1: Xiaoyang the Screenwriter — From Workaholic to Father

情境The first story: a screenwriter who found his worth not in scripts but in being present with his daughter. 语域：播客叙事 + 人物访谈转述。

01
中文

核心机制：小洋从河南小城靠写作贴补家用，2017年因目睹搭档「36元猫罐头」的体面生活进入影视编剧行业。从枪手到署名编剧，价值感长期绑定在「写出好作品、成为大编剧、坐主桌」上

EN

The mechanism: Xiaoyang left a small Henan town where writing helped pay the bills, and entered the TV industry in 2017 after seeing his partner's comfortable life — complete with 36-yuan cat food cans. From ghostwriter to credited screenwriter, his sense of worth stayed chained to 'writing a great script, becoming a big name, sitting at the head table.'

表达提示ghostwriter → 枪手；credited screenwriter → 署名编剧；pay the bills → 贴补家用；sit at the head table → 坐主桌

02
中文

关键理解：去年职业倦怠叠加成为新手妈妈、母亲病情恶化，他决定减产项目、带八口之家（四猫一狗）从上海回新乡。女儿第一次翻身让她看见「生命曾为一次翻身如此努力」——消解了生育必须「完整人生」的足议式自我剥削

EN

The insight: last year, burnout plus becoming a new parent and his mother's worsening illness pushed him to cut back on projects and move the eight-member family — four cats and a dog — from Shanghai back to Xinxiang. His daughter's first roll-over showed him that 'life works so hard for a single roll,' dissolving the self-exploitation of the idea that parenthood must fit into a 'complete life.'

表达提示burnout → 职业倦怠；cut back on → 减产；roll over → 翻身；self-exploitation → 自我剥削

03
中文

EN

Typical moment: when creative passion fades and family needs you, pull 'who I am' out of the industry's judgment and point it at family time that can't be repeated.

表达提示pull A out of B → 把A从B中抽离；industry judgment → 行业评价；can't be repeated → 不可重来

表达提示be here for each other → 陪伴着彼此；in the end → 到最后

05
中文

（引用）相关概念：编剧「窝囊费+挨骂费+创作费」三分结构 vs 观众以为的「一剧之本话语权」

EN

Related concept: a screenwriter's income splits into 'humiliation pay, criticism pay, and creation pay' — versus the audience's assumption that writers hold the creative power of 'the script is the foundation.'

表达提示humiliation pay → 窝囊费；criticism pay → 挨骂费；the script is the foundation → 一剧之本

稿费三部分：窝囊费（平衡各方意见）、挨骂费（播出必挨骂）、认真创作报酬。编剧像「情绪按摩师」，后期修改常脱离最初创作意图，但情绪稳定是第一性的

EN

The three parts of the paycheck: humiliation pay for balancing everyone's opinions, criticism pay for the backlash every broadcast brings, and pay for serious creation. A screenwriter is an 'emotional masseur' — late-stage revisions often drift far from the original intent, but keeping everyone calm comes first.

表达提示emotional masseur → 情绪按摩师；drift far from → 脱离；keep everyone calm → 稳住各方情绪

故事二：313 · 「我是一个好人」

Story 2: 313 — 'I Am a Good Person'

情境A state-owned-enterprise employee rebuilds her identity around kindness rather than her job. 语域：播客叙事 + 职场委屈转述。

01
中文

核心机制：建筑央企七年，区域创业期目标一致、考核向上；去年管理层换血，不了解业务的上级用职权打压，认真做事者承担更多责任却可能被牺牲

EN

The mechanism: seven years at a construction SOE, with a startup-period team united on goals and reviewing upward. Last year the management changed; new superiors who don't understand the business started using their authority to push people down, and those who work hard take on more responsibility only to be sacrificed.

表达提示SOE → 央企（state-owned enterprise）；management reshuffle → 管理层换血；push people down → 打压

02
中文

关键理解：313一直知道工作只是人生价值一部分，但此次事件让她追问「多做多错、不做不错，认真负责的意义在哪」。价值感核心变成「我是一个好人」——收养流浪小狗、救下受伤小猫，与伴侣共建六只猫狗的小家

EN

The insight: 313 always knew work was only part of her life's value, but this incident made her ask 'if doing more just gets you blamed, what's the point of being conscientious?' Her sense of worth became simply: 'I am a good person' — she adopts stray dogs, rescues injured cats, and she and her partner built a home for six cats and dogs.

表达提示conscientious → 认真负责的；adopt strays → 收养流浪动物；what's the point → 意义在哪

03
中文

典型场景：宠物需要更大空间→紧急换租→买房；小白狗像「月老红绳」牵来同频伴侣

EN

Typical story: the pets needed more space, so she rushed to a bigger rental, then bought a home. The little white dog was the 'matchmaker's red thread' that brought in a partner who vibes with her.

表达提示matchmaker's red thread → 月老红绳；vibe with sb → 与某人同频；rush to a new rental → 紧急换租

04
中文

（引用）原文依据：「之外，自己最大的价值是一个好人。」

EN

'Apart from that, my biggest value is being a good person.'

表达提示apart from that → 除此之外；my biggest value → 最大的价值

05
中文

EN

In an economic downturn inside a bureaucratic SOE, relationships can outweigh the work itself. If a thing goes well, it's the leader's ability; if it goes wrong, it's the frontline's execution that failed.

表达提示bureaucratic → 体制化的；outweigh → 压过；frontline execution → 一线执行

故事三：小猫 · 两次网贷与坦承

Story 3: Xiaomao — Twice Into Online Debt, and Learning to Tell the Truth

情境A young woman who fell into online loan traps twice and rebuilt herself through honest disclosure. 语域：播客叙事 + 财务自救。

01
中文

核心机制：小县城成绩不错、父母离异反复拉扯要钱，极度渴望独立体面。网贷「丝滑小连招」：分期几百块看似可还→借新还旧→两次合计近30万。不敢拉总账单，幻想「找到1万5工作就能还」

EN

The mechanism: she did well in school in a small county town, but her divorced parents kept pulling her into money disputes, and she desperately wanted to be independent and respectable. The loan apps run a 'smooth combo': a few hundred yuan in installments looks payable, then you borrow to repay, and twice it added up to nearly 300,000 yuan. She couldn't face the full statement, dreaming that 'a 15,000-yuan job would cover it.'

表达提示loan apps → 网贷平台；borrow to repay → 借新还旧；full statement → 总账单；face the numbers → 直面数字

02
中文

关键理解：第一次向爸爸求助被拒，向妈妈坦白后一起拉账单；第二次在额度抽干、以贷养贷断裂后才再次坦承。承认「我一个人解决不了」是真正走出陷阱的开始

EN

The insight: the first time she asked her father for help and was refused; she confessed to her mother and they pulled the bills together. The second time, she only came clean after her credit was drained and the borrow-to-repay chain broke. Admitting 'I can't solve this alone' is where escaping the trap actually begins.

表达提示come clean → 坦承；credit drained → 额度被抽干；the chain broke → 链条断裂；can't solve this alone → 一个人解决不了

03
中文

典型场景：还款日额度用尽、平台借不出任何钱——被迫面对现实

EN

Typical moment: on repayment day the credit runs out and no platform will lend another yuan — you're forced to face reality.

表达提示repayment day → 还款日；run out of credit → 额度用尽；face reality → 面对现实

04
中文

（引用）避坑：以贷养贷、不敢算总账、用消费维持「优秀体面」人设

EN

Pitfalls to avoid: borrowing to repay, refusing to total the accounts, and using spending to keep up the 'smart and respectable' persona.

表达提示borrow to repay → 以贷养贷；total the accounts → 算总账；persona → 人设

05
中文

（引用）落地：向信任的人（先表姐、再妈妈）坦白；列清所有平台账单和支出明细；制定每月存钱还款计划

EN

How to act: confess to someone you trust — her cousin first, then her mother; list every platform's bill and every expense; and make a monthly plan to save and repay.

表达提示confess to → 坦白；expense breakdown → 支出明细；monthly repayment plan → 每月还款计划

06
中文

羽白恳请听众不要急于审判——值得听的不只是欠了多少钱，而是为什么一次次用同样方式让自己深陷泥潭，以及如何开始承认需要他人帮助

EN

Yubai asks listeners not to rush to judgment — the story worth hearing isn't just how much she owed, but why she kept sinking into the same trap the same way, and how she finally started admitting she needed other people's help.

表达提示rush to judgment → 急于审判；sink into the same trap → 深陷同一个泥潭；admit needing help → 承认需要帮助

Paraphrase & Chunks 3 组表达

  1. 想说「以贷养贷」

    borrow to repay · rob Peter to pay Paul
    rob Peter to pay Paul 是地道谚语

  2. 想说「不敢面对总账」

    face the statement · add up the real number
    face 表「敢于面对」

  3. 想说「承认需要帮助」

    admit you need help · can't do this alone
    表达脆弱也是一种勇气

Story 4: Gaifan — Job-Hunting Anxiety and a West Lake Sunset

情境A fresh graduate's job anxiety and the moment a West Lake sunset reset her sense of value. 语域：播客叙事 + 生活感悟。

01
中文

核心机制：2024文科毕业生，疫情网课导致几乎无实习，价值全部绑在找工作上。拿到互联网大厂offer后工作压力致身体出问题，独自做手术

EN

表达提示liberal-arts graduate → 文科毕业生；land a job → 找到工作；have surgery alone → 独自做手术

02
中文

'So a weekday evening can be this beautiful too.' She realized she'd gained a good job but lost a lot along the way.

表达提示groggy from anesthesia → 麻药未退；mandarin ducks → 鸳鸯；weekday evening → 工作日的傍晚

03

EN

表达提示squeezed to the edge → 被挤到边缘；late-stage illness → 晚期病；rush home → 突击回家

表达提示bring back → 恢复；the only urgent and important thing → 唯一紧急重要之事

故事五：老李 · 第一次主动选择

Story 5: Lao Li — The First Choice He Made Himself

情境A former Shanghai restaurateur makes the first deliberate decision of his life. 语域：播客叙事 + 人生复盘。

01
中文

核心机制：97年，美国留学（母亲决定出国、升学老师选校选专业语言学）、上海餐饮（因前任在上海而留下）。从月薪3000小县城到宰下第一个20万、100万，每周只休半天、靠酒精抵消压力

EN

The mechanism: born in 1997, he studied in the US — his mother decided on studying abroad, and his counselor picked the school and the linguistics major — then worked in Shanghai restaurants because an ex lived there. From a 3,000-yuan monthly salary in a small county to his first 200,000 and then a million in revenue, he rested half a day a week and numbed the stress with alcohol.

表达提示counselor → 升学老师；numb the stress → 用酒精麻痹压力；rest half a day a week → 每周只休半天

02
中文

关键理解：2025年4月结束上海餐饮工作，纳瓦尔「三决定」（城市、事业、伴侣）+芒格逆向思维：不知道想要什么就先排除不想要的——上海不是答案，但不知哪里是答案，先迈出离开上海的第一步回成都

EN

The insight: in April 2025 he ended his Shanghai restaurant career, using Naval Ravikant's 'three decisions' — city, career, and partner — plus Munger-style inversion: if you don't know what you want, first remove what you don't. Shanghai wasn't the answer, but neither did he know where the answer was, so he took the first step of leaving for Chengdu.

表达提示inversion → 逆向思维；Naval's three decisions → 纳瓦尔三决定；take the first step → 迈出第一步

03
中文

典型场景：亲密关系结束、工作结束，所有留沪理由消失，第一次「把自己交给生命之流」

EN

Typical moment: the relationship ended, the work ended, every reason to stay in Shanghai disappeared — and for the first time he 'gave himself to the flow of life.'

表达提示give oneself to the flow of life → 把自己交给生命之流；reasons to stay → 留下来的理由

04
中文

（引用）原文依据：「我从来没有一次，是全然听从内心，给出答案并且行动。」

EN

'Never once in my life did I listen fully to my heart, give myself an answer, and act on it.'

表达提示listen to one's heart → 听从内心；act on it → 付诸行动

05
中文

9点开会（财务/采购/主厨/老板轮值）→10点半营业准备→11:30-14:00服务→14:00起策划pop-up活动、设计菜单，常工作至23:00，每周只休半天

EN

A typical day: a 9 a.m. meeting with finance, procurement, the head chef, and the owner rotating in; prep by 10:30; service from 11:30 to 2 p.m.; then from 2 p.m. on, planning pop-up events and designing menus, often working until 11 p.m., with half a day off each week.

表达提示pop-up events → 快闪活动；rotate in → 轮值；menu design → 菜单设计

06
中文

（引用）「决定许多人一生绝大部分生活质量的，其实就只有三个决定：在哪里生活、从事什么、与谁一起。」

EN

'What determines most of the quality of most people's lives comes down to just three decisions: where you live, what you do, and who you're with.'

表达提示come down to → 归结为；quality of life → 生活质量；who you're with → 与谁一起

Paraphrase & Chunks 3 组表达

  1. 想说「一直被安排的人生」

    decided by others · chosen for him
    被动句式传达「被安排」

  2. 想说「逆向排除法」

    rule out · subtract instead of add
    inversion 是芒格术语

  3. 想说「三个决定决定人生质量」

    shape life's quality · where you live, what you do, who you're with
    三词排比适合引用

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

用「be here for each other」说：什么都没解决，但我们在彼此身边。

Nothing got solved, but we were here for each other — and that was enough.

用「borrow to repay」说：以贷养贷是个死循环。

Borrowing to repay the old loans is a trap with no exit.

用「come at a price」说：得到工作也付出了代价。

The job offer came at a price she didn't notice until it was too late.

用「rule out」说：不知道想要什么就先排除不想要的。

When you don't know what you want, start by ruling out what you don't.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ I am a good man is my value.
✓ My biggest value is being a good person.

「价值感核心是…」要用名词结构 My biggest value is doing sth，不能用句子直接当宾语。

✕ He borrows new money to pay old money.
✓ He was borrowing to repay the old loans — robbing Peter to pay Paul.

borrow to repay 是固定表达；robbing Peter to pay Paul 地道地形容借新还旧。

✕ I got a good job but lost many things.
✓ She gained a good job, but it came at a price she didn't notice.

直译「失去很多」太泛；come at a price 把「代价」说得更准确。

✕ I never listened my heart.
✓ Never once did I listen fully to my heart and act on it.

listen 是不及物动词，要说 listen to；且 act on it 补齐「行动」这半句。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为价值感来自工作成绩和头衔 → 陪伴、照顾、坦承这些「不被打分」的事，才是价值感的底座

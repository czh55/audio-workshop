一、本届世界杯基础画像

The 2026 World Cup at a Glance

情境The hosts lay out the tournament's new format and geography. 语域：casual 闲聊科普。

01
中文

核心机制：史上首次三国（美国、加拿大、墨西哥）联合举办，从32强扩至48强，共104场比赛，夺冠需踢满8场。12个小组每组4队，前两名+8个成绩最好的第三名出线

EN

The mechanism: for the first time ever, three countries — the US, Canada, and Mexico — co-host, and the field grows from 32 to 48 teams across 104 matches; champions must play eight games. Twelve groups of four, with the top two plus the eight best third-place teams advancing.

表达提示co-host → 联合举办；advance → 出线；top two plus best third-place → 前两名+最好第三名

02
中文

EN

The insight: in past tournaments, weaker teams often tanked the final group game once elimination was certain; this year a third-place finish can still get you through, so the last round is far more suspenseful.

表达提示tank → 摆烂/消极比赛；suspenseful → 悬念十足；get through → 晋级

二、三国合办：经济账与政治账

Three Nations, Two Ledgers: Economics and Politics

情境The hosts explain why three countries are co-hosting. 语域：casual 闲聊拆解。

01
中文

省钱：48队104场若单一国家承办，体育场等基建成本过重；美加墨均有现成高标准场馆（如阿兹特克体育场），分摊投资

EN

Saving money: hosting 48 teams and 104 matches alone would overload one country's infrastructure budget; the US, Canada, and Mexico already have high-standard venues — like the Estadio Azteca — so the investment gets split.

表达提示infrastructure budget → 基建成本；split the investment → 分摊投资；high-standard venues → 高标准场馆

02
中文

竞争：2017-18申办时，2022已定亚洲、2018在欧洲，主要对手是摩洛哥。三国联合增强对非洲对手的竞争力，也让国际足联对首次48队大赛更放心

EN

Competition: when bidding in 2017-18, with 2022 already in Asia and 2018 in Europe, the main rival was Morocco. A three-nation bid strengthened their case against the African challenger and reassured FIFA about pulling off the first 48-team tournament.

表达提示bid → 申办；challenger → 竞争对手；reassure FIFA → 让国际足联放心

03
中文

EN

表达提示take the lead → 带头；a slice of the pie → 分一杯羹/喝点汤；automatic host berth → 主办国保送名额

✕ The football field expanded to 48 teams.
✓ The tournament expanded from 32 to 48 teams.

扩军指的是赛事规模（the field / the number of teams），不是球场 stadium/field；「参赛队数」用 the field 更地道。

✕ Messi and Ronaldo are very old this time.
✓ Messi at 38 and Ronaldo at 41 are entering the twilight of their careers.

「诸神黄昏」用 twilight / the final act，比 very old 高级且准确；中文「黄昏」对应 twilight。

✕ The ticket price is very expensive.
✓ This is the most expensive World Cup ever — average tickets jumped from 70 to 120 dollars.

expensive 的主语不能是 price；用 the most expensive World Cup + 具体数字对比才地道。

✕ China bid the World Cup in 2042.
✓ China could bid to host the World Cup as early as 2042.

申办世界杯是 bid to host；2042 只是最早可能的时间点，用 could + as early as 表达不确定性。

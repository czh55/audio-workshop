S1

一、什么是「自己」？

What Is the "Self"?

情境「做」是知行合一，「自己」是动态概念；与任性、人设的区别。语域：casual 对谈 + 哲学讨论。

01
中文

核心机制：「做自己」由「做」和「自己」组成。「做」意味着知行合一；「自己」则是冥想中的觉察/失觉察意识、现实中想说不敢说的想法，哪一层才是真我？

EN

The mechanism: "being yourself" is made of "being" and "yourself." "Being" means aligning knowledge with action; "yourself" is the awareness you notice in meditation, the thoughts you dare not speak in real life — which layer is the true self?

表达提示align knowledge with action → 知行合一；the true self → 真我；dare not speak → 不敢说

02
中文

关键理解：自己是一个动态概念，会经历多次推翻与重建。过去认定的「真实想法」可能只是表述，深层可能有道德审判或恐惧

EN

The key insight: the self is a dynamic concept that gets overturned and rebuilt many times. What you once believed was your "true thought" may only be a phrasing — beneath it could be moral judgment or fear.

表达提示dynamic concept → 动态概念；overturned and rebuilt → 推翻与重建；moral judgment → 道德审判

03
中文

典型场景：当你发现「我其实在意别人怎么看我」「我其实想要亲密关系」时，不是失败，而是松动固化的开始

EN

A typical scene: when you discover "I actually care what people think" or "I actually want intimacy," that's not failure — it's the beginning of loosening something rigid.

表达提示loosening → 松动；rigid → 固化的；care what people think → 在意别人的看法

04
中文

（引用）原文依据：「搞清楚自己的定义，可能才是做自己的第一步。但是自己是一个动态的东西。」

EN

"Figuring out the definition of yourself might be the first step to being yourself. But the self is a dynamic thing."

表达提示the first step → 第一步；dynamic → 动态的

05
中文

（引用）相关概念：与「任性」「人设」「社会形象」的区别——任性是逃避，做自己是觉察后的表达

EN

Related concept: the difference from "willfulness," "persona," and "social image" — willfulness is escape; being yourself is expression after awareness.

表达提示willfulness → 任性；persona → 人设；expression after awareness → 觉察后的表达

06
中文

杨天真认为，早期以为要分享职场干货才有价值，后来意识到她最大的价值是「好好活着」——从心底爱自己、不畏争议、把探索过程分享出来。对批评不在意，对赞美也不在意

EN

Yang Tianzhen used to think she was valuable only when sharing career tips; later she realized her greatest value is simply "living well" — loving herself from the heart, unafraid of controversy, and sharing the process of exploration. Criticism doesn't move her, and praise doesn't either.

表达提示living well → 好好活着；unafraid of controversy → 不畏争议

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「做自己没那么简单」

    harder than it sounds · not about being willful
    第一句口语，第二句点题

  2. 想说「松动固化认知」

    loosen the grip · unfreeze the identity
    第一句温和，第二句更形象
S2

二、壳、真我与「花钥匙」

The Shell, the True Self, and the "Flower Key"

情境壳的功能与代价；真我有限展示；壳可穿可脱。语域：casual 对谈 + 比喻讲解。

01
中文

核心机制：从小到大建设的「壳」让我们符合父母、老师、社交场合的预期，帮助生存、避免受伤。但壳也会限制感受——少一波曾以「没感情」的壳生活，看不到风景、不理解女儿哭泣

EN

The mechanism: the shell we build from childhood lets us meet the expectations of parents, teachers, and social settings — it helps us survive and avoid hurt. But the shell also limits feeling — Shao Yibo once lived behind an "emotionless" shell, blind to the scenery and unable to understand his daughter's tears.

表达提示the shell → 壳/人设；meet expectations → 符合预期；emotionless → 没感情

02
中文

关键理解：壳永远不会完全消散，也不必消散。目标是：不把壳当成自己，而是像盔甲一样可穿可脱。真我有无限的爱、勇气和慈悲

EN

The key insight: the shell never fully dissolves, and it doesn't have to. The goal is not to mistake the shell for yourself — treat it like armor you can put on and take off. The true self holds unlimited love, courage, and compassion.

表达提示put on and take off → 可穿可脱；compassion → 慈悲；mistake A for B → 把 A 当成 B

03
中文

典型场景：在嫌恶环境中穿上壳保护自己；在安全空间里脱下壳与真我连接

EN

A typical scene: put on the shell in hostile environments to protect yourself; take it off in safe spaces to reconnect with the true self.

表达提示hostile environment → 嫌恶环境；safe space → 安全空间

04
中文

（引用）原文依据：「我们不应该做橡皮泥，应该做固定的骨骼，但可以有很多花衣裳。」

EN

"We shouldn't be putty — we should be a fixed skeleton that can wear many pretty outfits."

表达提示putty → 橡皮泥；fixed skeleton → 固定的骨骼；pretty outfits → 花衣裳

05
中文

（引用）杨天真用《花仙子》花钥匙类比：不同场合扮演不同角色不是虚伪，真我需要被保护，只在有限空间和有限人面前展示

EN

Yang Tianzhen uses the Flower Fairy's magic key as an analogy: playing different roles in different settings isn't hypocrisy — the true self needs protection and only shows in limited spaces, before limited people.

表达提示magic key → 花钥匙；hypocrisy → 虚伪；limited spaces → 有限空间

06
中文

原因：壳很脆弱，需要不停维护；残酷打击会击溃壳或让它变硬加厚，与真我失联 20-50 年

EN

The reason: the shell is fragile and needs constant upkeep; cruel blows either shatter it or make it thicken and harden, cutting you off from the true self for 20 to 50 years.

表达提示constant upkeep → 不停维护；shatter → 击溃；cut off from → 与…失联

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「壳可穿可脱」

    armor not identity · put it on / take it off
    第一句定义，第二句具体场景

  2. 想说「角色不是虚伪」

    not being fake · different faces · protection
    第一句直接，第二句解释原因
S3

三、对「真」的追求

The Pursuit of "Truth"

情境真的定义与代价；说真话的修炼；melting love 的恐惧。语域：casual 对谈 + 自省。

01
中文

真不是主观定义，也不是摄像机客观场景，而是人与人之间「心知肚明就是这么回事」。关系必须面对真相，所有假的东西都不值得投入且会破碎

EN

"Truth" isn't a subjective definition, nor a camera's objective shot — it's what people privately know to be the case between them. Relationships must face the truth; everything fake isn't worth investing in and will shatter anyway.

表达提示privately know → 心知肚明；invest in → 投入；shatter → 破碎

02
中文

适用边界：真只有当事人和共同经历者才知道；表达真话的方式可以修炼（如杨天真学「船要沉了」如何更负责任地说）

EN

The boundary: truth is only known to those involved and those who shared the experience; the way you deliver it can be practiced — Yang Tianzhen once learned how to say "the ship is sinking" more responsibly.

表达提示deliver the truth → 表达真话；responsibly → 负责任地

03
中文

（引用）「我生命中有一个东西牵引着我，它可能是我所有能量的来源，这个东西就叫做你对真的追求。」

EN

"There's something pulling me through life, and it may be the source of all my energy — it's your pursuit of truth."

表达提示the source of my energy → 能量的来源；pursuit of truth → 对真的追求

04
中文

（引用）怎么落地：在亲密关系中停止表演成另一个人；说真话可能不得预期，但假装的代价是身心俱疲

EN

How to apply it: stop performing as someone else in intimate relationships; the truth may not land as expected, but the price of pretending is total exhaustion.

表达提示perform as → 表演成；land as expected → 得到预期效果；total exhaustion → 身心俱疲

05
中文

核心机制：杨天真曾恐惧「相容之爱」（自我溶解、无边界），表现出攻击性和「不需要」。深层是害怕得不到、不会处理，却用「爱情浪费生命」等人设包装

EN

The mechanism: Yang Tianzhen once feared "melting love" — the self dissolving without boundaries — and showed aggression and "I don't need it." Deep down she feared not getting it and not knowing how to handle it, wrapping it in the persona that "love wastes your life."

表达提示melting love → 相容之爱；wastes your life → 浪费生命

06
中文

关键理解：承认「我也非常想要这样的 melting love」需要巨大勇气——对自己承认，更对在公众面前承认

EN

The key insight: admitting "I also deeply want this melting love" takes enormous courage — admitting it to yourself, and even more, admitting it in public.

表达提示admit to oneself → 对自己承认；in public → 在公众面前

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「心知肚明的真」

    both know what's real · unspoken but clear
    第一句口语，第二句有画面

  2. 想说「怕的是得不到」

    fear of never having it · under the bravado
    第一句直白，第二句分析
S4

四、自我探索的五阶段

The Five Stages of Self-Exploration

情境认知松动有风险；危机驱动 vs 愿景驱动；从小改变获得甜头。语域：casual 对谈 + 方法论。

01
中文

（引用）杨天真补充：打破固化认知充满风险——安全感消失、自信动摇，有人退回去，有人更往前走

EN

Yang Tianzhen adds: breaking rigid beliefs is risky — the sense of safety vanishes, confidence wobbles, and some people retreat while others push further forward.

表达提示rigid belief → 固化认知；retreat → 退回去；push forward → 往前走

02
中文

原因：90% 动力来自「无法接受现状」的危机；仅 10% 来自愿景驱动，且难以持续

EN

The reason: 90 percent of the drive comes from the crisis of "can't accept the status quo"; only 10 percent is vision-driven, and that's hard to sustain.

表达提示status quo → 现状；vision-driven → 愿景驱动；sustain → 持续

03
中文

解法：先从小改变获得甜头和自由感，再驱动更大改变；接受探索路上会出现新焦虑（少一波承认焦虑花了半年）

EN

The solution: start with small changes to get a taste of payoff and freedom, then drive bigger changes; accept that new anxiety will surface along the way — Shao Yibo admits his anxiety lasted half a year.

表达提示a taste of payoff → 获得甜头；surface → 出现

04
中文

严重程度：可忽略——没有危机也不代表不能开始，好奇心也是入口

EN

Severity: negligible — no crisis doesn't mean you can't start; curiosity is also a doorway in.

表达提示a doorway in → 入口；curiosity → 好奇心

05
中文

（引用）杨天真滑雪受伤：每天背单词、做康复做不到，但为「真的想滑雪」可开始艰难康复之旅

EN

Yang Tianzhen's ski-injury story: she couldn't bring herself to memorize words daily or do rehab — but because she genuinely wanted to ski again, she started the grueling recovery.

表达提示recovery → 康复；grueling → 艰难的；bring oneself to → 说服自己去做

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「危机驱动 vs 愿景驱动」

    pain moves you · crisis ignites · vision guides
    第一句直白，第二句对仗

  2. 想说「从小改变开始」

    win small first · small wins unlock
    第一句口语，第二句带策略感
S5

五、做好人、责任与关系

Being Good, Responsibility, and Relationships

情境「做好人」也可能只是壳的自我肯定；氧气面罩原则；接受善意。语域：casual 对谈 + 生活故事。

01
中文

少一波反思：曾通过「做好人/做善事」获得自我肯定，与从前数学第一名、商业成功一样——变汤没变料，仍是壳的自我肯定

EN

Shao Yibo reflects: he used to earn self-approval by "being good and doing good deeds" — the same old pattern as topping math class or business success: new soup, same ingredients, still the shell's self-approval.

表达提示self-approval → 自我肯定；new soup, same ingredients → 变汤没变料

02
中文

飞机氧气面罩原则：紧急时刻先照顾好自己，有馀力再照顾别人。只保护自己就是自私，但先保护自己是守卫能量

EN

The airplane oxygen-mask principle: in an emergency, take care of yourself first, then others if you have anything left. Protecting only yourself is selfish, but protecting yourself first is guarding your energy.

表达提示oxygen mask → 氧气面罩；guarding your energy → 守卫能量

03
中文

（引用）「日行的小善——带着善意的善，intention 不一样，整个行为就会不一样。」

EN

"Small daily kindnesses — kindness with genuine intent. When the intention differs, the whole act differs."

表达提示genuine intent → 带着善意；the whole act differs → 整个行为就不同

04
中文

朋友远程安排餐厅，杨天真曾因「未经允许安排行程」坚持自己去吃，让大家不开心。如今意识到：接受对方的善意，比吃那顿想吃的饭更重要。好关系的目标是「你也要开心，我也要开心」，而非「只有我开心」。安排餐厅的朋友已过世，成为珍惜善意的提醒

EN

A friend booked a restaurant remotely; Yang Tianzhen once insisted on eating where she wanted because the trip was arranged "without permission," upsetting everyone. Now she sees that accepting someone's kindness matters more than eating the meal you had in mind. A good relationship aims for "you're happy, and so am I" — not "only I'm happy." The friend who booked that restaurant has since passed away, a reminder to treasure kindness.

表达提示book a restaurant → 订餐厅；treasure kindness → 珍惜善意；upset → 让…不开心

05
中文

（引用）原文依据：「如果你想要一个好的关系，你必须得以你们共同的利益为一个目标。」

EN

"If you want a good relationship, you have to make your shared interest the goal."

表达提示shared interest → 共同利益；the goal → 目标

06
中文

为什么重要：固化认知带来虚假安全感；松动后可能迷茫，但也会获得数量级的新自由

EN

Why it matters: rigid beliefs bring a false sense of safety; loosening them may bring confusion, but it also brings new freedom of a different magnitude.

表达提示false sense of safety → 虚假安全感；a different magnitude → 数量级的差异

Paraphrase & Chunks 3 组表达

  1. 想说「变汤没变料」

    new wrapper · same old core · same pattern
    第一句形象，第二句直接

  2. 想说「先照顾自己不是自私」

    self-care first · pour from an empty cup
    第一句直白，第二句是常见英文谚语

  3. 想说「好关系是双方都开心」

    both of us being happy · it's ours
    第一句平实，第二句金句

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ Doing yourself is not simple.
✓ Being yourself is far harder than it looks.

doing yourself 是「做自己」的直译；英文固定说法是 being yourself / be true to yourself。

✕ The shell can wear and take off.
✓ You can put the persona on and take it off like armor.

中式语序「可穿可脱」直译不成立；用 put on / take off。

✕ I very want this melting love.
✓ I also deeply want this kind of melting love.

very want 是中式加强法；用 deeply want / really want。

✕ Good relationship's goal is I happy, you also happy.
✓ A good relationship aims for both of us being happy.

中式拼句缺结构；用 aim for / both of us being happy。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

做自己=想干嘛干嘛（任性） → 做自己是搞清楚「自己」是谁，再诚实地表达

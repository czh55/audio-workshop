一、人生逻辑与社会角色

Life Logic vs. Social Roles

情境An interview philosophy that prioritizes a person's happiness over matching a job description. 语域：casual 对话分享。

01
中文

核心机制：面试是社会建构的角色游戏，但双方真正目标都是人生幸福。当岗位不匹配时，在人的层面帮对方找更适合的路，比完成招聘 KPI 更符合本质

EN

An interview is a role-playing game constructed by society, but both sides are really after a happy life. When a role doesn't fit, helping the person find a better path at the human level is truer to the essence than hitting a recruiting KPI.

表达提示role-playing game → 角色游戏；truer to the essence → 更符合本质；recruiting KPI → 招聘指标

02
中文

关键理解：雨白因此相信「帮助他人一点点」能产生巨大效果，并来到有知有行。张潇雨讨厌被固定在社会身份里，宁愿做「风」到处吹，也不做严谨耕云的「累」

EN

Yu Bai came to believe that 'helping others a little' can have a huge effect, and that belief is what brought her to Youzhiyouxing. Zhang Xiaoyu hates being fixed inside a social identity — he'd rather be the wind, blowing everywhere, than a rigid cloud that carefully plows the sky.

表达提示social identity → 社会身份；be the wind → 做风（自由流动的隐喻）

03
中文

典型场景：招聘、合作、带人时，先问「这件事能否让你更幸福」，而非「你是否符合这个岗位」

EN

A typical case: when recruiting, partnering, or mentoring, ask first — 'will this make you happier?' — not 'do you match this role?'

表达提示recruiting → 招聘；mentoring → 带人

04
中文

（引用）原文依据：「既然我们都是为了获得某种人生的幸福……那为什么不在这个层面去解决这问题呢？」

EN

The original quote: 'Since we're all here to gain some happiness in life... why not solve the problem on that level?'

表达提示gain happiness → 获得幸福；on that level → 在那个层面

05
中文

（引用）相关概念：与功利性「跳槽涨薪」不同，核心是人格层面的真诚，而非职场技巧

EN

Related point: unlike the utilitarian 'job-hopping for a raise,' the core here is sincerity at the level of personhood — not workplace tactics.

表达提示utilitarian → 功利性的；sincerity at the level of personhood → 人格层面的真诚

06
中文

在 A 行业卷出来的能力，搬到 B 行业可能摧枯拉朽。得到上打磨多年的知识萃取方法论，换到另一领域价值完全不同——从投资回报角度，应寻找能力被低估的战场

EN

An ability sharpened through brutal competition in industry A can be crushing when moved to industry B. The knowledge-extraction methodology he honed for years at Dedao takes on completely different value in another field — from an investment-return angle, you should seek the battlefield where your abilities are undervalued.

表达提示be crushing → 摧枯拉朽；knowledge-extraction methodology → 知识萃取方法论；undervalued → 被低估

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「先问是否让你更幸福，而不是合不合岗位」

    fit the box · the wrong question
    fit the box 指符合框定岗位，很口语

  2. 想说「能力换个地方价值更大」

    the right arena · undervalued
    arena 指战场/赛场；undervalued 指被低估

二、把自己当作人生作品

Treating Yourself as a Work of Art

情境Why Zhang Xiaoyu happily removed his own name from a book, and the purpose behind all his work. 语域：casual 叙述。

01
中文

核心机制：投资第一课出版时，读库老六建议只保留一个作者名以避免人称混淆。张潇雨爽快答应去掉署名——目的本是帮助用户成长，而非扬名立万

EN

When The First Lesson of Investing was about to publish, DuKu editor Liu Laoliu suggested keeping only one author name to avoid pronoun confusion. Zhang Xiaoyu agreed without hesitation to drop his byline — the point was to help users grow, not to make a name for himself.

表达提示byline → 署名；drop the byline → 去掉署名；make a name for oneself → 扬名立万

02
中文

关键理解：他会自问「我在意吗？」若在意会说，但不执着。课程、文章、播客都是了解自我的手段，「把自己作为作品打磨」才是目的

EN

He often asks himself, 'Do I actually care?' If he does, he says so — but he doesn't cling. Courses, articles, and podcasts are all just tools for understanding himself; polishing himself as a work of art is the real goal.

表达提示don't cling → 不执着；polish oneself as a work of art → 把自己当作品打磨

03
中文

典型场景：合作署名、功劳分配时，优先问目的而非面子

EN

A typical case: when it comes to bylines and credit distribution, ask about the purpose first, not about face.

表达提示credit distribution → 功劳分配；about face → 面子

04
中文

（引用）原文依据：「我做所有事情都是为了了解我自己，那些东西都是手段。」

EN

The original quote: 'Everything I do is for understanding myself — those things are just the means.'

表达提示just the means → 只是手段

05
中文

雨白从孤勇开播、羡慕大台嘉宾，到意识到听众要的是「普通人的投资理财与生活」——增设「小酒馆故事会」，征集各地听友真实故事（如「人生第一个十万」），成为最受欢迎栏目

EN

Yu Bai started her show solo, envying guests from big stations — until she realized listeners wanted 'ordinary people's investing and life.' That's how the 'Little Tavern Story Nights' segment was born, collecting real listener stories like 'my first 100,000 yuan,' and it became the most popular column.

表达提示envy guests → 羡慕嘉宾；Story Nights → 故事会；the most popular column → 最受欢迎栏目

06
中文

（引用）「我不应该去跟别人比拼嘉宾资源，我更应该去听普通用户的声音。」

EN

The quote: 'I shouldn't compete with other shows over guest resources — I should listen to ordinary users' voices instead.'

表达提示compete over guest resources → 比拼嘉宾资源；ordinary users' voices → 普通用户的声音

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「这些只是了解自己的手段」

    a tool for · the means and the end
    the means / the end 是「手段与目的」的标准表达

  2. 想说「别跟别人拼资源，去听普通人的声音」

    compete on star guests · hear the ordinary listener
    compete on 指在某个维度竞争
S3

三、无我行动与情绪练习

Action Without a Self and Emotional Practice

情境The three layers of any event, and why removing 'I' improves everything. 语域：casual 哲学练习。

01
中文

核心机制：任何事有三层——事件层（播客录砸了）、情绪层（懊悔自责）、叙事层（「我不适合干这个」）。多数人活在叙事层，给事件贴上「我」的标签，固化为自我认知（MBTI、创业者身份等）

EN

Anything has three layers: the event layer — the podcast flopped; the emotion layer — regret and self-blame; and the narrative layer — 'I'm not cut out for this.' Most people live in the narrative layer, stamping the event with 'me' and hardening it into self-identity, like an MBTI label or a founder identity.

表达提示event layer / emotion layer / narrative layer → 事件层/情绪层/叙事层；not cut out for → 不适合

02
中文

关键理解：网球运动员最自发时打得最好，「我」进来就糟了。去掉选择者，只留选择——像刚醒来刷牙，身体自然行动，尚未冒出「我今天要干这些事」的叙述者

EN

A tennis player plays best when most spontaneous; the moment 'I' steps in, it falls apart. Remove the chooser and leave only the choice — like brushing your teeth right after waking, the body just moves, before the narrator pipes up with 'today I have to do all these things.'

表达提示spontaneous → 自发；fall apart → 崩坏；the narrator pipes up → 叙述者冒出来

03
中文

典型场景：项目失败、公开出丑后，先处理事实与情绪，延迟叙事层的身份结论

EN

A typical case: after a project fails or you embarrass yourself publicly, handle the facts and the emotions first — and delay the identity verdict in the narrative layer.

表达提示embarrass oneself publicly → 公开出丑；identity verdict → 身份结论

04
中文

（引用）原文依据：「在整个过程中，只有选择，而没有选择者。」

EN

The original quote: 'In the whole process, there is only the choice — no chooser.'

表达提示no chooser → 没有选择者

05
中文

看日落时最幸福，因为脑子没有念头。念头一起来就开始解释、拍照、发圈——从体验滑入对体验的想法。活在当下即回到体验本身，而非体验的解释

EN

Watching a sunset is the happiest moment because the mind has no thoughts. The moment a thought arises, you start explaining, photographing, posting — sliding from the experience into ideas about the experience. Living in the present is returning to the experience itself, not the explanation of it.

表达提示slide from experience into ideas → 从体验滑入想法；live in the present → 活在当下

06
中文

适用边界：需要长期练习；难受时跳出很正常，下次再来即可

EN

Boundary conditions: this takes long-term practice — it's fine to fall out of it when you feel awful; just come back and try again next time.

表达提示long-term practice → 长期练习；fall out of it → 跳出来

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「只有选择，没有选择者」

    no chooser · drop the I
    drop the 'I' 是本集金句式口语

  2. 想说「从体验滑向对体验的想法」

    slide from A into B · narrating instead of living
    narrating instead of living 是精炼对照表达
S4

四、关系模式与命运课题

Relationship Patterns and Life's Repeating Lessons

情境Zhang Xiaoyu's unhealthy expectations in close relationships, and why fate repeats the same question. 语域：casual 真诚自白。

01
中文

对重视的关系有极其不健康的期待：把对方当 AI 喂数据，希望被「以我不懂自己的方式理解」；需要自己在对方生命中「特别特殊」，不断确认特殊性而不再体验关系本身

EN

For relationships he valued, he held deeply unhealthy expectations: treating the other person like an AI he could feed data to, hoping to be understood 'in ways he didn't understand himself,' and needing to be 'especially special' in the other's life — constantly re-confirming his specialness instead of experiencing the relationship itself.

表达提示feed data to → 喂数据；re-confirm one's specialness → 不断确认特殊性

02
中文

模式在惨烈崩溃中破裂——痛定思痛后，更淡然地体验关系，不求特殊，不过度分析

EN

The pattern broke down in a brutal collapse. After thinking it through in the pain, he now experiences relationships more calmly — no demand for specialness, no over-analyzing.

表达提示brutal collapse → 惨烈崩溃；over-analyze → 过度分析

03
中文

（引用）「我不用是你的最爱……但我需要我在里面特别特别特殊的，至于怎么特殊我都不知道。」

EN

The quote: 'I don't need to be your favorite... but I need to be especially special in it — and I don't even know how I want to be special.'

表达提示especially special → 特别特别特殊（有意夸张）

04
中文

核心机制：命运会持续给你一样的问题，直到你给出不同答案。关系、投资、自我完善——每次「完善」后可能再来一个大的（杀猪盘），识别能力会提高，掉进去也能更快出来

EN

The mechanism: fate keeps handing you the same problem until you give a different answer. Relationships, investing, self-improvement — each time you 'fix' yourself, a bigger one may come, like a pig-slaughtering scam, but your ability to recognize it rises, and you can get out faster.

表达提示pig-slaughtering scam → 杀猪盘（诈骗类型）；recognize the pattern → 识别模式

05
中文

关键理解：戳破稳固价值体系的人是福报；破了模式就是新人。张潇雨对雨白：「如果我今天说的导致节目越做越差，我就是你的命运、你的报应。」

EN

The insight: people who puncture your rock-solid value system are a blessing. Once the pattern breaks, you're a new person. Zhang Xiaoyu told Yu Bai: 'If what I say today makes your show worse and worse, then I'm your fate — your karma.'

表达提示puncture → 戳破；blessing → 福报；karma → 报应

06
中文

（引用）相关概念：与「创伤后成长」不同，强调主动识别重复课题而非被动等待时间治愈

EN

Related idea: unlike 'post-traumatic growth,' this emphasizes actively recognizing the repeating lesson instead of passively waiting for time to heal you.

表达提示post-traumatic growth → 创伤后成长；repeating lesson → 重复课题

Paraphrase & Chunks 2 组表达

  1. 想说「命运会一直给同样的问题，直到你给出不同答案」

    the same exam · repeat the question
    宇宙派题这种说法适合闲聊；pass the exam 更直接

  2. 想说「戳破你价值体系的人是贵人」

    crack one's certainty · break one's patterns
    crack 比 break 更带「撬开」的意味

今日可练 PRACTICE TODAY

用「be cut out for」说：我不适合干这个。

The narrative layer whispers 'I'm not cut out for this' — that's where most people live.

用「drop the I」说：去掉选择者，只留选择。

Once you drop the 'I', you're left with pure choice — the action just happens.

用「puncture」说：戳破你稳固价值体系的人是福报。

The people who puncture your rock-solid belief system are quietly a blessing.

用「the means, the end」说：作品只是手段，了解自己是目的。

Every project is the means; knowing yourself is the end.

避坑 PITFALLS

✕ I want to be very special in your life.
✓ I need to be especially special in your life — even if I can't say how.

very special 不够；原文的「特别特别特殊」是有意夸张的自我暴露，用 especially special 才保留那份坦诚。

✕ The podcast was recorded badly, I am very regretful.
✓ The event layer is 'the episode flopped'; the emotion layer is regret — don't let the narrative layer turn it into identity.

三层结构是本集核心框架；只翻一句悔恨就丢了「别给事件贴标签」的要点。

✕ I am very suitable for this job.
✓ The real question is whether the job makes you happier, not whether you fit it.

suitable 直译没抓到重点——张潇雨主张「幸福优先于匹配」，要翻出这层对比。

✕ I should not compare guests with other podcasts.
✓ Don't compete on guest resources — listen to ordinary users' voices.

compare guests 是「比较」；核心是「别在嘉宾资源这个维度竞争」，用 compete on 才准确。

认知转变 MINDSET SHIFTS

以为面试/合作就是匹配岗位与能力 → 双方真正要的是人生幸福——在人的层面真诚帮助，比完成 KPI 更接近本质
以为课程、作品是为了扬名立万、证明自己 → 它们只是了解自己的手段，「把自己作为作品打磨」才是目的
以为出丑失败就要定义自己「不适合这个」 → 事件层、情绪层、叙事层要分开——别急着把事件贴成自我身份
